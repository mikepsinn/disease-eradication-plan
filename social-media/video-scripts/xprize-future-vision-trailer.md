# Future Vision XPRIZE Entry
## "The Instruction Manual"
### 3-Minute Sci-Fi Trailer + Treatment Outline

**Concept:** An alien intelligence has been watching Earth for 80 years. It finally sends humanity an instruction manual. The trailer shows two futures — the one where humanity ignores it, and the one where they don't. It's a documentary from the future, narrated by the alien, in the style of a BBC nature documentary about a species that might not make it.

**Tone:** Philomena Cunk meets Arrival meets An Inconvenient Truth. Deadpan. Funny. Then devastating. Then hopeful. The humor makes the data land harder.

**Deadline:** August 15, 2026
**Prize:** $2.6M grand prize (production funding for feature film)
**Requirement:** 3-min max trailer + up to 12-page treatment

## 12-PAGE TREATMENT OUTLINE (for XPRIZE submission)

### Feature Film Concept: "THE INSTRUCTION MANUAL"

**Logline:** An alien intelligence that has been watching Earth for 80 years finally breaks protocol and sends humanity an instruction manual for ending war and disease — but the manual's biggest obstacle isn't the science, the money, or the politics. It's convincing humans that a plan this obvious isn't a trap.

**Genre:** Sci-fi documentary / mockumentary hybrid
**Tone:** Philomena Cunk's Wonders of the World meets Arrival meets The Big Short

**Structure:**
- Act 1: WISHONIA observes Earth, baffled by human dysfunction. Introduces the problem through comedy.
- Act 2: The manual arrives. Different humans react — a billionaire skeptic, a desperate parent, a cynical politician, a young activist. Each one goes through the nine questions.
- Act 3: The chain reaction. One person forwards it. Then another. The math takes over. We watch the treaty campaign unfold — the bonds, the lobbying pivot, the referendum.
- Act 4: Future B. The world 20 years later. Not utopia — WISHONIA is clear about this — but unrecognizable. The diseases that killed Act 1's characters are gone. The child from the opening scene is grown up, healthy, working in one of the new labs.

**Why This Wins XPRIZE:**
- It's optimistic AND grounded — every number in the film is real
- It's funny — humor is the Trojan horse for caring
- It's literally a blueprint — the film IS the instruction manual
- The feature film wouldn't just depict an optimistic future — it would help CREATE one by reaching the audience with the actual plan
- The alien narrator device makes hard economic data entertaining
- It scales: the film drives viewers to warondisease.org to actually vote

**Production Approach:**
- AI-generated visuals (Sora/Runway/Kling) for future scenes
- Stock footage + motion graphics for present-day data
- Voice performance for WISHONIA (could be Mike or professional VO)
- Budget-efficient: the power is in the script, not the VFX
