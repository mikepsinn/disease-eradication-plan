# Foundation Transformation

**Target**: Gates Foundation, Wellcome Trust, Open Philanthropy

## MINIMAL CHANGES ONLY

1. Soften casual conversational intros (keep the substance):
   - "Fun fact:" → remove or "Note:"
   - "Here's the cosmic joke:" → "Consider this paradox:"
   - "Quick business question:" → "Consider:"
2. Remove profanity and crude language:
   - Section headers: "Clusterfuck" → "Coordination Challenge" or "Strategic Impasse"
   - Parentheticals: "(spam me daddy)" → "(send me updates)"
   - Crude casual phrases: tone down to PG-rated equivalents
3. Soften cynical language that implies dishonesty:
   - "How to Pretend You Care" → "Data Privacy Considerations"
   - "How to Pretend..." → "Demonstrating..." or remove "Pretend"
4. Update terminology:
   - "Decentralized FDA" → "dFDA"
   - "The DIH" → "decentralized institutes of health"
5. Remove language that could jeopardize 501(c)(3) status:
   - "propaganda" → "communications" or "outreach"
   - "approach politicians" / "we have voters" → "engage stakeholders" / "public support"
   - "lies" / "fabricated" / "false advertising" → "optimistic projections" / "strategic messaging"
   - Remove explicit references to political lobbying or voter mobilization

## PRESERVE EVERYTHING ELSE
- Keep titles and descriptions as-is (foundations appreciate compelling titles)
- Keep ALL humor, sarcasm, and personality ("murder gangs", "meat puppets", "idiots", etc.)
- Keep provocative section headers ("The Selfish Gene Made You an Idiot")
- Keep second-person voice ("your brain", "you die") - more engaging
- Keep ALL vivid analogies and sarcasm
- Keep ALL numbers, statistics, calculations, formulas, citations
- 95% of text remains identical
