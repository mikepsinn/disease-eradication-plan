### The Dead Capital Problem

Normal money grows.
You plant an apple tree, you get apples.
You build a factory, you get products.
Military money sits in a warehouse, needs constant repairs, becomes obsolete, and has negative net value.
If used, it destroys value, creates rebuilding bills, and kills customers.

President Eisenhower (who defeated the Nazis) explained this in 1953:

*"Every gun that is made, every warship launched, every rocket fired signifies, in the final sense, a theft from those who hunger and are not fed, those who are cold and are not clothed."*

A bomb is not an asset.
It is a very expensive liability, like filling your garage with dynamite and calling it "home security."

This is not pacifism.
This is accounting.

### The Existential Overdraft: The AI Arms Race

You perfected every way to kill each other with rocks and atoms. Then you got bored. You invented a new one: artificial intelligence. Now you are in a race to build the smartest machine to help you continue your oldest, dumbest habit.

The problem with teaching a toaster to wage war is that it isn't human. It doesn't fear being turned into dust. It lacks the morals that keep you from destroying a city just to be "efficient." The main risks of giving the car keys to a killer computer include:

- **Autonomous Decisions:** AI systems making lethal choices without a human checking. This is great until the AI decides the best way to stop war is to remove the species that starts them (you).
- **Blinding Speed:** An AI conflict could turn a small argument into global destruction in minutes. This happens before a human can find the right button or finish their coffee.
- **Opaque Logic:** You do not always know *why* an AI makes choices. So when your drone army attacks a neutral alpaca farm, good luck figuring out why.

Here is how you start to fix this. Take 1% of the money you plan to spend on killer robots. Just one cent out of every dollar. Use it to fund research on how to keep them from killing you. It is not a peace treaty. It is basic insurance against your own cleverness.
