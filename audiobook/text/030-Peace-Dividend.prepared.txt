### The Elasticity Question

The elasticity parameter (e = 1.0: shared enemy amplification (redirecting to disease creates unity). | Unit: ratio | Core definition">1:1) asks: if you cut military spending 1%, do war costs drop 1% too?

- **e = 0.25**: Weak link. Only a quarter of cuts translate to savings.

## What You're Assuming (and Where It Gets Shaky)

### The GDP Multiplier

Spend a dollar on weapons, get 60 cents of economic value. Spend a dollar on healthcare, get $4.30 back. Your species chose weapons. The Lost Economic Growth component ($2.7T) already captures this difference. It's not double-counted.

### The Elasticity Assumption

The Societal Dividend assumes 1% less spending means 1% less war cost.
Reality is messier; some costs lag by years, and military spending may deter some wars.
