The Unexplored Therapeutic Frontier.

Ninety-five percent of your diseases have zero F.D.A.-approved treatments. This means Food and Drug Administration has not administered drugs for ninety-five percent of food-and-drug-related problems. Only 15 diseases get their first effective treatment each year. Six thousand six hundred and fifty diseases are still waiting.

There are nine thousand five hundred known safe compounds, and ninety-nine point seven percent of their potential uses have never been tested.

Your National Institutes of Health spends ninety-six point seven percent of its budget on everything except testing which treatments work in humans and hasn't cured a single disease in 50 years.

At the current discovery rate, discovering treatments will take about 443 years. Everyone currently alive will be dead by then.

Pragmatic trials (testing drugs on real patients in real hospitals instead of 200 people hand-picked by pharmaceutical companies) at scale increase discovery rate twelve point three times (from 15 diseases per year to 185 diseases per year), compressing that to about 36 years.

Two things save lives here. First, finding cures faster: treatments that would have sat undiscovered for centuries get found 204 years sooner on average. Second, eliminating the eight point two year bureaucratic delay after already proving a drug is safe and when they let dying humans take it.

Combined, treatments reach patients 212 years sooner on average. That timeline shift prevents 10.7 billion deaths.
