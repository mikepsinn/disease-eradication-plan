Three. No money goes to politicians directly, as they benefit only indirectly through better scores, electoral support, and career options, which is how democracy was supposed to work before your species broke it.

Four. Scores are based on public voting records where a YES vote triggers the ten percent fund automatically based on treasury inflows and pure math.
