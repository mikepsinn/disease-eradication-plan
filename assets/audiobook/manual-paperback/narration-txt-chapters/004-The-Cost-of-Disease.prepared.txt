How to Measure Suffering Without Feeling Feelings (The D.A.L.Y.).

disability-adjusted life years, or D.A.L.Y. stands for "Disability-Adjusted Life Year." That's economist-speak for "quantifying human misery without having to look people in the eye.".

One D.A.L.Y. equals One year of healthy human life, deleted.

Die at 40 instead of 80?
That's 40 D.A.L.Y.s.
Spend 10 years severely depressed?
That's 5 D.A.L.Y.s (economists calculated that being miserable counts as being half-dead, which is oddly accurate).

The entire human species combined? 2.55 BILLION D.A.L.Y.s annually. That's two point five five billion years of human potential, dissolving annually into medical bills and funeral expenses.
