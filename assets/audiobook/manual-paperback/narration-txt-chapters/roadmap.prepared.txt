This isn't a protest. It's a recipe. Like making bread, except instead of bread it's curing diseases, and instead of yeast it's (legal) bribes.

High-Level Strategy: The Three-Step Recipe for Not Dying.

Humans love steps. You put them on everything. Step one: Breathe. Step two: Keep breathing. Step three: Try not to die. You're very methodical about staying alive. That makes it curious that you spend six hundred and four times more on weapons than testing which medicines work.

Here's your three-step algorithm for fixing this adorable contradiction:

Step one: Collect Papers from Rich People.

You convince wealthy humans to give you a large pile of papers by promising them returns that make military contractors nervous. This works because rich people are excellent at counting papers but terrible at not wanting more papers.

The pitch is simple: "Give us papers now, get more papers forever, also maybe don't die from preventable diseases." Even humans who think vaccines cause five G can understand "more papers good."

Step two: Tell Humans the Referendum Exists.

Use some papers to launch a global marketing campaign so two hundred and eighty million people learn there's a referendum and vote on it. This achieves more than most political movements involving shouting.

The referendum isn't a simple yes/no. Each voter gets a wishocratic slider to set their preferred allocation between military spending and clinical trials. The results are aggregated to determine humanity's collective preferred split. It's a species-wide budget meeting.
You'd think the outcome would be obvious. But humans once thought the sun went around Earth, so you have to ask.

This creates what's called "political pressure." That's when politicians realize that voters exist and some of them have opinions.

Step three: Apply Papers to Politicians Until Treaty Happens.

Deploy the remaining papers to convince politicians that supporting the treaty is the only way to remain employed.

The treaty passes. twenty-seven point two billion dollars per year flows from murder to medicine. Forever. Or until the sun explodes. Whichever happens first (probably the sun).

The Sacred (Legal) Bribery Sequence.

Humans respond to papers. This is your most important discovery about Earth. You tried appealing to morality (failed). You tried appealing to logic (failed harder). But papers? Papers work every time.

Here's the precise order for distributing papers to specific categories of humans:

(Legal) Bribe Category number one: Rich People (The Paper Collectors).

Who you're (legally) bribing: Wealthy humans who have many papers and want even more papers, despite already having more papers than they could spend in several lifetimes.

The (legal) bribe you offer: Modeled on World War Two War Bonds, except instead of four percent returns for funding death, these pay two hundred and seventy-two percent for funding not-death. Like war bonds except backwards.

Why they accept: Because rich humans can read a spreadsheet, and no spreadsheet in history has ever said "give up two hundred and seventy-two percent annual returns because you feel weird about the source.".

Money-back guarantee: If the treaty fails, they get their papers back via assurance contracts. Funds are escrowed until milestones hit. Automatic refund if targets are missed.
This is basically saying "if everyone dies from preventable diseases, you'll be too dead to complain about losing money.".

What happens: Defense contractors lose one percent of their revenue but discover a new twenty-seven point two billion dollars a year health spending market has appeared where there used to be nothing. The same companies that build missile guidance systems can build medical device supply chains.
It turns out building things that go inside humans isn't that different from building things that go inside other humans' countries. The main difference is whether the end user survives.

The Specific Offer to Bomb Makers.

You show weapons manufacturers a spreadsheet (they love spreadsheets):

Option A (Current Job).

The operating margin is eight percent.

The product is things that make humans stop existing.

Customer satisfaction is N. A. (customers deceased).

The market outlook is shrinking by one percent.

Option B (Your Offer).

The operating margin is similar, except nobody dies (this is new for them).

The product is things that make humans continue existing.

Customer satisfaction is very high (customers still alive to provide feedback).

The market outlook is expanding (see Phase four plus).

They choose Option B. Not because they've had a moral awakening, but because their shareholders can read a market forecast. Weapons manufacturers don't have feelings, but they do have quarterly earnings calls.

(Legal) Bribe Category number two: The True Believers (Early Adopters).

Who you're (legally) bribing: Humans who believe in curing diseases AND want to get rich doing it. These exist. They're called "effective altruists" except they also want a yacht.
On Wishonia we call them "normal," but on Earth apparently wanting to do good AND get paid for it is a whole personality type.

The (legal) bribe you offer: Give two hundred million dollars to get the referendum started (the full thing costs two hundred and fifty million dollars, but you don't need all of it upfront because humans love joining things that are already working).
In return, they get VICTORY Incentive Alignment Bonds paying two hundred and seventy-two percent annually. They're first in line for a twenty-seven point two billion dollars a year pie that didn't exist before they showed up.

What they fund: The Global Referendum marketing campaign. Getting two hundred and eighty million people to learn the referendum exists and vote on it.

What they get: two hundred and seventy-two percent returns for as long as the treaty holds. And since their personal wealth now depends on the treaty surviving, they will use their considerable pre-existing influence over government, media, and other humans to make sure nobody repeals it. You didn't buy their influence.
You bought their self-interest. Their influence comes free.

Why this works: You give them money. They give you money back, plus more money. On Wishonia this is called "a transaction." On Earth it's called "investing," and humans will do absolutely anything for it.

(Legal) Bribe Category Number Three: Regular People (Everyone Else).

Who you're (legally) bribing: two hundred and eighty million regular humans (three point five percent of your species, which history proves is enough to change anything). It's like reaching critical mass, except instead of a nuclear explosion it's democracy (much less lethal).

The (legal) bribe you offer: Vote on the referendum via wishocratic slider. Get paid for telling your friends about it. It's a pyramid scheme where the thing at the top of the pyramid is not dying from preventable diseases.

What happens after: Treaty passes. twenty-seven point two billion dollars a year flows to clinical trials. Diseases get cured faster. Your healthcare improves.
Also, two hundred and eighty million humans now have a personal stake in this thing continuing, and there is nothing more dangerous on Earth than a large group of humans who were promised something.

Why this works: Humans love having opinions. Humans love getting paid. Humans (allegedly) love not dying. This combines all three. When three point five percent of the population has voted on something, politicians experience what you call "responsive governance."

(Legal) Bribe Category Number Four: Politicians (The Elected Paper Recipients).

Who you're (legally) bribing: Humans whose job is "get elected by promising things, then do whatever the people with papers want." Politicians are simple organisms with one evolutionary drive: reelection. Currently, weapons manufacturers feed this drive.
You're going to feed it better, the way you'd lure a dog away from garbage with a steak.

The (legal) bribe you offer: Super PAC support from the six hundred and fifty million dollar lobbying budget. Two hundred and eighty million voters demanding the treaty. Plus cushy jobs after they leave office for politicians who vote yes: fellowships, board seats, speaking circuits.
You know, the things politicians actually want. Insurance companies realize healthy people file fewer death claims. Suddenly their lobbyists become your lobbyists. It's beautiful. Like watching a dog realize the mailman has treats.

The Package.

Papers for campaigns. Massive Super P.A.C. funding (legal bribery).

Votes for staying employed. Referendum voters who will vote for treaty supporters.

Post-office rewards. Fellowships, board seats, and speaking circuits for politicians who vote yes.

Power to reward friends. Direct a portion of the one percent Treaty Fund's twenty-seven point two billion dollars to their home districts (funding pragmatic clinical trials and building decentralized institutes of health for decades of ribbon-cutting ceremonies).

The choice you give them.

Option A: Support treaty, get papers plus votes plus legacy as "politician who cured diseases".

Option B: Oppose treaty, discover two hundred and eighty million voters wanted to not die (they're voting against you).

Most politicians are excellent at counting votes. The math becomes simple even for them.

What happens: Politicians discover they've always been passionate about curing diseases. This sudden passion perfectly coincides with receiving papers and votes. On your planet, that's called "representative democracy."

The Success Spiral.

Each category of bribe creates the next. Rich people fund the referendum. The referendum creates voters. Voters pressure politicians. Politicians pass the treaty. The treaty makes money. Money funds more cures. Cures prove it works. More people demand expansion.

This is the first time in your history that "I prefer being alive" has been organized into a political position. This seems obvious, but for humanity it's revolutionary.

And unlike current government, where money and influence are tangled in ways nobody can trace, this system keeps them separate. Investors get money (ten percent). Politicians get career incentives (ten percent).

Neither group touches the eighty percent that goes to clinical trials via wishocratic allocation. The corruption is capped at twenty percent, transparent, and completely disentangled from the public goods funding. Your current system can't say any of those things.

You want to end war and disease. You need one billion dollars in papers. You currently have zero dollars in papers (probably). Here's the recipe.

The Legal Architecture: How to Be a Charity, a Lobbying Group, and a Hedge Fund Simultaneously.

You need to fight on three fronts to win: moral (charity), political (lobbying), and financial (investment). The problem is your legal system says one organization can't do all three.

Your legal system is adorably complicated, like a board game designed by lawyers having a nightmare.

So you build a four-part machine:

The Charity (five zero one c three): This is the "brains."

It does research and education.

Rich foundations give papers here and get to feel good about themselves (plus tax deductions, which are like discount coupons for virtue).

The Lobbying Group (five zero one c four): This is the "sword."

It takes the research and uses it to wage unlimited political warfare.

That sounds scary but mostly involves lunch meetings and PowerPoints.

The Corporation (C-Corp): This is the "engine."

A for-profit entity that issues the VICTORY Incentive Alignment Bonds and collects the papers.

Yes, you need a for-profit corporation to end death.

Your species is hilarious.

The Swiss Foundation: This is the "soul."

The parent organization owns the corporation, ensuring profits can only fund the mission.

You put it in Switzerland because they're good at keeping things neutral and also chocolate.

This structure lets you legally accept papers from everyone (foundations, political donors, and Wall Street) and aim them all at diseases instead of each other.

Phase four plus: The Expansion Phases (Years four to fifty).

The one percent treaty is just the beginning. Like all addictions, not-dying starts with a small dose and escalates. The real goal is eventually redirecting *most* military spending to health.

Why Expansion Is Built Into The System.

The treaty money splits eighty-ten-ten: eighty percent to clinical trials (the point), ten percent to investors (the bribe), and ten percent to political incentives (the bribe for the other kind of human).
That last slice is the interesting part, because it grows every time the treaty expands:

In phase three, spanning years one to three, the treaty is one percent, creating a political incentive pool of two point seven two billion dollars per year. The treaty passes, and humans cautiously try not dying.

In phase four, spanning years four to seven, the treaty rises to two percent, with five point four four billion dollars per year in the incentive pool. Politicians compete to expand (they've discovered voters like living).

In phase five, spanning years eight to twelve, the treaty rises to five percent, with thirteen point six billion dollars per year in the incentive pool. The health lobby rivals the defense lobby and lobbyists switch suits.

In phase six, spanning years thirteen to twenty, the treaty reaches ten percent, creating twenty-seven point two billion dollars per year in the incentive pool. "Funded pragmatic clinical trials" becomes the most rewarded phrase in politics.

In phase seven, spanning years twenty-one to thirty-five, the treaty reaches twenty-five percent, with sixty-eight billion dollars per year in the incentive pool. Superpowers compete on health outcomes (much less radioactive).

In phase eight, spanning years thirty-six to fifty, the treaty reaches fifty percent or more, with over one hundred and thirty-six billion dollars per year in the incentive pool. Historians write confused books about why this took so long.

Here's Why It Keeps Growing.

Bond investors get ten percent of all treaty funds. At one percent, that's two hundred and seventy-two percent returns. At two percent, it doubles. At ten percent, they're collecting ten times their original payout. So they will call their senator and say "please expand the treaty," not because they care about diseases but because they care about money.

You don't have to ask them to do this. They will do it automatically, the way a plant grows toward sunlight except the sunlight is money and the plant is a billionaire. You've built a defense lobby in reverse.

Politicians see their Incentive Alignment Bond, or I.A.B.-funded rewards, grow with each expansion. By Stage four, the political incentive pool (twenty-seven point two billion dollars per year) exceeds all current global lobbying combined.

At that point, "I support pragmatic clinical trial funding" becomes the most profitable sentence a politician can say, which means they will say it constantly, which means they will eventually believe it. This is how your species forms convictions.

The Endgame.

By twenty seventy-five, the math becomes irresistible:

Over one point three six trillion dollars flows to health annually.

Over one hundred and thirty-six billion dollars funds political incentives (more than all lobbying ever).

Over one point zero eight eight trillion dollars funds pragmatic clinical trials.

The one percent treaty isn't the goal. It's the first one percent. Every investor and every politician makes more money if the number goes up, so they will push the number up. How far it goes depends on whether curing diseases turns out to be more popular than building bombs.

Given that diseases kill approximately everyone and bombs kill approximately fewer people than diseases, the odds seem favorable. But you're humans, so nothing is certain.
