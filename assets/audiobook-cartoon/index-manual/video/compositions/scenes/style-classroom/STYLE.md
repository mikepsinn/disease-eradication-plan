# Style test: "Classroom film"

Part 2 ("The Human Economy", `parts/part-02-classroom.json`) redone as an earnest 1950s classroom film, to compare with the diagram look of `parts/part-02.json`.

- **One idea per shot, three things at most.** Paper cut-out figures (with a soft paper shadow) on big flat fields of color: cream sky or wall, tan ground, a slate stage.
- **The camera does the work.** Slow push-ins, pans along one long landscape, cuts on the narrator's phrases. The money loop is five tableaux on one strip (press, factory, town, farm stand, press again), and the loop closes by panning back onto the first press, with no circle diagram. The papers march to the factory in step, at one pace, and the pan rides along with them. On "grows ... for free" the camera pushes slowly in on the farmer and his wheat, so the price tag fills the frame.
- **Each shape gets its own staging.** 9:16 is too narrow for a queue beside the press, so there the papers line up in front of it and the horizon sits higher. In the host shot Wishonia stands at the back of the stage, top left, and the hungry man stands in the foreground, bottom right, so the sandwich crosses the frame on a diagonal.
- **Period transitions only:** the title card dissolves into the first shot; an iris opens and closes the loop and the host shot.
- **Limited animation.** Held poses swapped on the words, one hinged arm, papers walking on two leg drawings, things sliding in as cut-outs.
- **Almost no text.** A hand-lettered title card ("A Wishonia Educational Film" over THE HUMAN ECONOMY) and one hand-lettered number, 310,000,000. No labels, signs, banners or arrows; the price tag is blank.
- **The narrator is unseen.** Wishonia appears once, as the on-camera host in the closing shot, never in a corner.
- **Every gag gets one clean, held image:** the paper takes a bow, a chimney turns out to be a missile, the bomb, the charred family still waving, the hard hat landing on the father's hair, the skeletons keeping their grins (and the hat), the sandwich with no price tag.
- **The film is the same on every shot,** and sits outside the camera: a faded warm wash, boiling grain, dust and a rare scratch, a soft flicker, a vignette, and a little gate weave. Colors come from `KIT.C` only.
- **Sound is sparse:** a stamp, a ding, a boom, a few pops, a slide whistle; silence under the HOLD.
- The shared blocks (film, camera, iris) are the same in every scene file of this variant: change them all together.
