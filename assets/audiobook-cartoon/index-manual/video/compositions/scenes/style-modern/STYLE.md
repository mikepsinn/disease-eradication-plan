# Style test: modern limited animation

Part 2 ("The Human Economy", `parts/part-02-modern.json`) redrawn as a mid-century modern limited-animation classroom film, to compare with the current diagram version of the same 40 seconds.

- **One flat color field per shot**, from `KIT.C`, and nothing else behind the subject: cream (title), deep navy (the stage), mustard (print), rust (weapons makers), cream sky snapping to red (the bomb), peri (jobs), cyan (food), cream (the wheel), cyan draining to slate (the dead), cream (the host).
- **One green paper tells the money loop.** It travels left to right through four fields (mustard, rust, peri, cyan); "the economy" is those four colors as one wheel that it runs on top of and never gets off.
- **At most three things on screen**, staged abstractly: one line for a horizon, boxes for houses, a cone for a spotlight, a disc behind the host, a podium in the tall version.
- **Held poses that snap.** Characters (`KIT.person`, `KIT.cast`) are whole drawings swapped with `tl.set` on the words; walks and runs are two drawings swapped; flights move in held steps twelve times a second. Only small parts move between poses (eyes, smoke, legs).
- **The camera does the work:** a slow creep in on every shot; two pull-back reveals (the paper is running on a wheel; the dead family stands in a field of graves that runs off both edges); two hard cuts in on a phrase, the same picture bigger ("give" to the father's open hand, "(which" to the farmer and the bare ground), where whoever would be cut in half by the edge is left out; a push-in on the frozen paper into the cut to the dead; an iris in and out on the host. Nothing pops in to say what the camera can show.
- **Text:** the title card and 310,000,000 (stamped over the picture, outside the camera, so it stays put). No labels, banners, arrows or signs; the price tag is blank.
- **Wishonia is the unseen narrator** until the last shot, where she is the on-camera host (big, never in a corner).
- **Payoffs are single held images:** the moth out of the empty hat, the smug missile and the cloud on a red sky, the charred family in hard hats, the price tag on wheat that grew by itself, the grinning skeletons in front of the headstones, the bite.
- **Period film on every shot**, in an overlay outside the camera: a cream wash, a round vignette, `KIT.grain`, a frame flicker with dust specks, and a pixel or two of gate weave under it.
- **Sparse sound:** a thunk per title word, a switch, pops, a ding, a falling whistle and a boom, a slide whistle, a stamp, a whirr, a record scratch, a zap.
