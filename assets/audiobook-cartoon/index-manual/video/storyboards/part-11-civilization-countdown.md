# Part 11 storyboard: "Your Civilization Has a Countdown"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd` "Your Civilization Has a Countdown", and `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd` (the destructive economy, its growth and the Soviet comparison).
- **The numbers:**
  - Official military spending: `global_military_spending_annual_2024` $2.72 trillion a year.
  - Cybercrime: `global_cybercrime_cost_annual_2025` $10.5 trillion a year (nearly 4 times the military), growing `global_cybercrime_cagr` 15% a year.
  - North Korea: $1.5 billion of cryptocurrency in one afternoon (the Bybit theft, February 2025; cited in the chapter, not a parameter).
  - The destructive economy: `global_destructive_economy_annual_2025` $13.2 trillion, which is `global_destructive_economy_pct_gdp` 11.5% of `global_gdp_2025` $115 trillion. 11.5% of a pie is a 41° wedge.
  - It grows `global_destructive_economy_growth_rate` 12.6% a year; GDP grows `gdp_baseline_growth_rate` 2.5%. Indexed to 2025 = 1, by 2040 destruction is at 5.9 and making things at 1.45 (about 5 times the growth rate).
  - Its share of GDP reaches 25% in `destructive_economy_25pct_year` 2033, 35% in `destructive_economy_35pct_year` 2037 and 50% in `destructive_economy_50pct_year` 2040. Derived: on that curve it crosses the Soviet 15% in about 2028.
  - The Soviet Union: about 15% of GDP on the military when it collapsed in 1991 (the chapter; the knowledge page gives 15 to 18%).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** war plus cybercrime is a $13.2 trillion economy that destroys things, growing about five times faster than the one that makes things; it passes the share that sank the Soviet Union around 2028, and the treaty works by taking the money out of the loop that feeds it.
