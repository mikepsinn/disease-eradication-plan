# Part 8 storyboard: "The Problem: The Daily Deletion Event"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper; the money is green, and its seal says what it buys (a red missile for war, a teal cross for medicine). "PiP" means Wishonia in her Layer B corner: standing in the bottom right corner in 16:9 (cut off by the frame's bottom edge), in her flying saucer at the top right in 9:16, with her gesture body showing.

**Spine.** The NOW SERVING sign. It hangs over the Grim Reaper's deli counter in deletion-board and stops on 150,000, EVERY 24 HOURS. When the second customer is served it unhooks, shrinks into the top-left corner and keeps clicking up at the true rate (one number every 0.58 seconds: 86,400 seconds a day / 150,000) through holocaust-every-40-days and fifty-nine-elevens, so the count runs while the narrator converts it. It leaves on "Your" and comes back once more in dissolving-on-schedule, hanging behind the reader's armchair with its face to the wall: the schedule he can't see, ticking. The reader's bookmark is one of its red tickets.

**Callback.** The Grim Reaper from the theme song and loved-ones (Part 1) runs the deli counter. The moth from the empty wallet in without-with-papers (Part 1) flies out of the disease blob's pockets. The beetle from deletion-board's "MEAT SOFTWARE" comes back in meat-robot-blueprint, where a wrench finally flattens it.

**Callback.** The reader from falling-apart sits on the wall screen again in weirdly-calm (Part 9), still reading, red ticket for a bookmark.

**Held frame.** deletion-board, from "hours" to "bugs". holocaust-every-40-days, from "days" to "insurance". fifty-nine-elevens, from "day" to "invades". In each hold only the corner sign's digits move (they are the count, not a gag), and the hold is silent. falling-apart and dissolving-on-schedule are the slowest scenes in the episode: one slow push-in and small moves only.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
- No Nazi uniforms, symbols, camps or victims, and no towers, planes or pictures of the 9/11 attacks: the calendar pages and the date tags carry those numbers.

## Changes from v1

- The countdown board is gone from the kit. The NOW SERVING sign over the Reaper's deli counter carries the same information: 150,000 every 24 hours, then a live count in the corner of the next two scenes, then the same sign turned to the wall behind the reader.
- The humans are the stereotype cast: the fat cat first in line at the counter, the weapons maker walking a missile on a leash; the queue, the clerk, the patient, the reader and the doctor are everyday humans whose builds come from their outfits.
- The gags go all the way: the eraser on the title card, the bribe the Reaper takes and then serves the fat cat anyway, the skeleton still filling in claim forms under the pile, the missile that ignores the disease and blows up the oil, the jalopy parts on the blueprint.
- deletion-board slides in from the title card (a title card has no wall screen to zoom into), as loved-ones does in Part 1. fifty-nine-elevens continues holocaust-every-40-days' calendar with no seam.
- Every scene has a Sound line.
