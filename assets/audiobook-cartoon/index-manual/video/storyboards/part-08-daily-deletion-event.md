# Part 8 storyboard: "The Problem: The Daily Deletion Event"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "The Daily Deletion Event". It links no `knowledge/` pages; the daily toll is a variable, and the comparisons are worked from it.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_disease_deaths_daily` = 150 thousand deaths a day. That is one every 0.576 s (86,400 s / 150,000).
  - The Holocaust killed about 6 million: 6,000,000 / 150,000 = 40 days.
  - The 11 September attacks killed 2,977: 150,000 / 2,977 = about 50 a day.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** disease deletes 150,000 people a day, a Holocaust every 40 days, and each death is a fixable fault nobody is fixing.
- **Plates:**
  - `deli`: the Grim Reaper's deli counter, an engraved Victorian butcher's shop on aged paper. Over the counter hangs a NOW SERVING board with flip digits. Behind the counter, on the shop wall, a big chalkboard: the part's charts are chalked on it. A door marked DELETED at the counter's end; the queue runs out of the shop and off the paper.
  - `living-room`: YOU in his wingback armchair under a lamp, drawn as a Victorian anatomical engraving, his front cut away to show his insides. Through a cutaway wall on the right, a doctor's consulting room.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** `reaper` (an engraving version of `assets/art/reaper`, in a butcher's apron and paper cap), `now-serving` (the board, digits in code), `chalkboard`, `skeleton`, `fat-cat`, `customer` (a barrel man in overalls with a chest hatch), `beetle`, `punched-tape`, `calendar`, `hitler`, `claims-clerk`, `claim-form`, `towers` (a small pair of towers, the 9/11 unit), `uncle-sam` (existing), `missile` on a `leash`, `germ`, `oil-derrick`, `mushroom` (existing), `you` (with pieces: a cutaway torso and organs), `armchair`, `doctor`, `folded-card`, `cell`, `protein`, `scar`.
- **Callbacks:** YOU and his armchair come back in Part 9 (weirdly-calm) and Part 17 (stop-reading). The deli, its NOW SERVING board and the count (150,001 on "software.", then one every 0.576 s) come back in Part 26 (countdown-104).
- **New art:** the `deli` shop with the empty chalkboard; the `living-room` with the doctor's room through the wall; every cut-out in the Cast except `uncle-sam` and `mushroom`. Digits, chalk labels, stamps and card text are in code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `count`: flip digits that run from one number to another, or tick up at a set rate (the NOW SERVING board).
  - `stack`: copies of one cut-out piling into a bar, one per drawing (40 calendar pages; 50 towers).
  - `chart`: chalk axes, bars and labels drawn on the chalkboard (a chalk look of the same move Part 11 needs).
  - `xray`: a white flash and a swap to the cut-out's skeleton.
