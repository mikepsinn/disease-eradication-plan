# Part 8 storyboard: "The Problem: The Daily Deletion Event"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper with red (war money) and teal (medicine money). "PiP" means Wishonia talks from the round corner frame.

**Spine.** The countdown board, then one body. The board fills the frame at 150,000 a day, shrinks to the top-left corner and keeps counting while the same number becomes a calendar and a row of date tags. It slides out when the camera turns to one human reading, and the part ends inside that human: first the breaking parts, then the blueprint that says each part can be fixed.

**Callback.** The countdown board from loved-ones (Part 1) comes back full size in deletion-board.

**Callback.** The reader from falling-apart sits on the wall screen again in weirdly-calm (Part 9).

**Held frame.** deletion-board, from "hours" to "bugs". holocaust-every-40-days, from "days" to "insurance". fifty-nine-elevens, from "day" to "invades". falling-apart and dissolving-on-schedule are the slowest scenes in the episode: one slow push-in and small moves only.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
