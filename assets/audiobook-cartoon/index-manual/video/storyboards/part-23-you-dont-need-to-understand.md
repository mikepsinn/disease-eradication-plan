- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "But You Don't Need to Understand Any of That". The pencil quote is Milton Friedman on *Free to Choose* (PBS, 1980; `friedman1980` in `references.bib`), after Leonard Read's essay "I, Pencil". The Scoreboard's two numbers are from `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - A pencil costs 25 cents (spoken; a one-off figure, not a parameter).
  - Thousands of strangers, dozens of countries, billions of times a day, a million voters, millions of people: all spoken, none of them parameters.
  - The Scoreboard's two numbers (not spoken; they set up Part 24): `global_hale_current`, 63.3 years of healthy life, and `global_median_after_tax_income_2025`, $2,138 a year.
