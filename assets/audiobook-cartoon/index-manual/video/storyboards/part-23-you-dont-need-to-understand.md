# Part 23 storyboard: "But You Don't Need to Understand Any of That"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper with red (war money) and teal (medicine money). "PiP" means Wishonia talks from the round corner frame.

**Spine.** The engraved world map. The pencil's parts fly across it in pencil-parts-map, its strangers line its routes in pencil-strangers, the cure's chain of strangers spans it in the two cure-chain scenes, and in millions-cooperated it fills with dots. The pencil half and the disease half use the same map, the same TV set and the same empty office, so the second half reads as a copy of the first.

**Callback.** empty-central-office comes back in millions-cooperated, dustier. The TV set from pencil-on-tv comes back in cured-disease-jar.

**Callback set up here.** The Scoreboard in two-numbers-did-it is the game board's Scoreboard in Part 24. The switch in the-switch is the game.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos. The economist on TV is a generic human; the narrator does not name him.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
