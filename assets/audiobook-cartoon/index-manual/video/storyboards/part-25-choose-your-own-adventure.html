Changes from v2

  • Wishonia's office, the wall screen and her corner are gone. She has five host shots at the lectern (fork-in-the-road, not-complicated, the reaction cuts audit-side-eye and retrospect-look, dont-know-what-to-tell-you), and the rest is four plates; the two futures are one plate, the-roads, a road seen from the side with the fork in the middle, Future A going left and Future B going right.
  • The fork is a fingerpost that springs up beside her lectern, and "I modeled both paths" splits YOU in two there. The graph paper, the dotted roads and the trend arrows are gone: the economist's straight line is a plank laid across a row of dominoes, and it flattens him when they fall.
  • New host shot, not-complicated: the charred YOU from earn-1-57-times walks onto the set and his arm falls off, instead of a "NOT COMPLICATED" sticker.
  • Text that wrote the narration out is cut: the 20 YEARS bracket, "SOVIET UNION: 15%", the PARASITIC tape, FAKE CASES and REAL CASES, the GAUZE and EXTORTION budget board, the PRESENT tags, the RANSOMWARE screen (a grinning skull now), the loop's station names, the equation card, the "What's 'war'?" bubble and "TERRIBLE IDEA" (her stamp now says SUBOPTIMAL). The fax machine with a red X is a carrier pigeon.
  • rot-in-place's pull-back to a small Earth is now the end of road A: past the COLOMBIA sign the road stops at a cliff, the refugees walk off it in step without noticing and drop, and 2043 to 2050 are on the valley floor below. No hang in mid-air, no look at the camera and no "?" sign: Part 9's no-roads has that gag.
  • Part 11's gauge, headstone and blank PLAN clipboard stand in 2033, and future-b-2040's loop is Part 2's money loop, the good copy.
  • Two reaction cuts of about a second split the-roads, which ran 195 s without her: audit-side-eye on "audit." (a side-eye at road A's collapse) and retrospect-look on "retrospect." (a skeptical look at the economists). The plate comes back where the camera would have got to.
  • future-b-2035: YOU rides along on top of the goods on the conveyor, and the economist's desk joins him on the belt, instead of YOU buried under a heap with his head out (Part 23's billions-a-day buries him).
