# Part 14 storyboard: "Your Decentralized FDA"

**Source:** `index-manual.qmd` "Your Decentralized FDA", with its "Real-Time Safety Monitoring", "Treatment Rankings" and "Outcome Labels" subsections and the Optimitron paragraph; `knowledge/solution/dfda.qmd` and `knowledge/solution/optimocracy.qmd`.

**The numbers (from `_variables.yml`):**
- `dih_treasury_medical_research_pct` 80% of `treaty_annual_funding` $27.2 billion a year goes to patients in trials (about $21.8 billion).
- `dfda_pragmatic_trial_cost_per_patient` $929 against `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` $41,000: `dfda_trial_cost_reduction_factor` 44.1×. On a bar chart, the $41,000 bar is 44 of the $929 bars.
- `antidepressant_trial_exclusion_rate` 86.1% of real patients are turned away from today's trials (from `dfda.qmd`; not spoken): about 6 in every 7.
- `dih_patients_fundable_annually` 23.4 million patients a year against `current_trial_slots_available` 1.9 million: `dfda_trial_capacity_multiplier` 12.3×. The 1.9 million bar is 12 times shorter.
- `status_quo_queue_clearance_years` 443 years: `diseases_without_effective_treatment` 6,650 diseases ÷ `new_disease_first_treatments_per_year` 15 a year, so the last one waits until about 2469. At 12.3× it is about 185 a year, and `dfda_queue_clearance_years` 36 years: done by about 2062.
- `dfda_trial_capacity_treatment_acceleration_years` 204 years sooner (the average wait for a first treatment, `status_quo_avg_years_to_first_treatment` 222 years, falls to about 18), plus `efficacy_lag_years` 8.2 years, makes `dfda_trial_capacity_plus_efficacy_lag_years` 212 years.
- `dfda_trial_capacity_plus_efficacy_lag_lives_saved` 10.7 billion deaths: 107 figures in a pictogram at 100 million each.
- Spoken but not parameters: 3,000 years ago, 200 humans, "12% got headaches, 3% were severe", donuts in 2003, 40-page inserts, 7 minutes per appointment, 10,000 jurisdictions.

**What the viewer must leave with:** pay patients to join trials instead of paying committees, at $929 a patient instead of $41,000, and twelve times as many people get tested treatments, which reach everyone 212 years sooner and prevent 10.7 billion deaths; and every drug gets a live, honest label.
