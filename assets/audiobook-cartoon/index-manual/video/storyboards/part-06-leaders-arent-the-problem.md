# Part 6 storyboard: "Why Your Leaders Aren't the Problem"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "Why Your Leaders Aren't the Problem". The section links no `knowledge/` pages; its numbers come from `_variables.yml`.
- **The numbers:**
  - "over two billion humans suffering from disease": spoken, not a variable. About one human in four.
  - `military_to_government_clinical_trials_spending_ratio` = 604. Military spending is `global_military_spending_annual_2024` = $2.72 trillion, so government clinical trials get about $4.5 billion ($2.72T / 604).
  - The redirect is 1% of military spending: the weapons makers lose 1 bill in 100.
  - `treaty_trajectory_gdp_vs_current_trajectory_multiplier_year_15` = 1.43x: the world economy 15 years after the treaty, against the current path.
  - `treaty_annual_funding` = $27.2 billion a year (1% of $2.72T), into the biotech sector.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** nobody in the loop is a monster and nobody in it wins, so the plan keeps the loop and pays it to cure diseases instead.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-loop`: an engraved flow diagram on aged paper. Four stations round a ring, joined by inked arrows that run counterclockwise: the weapons factory at the top, the Politician's desk on the left, the ballot box at the bottom, the Capitol on the right. A lane leaves the ring at 2 o'clock and runs up to the Weapons Maker's street: his house, and across from it a dark lab. Most of the part is the camera travelling over this one sheet.
  - `living-room`: a Victorian parlour wall, half striped wallpaper, with curtains and a fire extinguisher.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth hanging in the glass wall beside her.
- **Cast:** `politician` (existing; with a tie piece tinted red or blue), `boot` (existing, the Gilliam foot), `weapons-maker`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `devil-costume` (horns, mask and tail as separate pieces), `teacup`, `desk`, `factory`, `ballot-box`, `citizen` (three tints), `capitol`, `democracy-dog` (with pieces: head, legs, a tail that swaps between `tail-war` and `tail-pills`), `skeleton`, `trapdoor`, `germ`, `weapons-maker-family` (wife and two children), `portrait`, `reaper`, `house`, `lab`, `scissors`, `share-certificate`, `shackle`, `halo`, `wallpaper-red`, `wallpaper-blue`, `paste-brush`, `flames`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 1's 604 bar (604-papers) is the bar chart in politician-drinks-tea. The Democracy Dog's two tails come back in Part 20 (democracy-auction: the war tail) and Part 25 (future-b-2040: the pill tail); this part draws them first.
- **New art:** every cut-out in the Cast except `politician` and `boot`; the `the-loop` sheet itself (the ring, the four stations, the lane and the street) as one large engraving with no words on it; the `living-room` wall. Signs and labels are cards set in code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `ink`: a line or arrow draws itself from one point to another over a set time (the loop's arrows, the bar chart's axis).
  - `march`: copies of one cut-out walk a path in single file, popping in at its start (bills round the ring, customers to the door).
  - `stack`: copies of one cut-out pile into a column, one per drawing, to a set count or height (the 604 bar, the revenue stack of 100).
  - `string`: a line that stays tied between two moving points (the halo's string, the shackles' chains).
  - `tint`: recolour a piece (the tie, red to blue and back).
