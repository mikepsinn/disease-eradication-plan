# Part 2 storyboard: "The Human Economy"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "The Human Economy". It links no `knowledge/` pages; its number comes from `knowledge/appendix/parameters-and-calculations.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `war_deaths_since_1900`: 310 million (wars, conflicts, genocides and policy famines since 1900: democide 264 million, battle deaths 39 million and civilians caught in the fighting 30 million, less 25 million of overlap).
  - For the chart: deaths add up from 1900 to 310 million in 2025, climbing steepest in the two world wars (1914 to 1918 and 1939 to 1945) and in smaller steps between and after.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the economy is a loop that prints paper from nothing, pays for weapons, pays the survivors to build more, sells them food that grows for free, and has killed 310 million people since 1900.
- **Plates:**
  - `loop-road`: a loop road on aged paper with four stops: the printing press, the weapons factory, the family's house, the farm stand.
  - `headstone-field`: a line chart on aged paper, "WAR DEATHS SINCE 1900", its line a chain of headstones.
  - Host scenes (host-opens, host-closes): Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `politician`, `mushroom`, `rubble`, `headstone`. From Part 1: `moth`, `weapons-maker`, `bill-war`, `wheat`, `hungry-man`. New: `printing-press`, `factory`, `baby-missile`, `family`, `family-skeleton`, `house`, `pay-window`, `farm-stand`, `farmer`, `loaf`, `price-tag`, `sandwich`.
- **Callbacks:**
  - Part 1 (without-with-papers): the moth from the hungry man's empty wallet flutters out of the press's empty paper tray; the wheat that grows for free grows by the farm stand and gets a price tag; the hungry man is host-closes's guest.
  - Part 1 (without-with-papers): the Weapons Maker, charred by his own bomb, is the one at the factory door.
- **New art:**
  - a Victorian flatbed printing press with a crank wheel and an empty paper tray
  - a brick factory whose three chimneys are grinning missiles, a door, and a pay window with a shutter; a hairy forearm with a missile tattoo
  - the Weapons Maker's smiling baby missile, which can fly off
  - a little house, and the same house as rubble
  - the family of three (a father, a mother, a small kid), charred versions, and their skeletons, each holding a loaf
  - a farm stand with a striped awning and loaves, a farmer in a straw hat, a price tag on a string
  - a 1950s club sandwich with a toothpick and an olive, and the same with one bite gone
  - a cut-out of the money loop small enough to sit on the console ledge (press, factory, house, farm stand on a ring of road), and scissors
- **Kit additions:**
  - `walk` along a closed path (the loop road), for a line of bills.
  - `chain`: copies of a cut-out placed along a curve over time, each popping out of the last (the headstone line).
  - `chart`: axes, gridlines, labels.
  - `xray` and `drain` (the plate goes gray except the named cut-outs).
  - Host props for `KIT.studio`: the paper loop on the ledge, scissors, tape, the sandwich on her palm; a spotlight on the Earth in the window.
