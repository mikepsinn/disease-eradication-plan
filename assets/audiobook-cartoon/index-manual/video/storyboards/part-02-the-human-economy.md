# Part 2 storyboard: "The Human Economy"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper; the money is green, and its seal says what it buys (a red missile for war, a teal cross for medicine). "PiP" means Wishonia in her Layer B corner: standing in the bottom right corner in 16:9 (cut off by the frame's bottom edge), in her flying saucer at the top right in 9:16, with her gesture body showing.

**Spine.** The economy circle. It is born as the O of ECONOMY on the title card (a hamster wheel with one paper running in it), opens into a ring track with five stations that start up on the words in economy-circle, freezes, drains to gray, turns its people into skeletons and its track into headstones in economy-310-million, and stays frozen on the wall screen while Wishonia skips the whole thing with a sandwich in food-directly. One drawing, four scenes: the circle's drawing code is copied between the four files.

**Callbacks.** From Part 1 (without-with-papers): the wheat stalk that grows by itself comes back behind the farm stand, and the moth from the empty wallet flies out of the printing press's empty paper tray. From Part 1 (papers-with-presidents): the papers' squeaky little footsteps. The mug sip (style bible, gag 3).

**Set up here.** The sandwich and the hungry human from food-directly: he is the walking human who eats it all through debug-37-trillion-cells (Part 4), so draw him with the options recorded in food-directly's file. The printing press with the empty tray comes back as the 1913 press in nothing-papers (Part 10); the smiling missiles with price tags come back in Part 10's shopping cart. The ring with stations comes back as the Democracy Dog loop (Part 6).

**Held frame.** economy-310-million, from after "million". Nothing moves until "On" in food-directly.

**Transitions.** The title card cuts in. Its O becomes the circle: economy-circle zooms into the O's hole, which is the Earth paper. economy-310-million is the same picture with no seam. food-directly zooms out of the wall screen, which still shows the held frame.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
- No real banknotes or currency symbols on the papers; they are the generic papers from Part 1. (The dollar signs in the Weapons Maker's eyes are the style bible's greedy eyes, not money.)

## Changes from v1

- The humans are the stereotype cast: the Politician cranks the printing press, the Weapons Maker cradles his missile at the factory door, and an everyday family (charred after the bomb) works the jobs window and buys the bread.
- Every gag goes all the way: the missiles blow up the town on "everything", the family stands there charred, and on "killed" every human in the circle x-rays into a grinning skeleton while the track stands up as headstones.
- The title card's O is the circle (a hamster wheel), so the part opens on its spine instead of a plain card.
- Money is green with seals: the circle runs on war-seal papers, and they are the only thing that keeps its color when the circle drains.

- act: The economy

- status: planned

Moves: "Humanity" → the zoom through the O lands: the ring fills the frame, empty, with five blank plaques round it, and the hamster-wheel paper still jogging in place at the bottom. "magnificent:" → a 1ST PRIZE rosette drops from the sky and pins itself on the jogging paper; it stops, puffs out its chest and struts up the ring to plaque 1 (humanity's magnificent creation is the money). "print" → the printing press thumps down on plaque 1, and the Politician (toothy grin, flag pin, very wide tie) cranks its handle. "papers" (first) → ka-chunk: a war-seal paper pops out with each turn and lines up behind the prize paper. "nothing" → the press's paper tray is empty; a moth flutters out of it (the moth from Part 1's empty wallet), and the press keeps printing. "monetary policy" → a brass plaque "MONETARY POLICY" screws itself onto the press, right over the empty tray. "nothing-papers" → the papers hop off the press and conga clockwise along the ring on squeaky little feet, the prize paper in front. "weapons makers." → plaque 2: the factory, its chimneys shaped like missiles; the Weapons Maker stands in the door cradling his smiling missile like a baby while the papers file in past him, and his eyes ring up dollar signs, one per paper. "They" → plaque 3 fills in: a tidy little town of three houses, a family of three on the front lawn. "make" → the chimneys puff and a conveyor rolls smiling missiles out of the factory's side door, each with a price tag swinging from its nose. "that" → the missiles take off along the ring toward the town; the family waves at them, and the missiles wave back. "destroy" → they dive. "everything." → BOOM: a white flash, the frame shakes, a mushroom cloud stands over the town. after "everything." → the cloud clears: the houses are flat, and the family stands in the rubble charred black and smoking, hair straight up, only their eyes blinking, still waving. "jobs" → plaque 4: a pay window pops up out of the rubble, its "JOBS" sign held up by two white-gloved hands making air quotes, and the family lines up at it. "give people papers." → a hairy forearm with a missile tattoo reaches out of the window and gives each of them a hard hat and one war-seal paper; they put the hard hats on over the soot. "People use papers" → they march along the ring to plaque 5, a farm stand piled with bread, where a farmer in overalls waits. "buy food" → each pays one paper and gets one loaf. "grows" → behind the stand, a wheat stalk grows by itself (the self-growing wheat from Part 1), then the whole field shoots up. after "free)." → the farmer runs over and slaps a price tag on the wheat. "economy". → a banner unrolls across the middle of the ring: "THE ECONOMY". after "economy". → the farmer's papers conga on along the ring, back up to the press, closing the circle, and the factory launches the next smiling missile; at the farm stand the charred family, mid-bite, looks up at it.
