# Part 27 storyboard: "Outro"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper; the money is green, and its seal says what it buys (a red missile for war, a teal cross for medicine). "PiP" means Wishonia in her Layer B corner: standing in the bottom right corner in 16:9 (cut off by the frame's bottom edge), in her flying saucer at the top right in 9:16, with her gesture body showing.

**Spine.** The glowing rectangle. Wishonia stays at the desk the whole time; her wall screen shows the Earth paper, where YOU (below) holds a glowing rectangle that carries each ask on its screen: SHARE, the stars, the warondisease.org page, the YES box. Every "too busy" shrinks the rectangle a size (a slide whistle down), so the asks get smaller while YOU gets busier and the world around the rectangle takes over the picture (the friend, the ALGORITHM, Grandma, the slider, the WANTED poster). On "Go to WarOnDisease.org" the biggest glowing rectangle in the room, Wishonia's own wall screen, becomes the end card.

**YOU.** The viewer is the busy human in the bowler from Part 1's title card, as in Parts 10, 12 and 13 (`{ tone: 2, outfit: "suit", hat: "bowler" }`, the same options in every scene). **Running gag: too busy.** Each "too busy" makes him busier, three times with a twist: asleep (thanked anyway), buried in calendar pages, juggling, then on fire, running in circles. The fire goes out on "least" and he stays charred black, hair straight up and smoking, to the end of the episode: he drags the slider, he is the mugshot on the WANTED poster, and he runs to warondisease.org.

**Callback.** The stamped EARTH: CASE #847 file from eligible-final-stamp (Part 26) goes into the PLANETS out-tray from eligible (Part 1), on top of VENUS: TOO HOT and MARS: NOBODY HOME. Grandma from grandmas-war-bond (Part 17), who "still died of cancer in 1987", comes back as a skeleton with her BUY BONDS poster. The slider pops out of the phone like the toast in split-the-atom (Part 1). "2.6 lives" and "468,000 hours" are the numbers from countdown-104 (Part 26). The runner from Part 1's title card (the bowler, the wristwatch) closes the episode. Wishonia's blink-smile (a wink) on "Love you, bye!" (style bible).

**Held frame.** None. No death toll is spoken in this part.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos. The glowing rectangle, its share arrow, the stars and the vote page are drawn, not a real phone, app or store screenshot. Real names appear as text only (the end card credit).
- No countdown board (it is gone from the kit; this part never used it).
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Wishonia moves on the words in every scene, and so does something on her wall screen.
- Wishonia does not use body-point-right in this part: it points to the viewer's right, away from the wall screen in 16:9.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
