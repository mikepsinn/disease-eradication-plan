# Part 27 storyboard: "Outro"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper with red (war money) and teal (medicine money). "PiP" means Wishonia talks from the round corner frame.

**Spine.** The shrinking asks. Wishonia stays at the desk the whole time, and each ask is a card on the wall screen. Every "too busy" shrinks the card a size, so share, review, allocate and vote get smaller in turn, until the end card fills the screen again.

**Callback.** The color flip in wishocratic-slider (the slider walks red papers through the funnel to the teal side). "2.6 lives" and "468,000 hours" from countdown-104 (Part 26). Wishonia's blink-smile on "Love you, bye!" (style bible).

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos. The share button, the stars and the vote page are drawn, not app or store screenshots.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Wishonia moves on the words in every scene.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
