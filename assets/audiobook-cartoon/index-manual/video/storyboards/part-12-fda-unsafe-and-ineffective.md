# Part 12 storyboard: "The FDA Is Unsafe and Ineffective"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper; the money is green, and its seal says what it buys (a red missile for war, a teal cross for medicine). "PiP" means Wishonia in her Layer B corner: standing in the bottom right corner in 16:9 (cut off by the frame's bottom edge), in her flying saucer at the top right in 9:16, with her gesture body showing.

**Spine.** The approval cabinet. A glass cabinet with a padlock. The safe drug goes into it in committee-8-years, the lifeguard locks the life preserver in the same kind of cabinet in lifeguard-cabinet, and the 3,389 wait in front of it in 3389-to-1. The alarms that should open it arrive late: by mail, by fax, or taped over.

**Callback.** In suboptimal-stamp, Wishonia stamps the Earth case file "SUBOPTIMAL" (style bible, gag 5), the second stamp after "MAY BE ELIGIBLE FOR OPTIMIZATION" in eligible (Part 1).

**Callback.** The family portrait from loved-ones (Part 1) and the warehouse shelf from untested-warehouse (Part 9) come back in someone-you-love. The receipt from bought-the-other-thing (Part 10) comes back as murder-receipt.

**Held frame.** vioxx-by-fax, from "thousand" to "approved". 3389-to-1, from "waiting" to "locked". main-product-dead-patients, from "patients" to the end of the scene. child-scientist, from "cure" to the end. murder-receipt, from "murder" to "suboptimal" in the next scene.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
- Vioxx, Thalidomide, FDA and NIH appear as plain text labels only; no real drug packaging and no agency seals.
