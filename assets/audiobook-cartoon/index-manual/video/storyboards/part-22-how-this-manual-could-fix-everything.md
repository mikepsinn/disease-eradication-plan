# Part 22 storyboard: "How This Manual Could Fix Everything"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "How This Manual Could Fix Everything". The section links no `knowledge/` page.
- **The numbers:** none. The section is a list of what the manual contains, so nothing on screen is a figure. The bills in "Exact amounts" carry no numbers: the joke is precision, shown with scissors.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the manual is built for humans as they are (sick, busy, greedy and vain), and it bribes them into not dying instead of asking them to be better.
- **Plates:**
  - Host shots: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window. In manual-contains the manual lands on the ledge. In bribe-humanity a tin wastebasket stands by the console.

  - dysfunction-engine: plain aged paper, with one Gilliam contraption. A treadmill drives a belt, the belt turns three gears, and the gears work a pill press that drops bottles down a chute to a line of patients.
