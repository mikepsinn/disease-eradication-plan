# Part 19 storyboard: "Step 3: Bribe the Bribers"

**Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "Step 3: Bribe the Bribers" (with "Current Job" and "Your Offer"); `knowledge/solution/aligning-incentives.qmd` (the lobbyists' returns, the two jobs).

**The numbers:**
- Lobbying return: $1,813 back per dollar (narrated; the book cites `lobbying-roi-calc`; the aligning-incentives page says this is the top five firms over the twenty-year Afghan War). On a bar chart the GOT BACK bar is 1,813 times the PUT IN bar: if one coin is 10 px tall, the column is 18,130 px, about 17 screen heights.
- `lobbyist_salary_min_k`: $500,000 a year, the same in both jobs.
- `treaty_trajectory_gdp_vs_current_trajectory_multiplier_year_15`: the economy 1.43 times larger at year 15 on the treaty path than on the current one.

**What the viewer must leave with:** lobbyists work for whoever pays, so you hire them away at the same salary to lobby for trials, in a world where they don't die and the economy is 1.43 times larger.

- title card: the period card on navy, the atom emblem with three war bills running its orbits.
- host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth hanging in space beside her; the Lobbyist is her guest beside the console.
- democracy-slot: the Capitol on a plain field with a coin slot in its door, and a two-bar chart beside it, "PUT IN" and "GOT BACK", whose second bar runs far above the frame.
- the-ledger: one ledger page, two columns, "CURRENT JOB" and "YOUR OFFER", three rows, "SALARY", "MORAL STATUS" and "LEGACY". The MORAL STATUS row is one number line running across both columns.
- two-worlds: a line chart on aged paper, "THE ECONOMY, NEXT 15 YEARS", two lines from the same start; each ends at a little globe sized to its value, 1 and 1.43.
