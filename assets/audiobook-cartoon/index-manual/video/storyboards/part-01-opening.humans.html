Human Design Options

Each candidate design for the humans, drawn large in four moods, then in two Part 1 scenes: the family portrait, where they must still feel like people, and the crowd snapping toward the paper, where they must be funny.

1. Expressive beans

  • The same bean bodies, rivets and chest lights, with big eyes that can look and roll, eyebrows, six mouth shapes and white gloves.
  • The cheapest change: one drawing function, and every bean in the storyboards keeps working.

2. Mid-century people

  • Flat geometric people in Wishonia's own 1960s cartoon style: oval heads, suits and dresses, hats, eyebrows that act, and noses that point where they look.
  • The meat-robot joke stays: rivets at the elbows and knees, and the chest light on the suit.
  • They can play types (politician, lobbyist, scientist, Grandma) by clothes and hair, not only by props.

3. Cut-out paper puppets

  • Flat cut pieces with a cream paper edge and a drop shadow, joined by brass pins that stand in for the rivets; heads in profile with a jaw that drops open.
  • They suit the engraved-paper Earth scenes, and animate as stiff, jerky hinges, which is funny in itself.

4. Sitcom people

  • An original TV-cartoon design: heads a third of the body, big round eyes with pupils and lids that act, clear hair silhouettes, a few lip-sync mouths, four-finger white gloves.
  • It borrows how TV cartoons work, not any show's look: real skin tones, no overbite, eyes inside the face.
  • The meat-robot joke stays: rivets at the elbows and knees, and the chest light on the shirt.

5. Stick figures with hats

  • Line bodies and round heads with a simple face; who someone is comes from the hat (top hat for the Rich Human, hard hat, bowler) and the pose, the way OverSimplified tells whole wars.
  • The fastest to draw and animate, and crowds of thousands stay cheap; the rivets become the joint dots, and the chest light sits on the body line.
  • The joke of the contrast: Wishonia is a detailed cartoon, and humans are the simple diagrams Wishonia draws of them.
