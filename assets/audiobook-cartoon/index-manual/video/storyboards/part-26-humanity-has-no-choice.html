Part 26: The Part Where Humanity Has No Choicev3

Every group's greed pulls the same way, so humanity may cure disease by accident; the viewer's part is one vote at warondisease.org and two friends, while 104 humans stop every minute.

1920 × 1080 · 1:15.7 · 8 scenes · audio index-manual.cbaf659d.mp3, 55:37.7 to 56:53.5

Changes from v2

  • Wishonia's office, the wall screen, the engraved paper and her corner (PiP) are gone. She hosts three shots from her lectern (the-twist, cheese-genius, eligible-final-stamp); the cart, the plain and the deli are plates the camera travels over, and greed-ropes and cure-death-by-mistake are one plate with no seam.
  • "The twist ... (though it is)" is now a host shot: the halo lowers onto Wishonia's antennae and rolls off the lectern on "but because:", then rolls back in on the treaty road and lands on the fat cat's top hat.
  • The four groups no longer bring their prizes: each harness is a trap baited with its prize on a pole, so every puller walks in after the bait and the harness snaps shut round him, and "aligns" is the four baits swinging into one row ahead of one column.
  • The fat cat's walk into the UTOPIA 1 MILE sign moved off the wall screen onto the plate, on "Humans aren't stupid."; the cheese, the milk and the medal stand on a county-fair prize table beside the lectern.
  • The alien saucers are 1950s B-movie saucers on visible strings that dangle into the host shot and get yanked away; the case file is on the lectern (as in Parts 12 to 15), not in the out-tray.
  • No slides between plates: every seam is a cut, an iris or no seam.
  • doubling-starts left the stadium, which repeated Part 18's you-are-everyone (a lone voter, an empty stadium that fills, a crowd-surf): the voters branch out across a bare plain on their cane hooks, under a tally board with room for billions, and the payoff is the bird on its never-turned billions tile.
