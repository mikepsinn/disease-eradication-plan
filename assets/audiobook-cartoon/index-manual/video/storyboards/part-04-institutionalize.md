# Part 4 storyboard: "What to Do When They Try to Institutionalize You"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. The part is the 1950s atomic-age instructional film. Layer A is Wishonia at her lectern on the studio set (`KIT.studio`), talking to the viewer, with her mug and the Earth case file on the lectern; Layer B is an illustrated plate the camera travels over (`KIT.camera`), one framing per phrase, and Wishonia is only the voice there. Money is money green; a missile seal is war money, a cross seal medicine money. Part 2's scenes (`compositions/scenes/part-02/`) are the reference for each kind of shot.
