Part 13: The Solution: A 1% Treatyv3

The fix is one treaty: every country moves 1% of its military budget to clinical trials at the same time, and humans have already signed treaties that asked for more.

1920 × 1080 · 2:12.1 · 12 scenes · audio index-manual.cbaf659d.mp3, 25:42.0 to 27:54.0

Changes from v2

  • The film look replaces the office and the corner: Wishonia is on screen only at her lectern, in four host shots (sounds-crazy, thats-the-treaty, more-than-sufficient, human-greed), and every Layer B scene is a plate the camera travels over, drawn large.
  • The labels are gone (the funnel's "1%", "OBJECTION 2", "ENDS CIVILIZATION", "STOPPED AT PAGE 1", the "ANTIBIOTICS" tag, "THE DATA", the wall-screen titles). The snowflake grid becomes a tower of 122 crates: one comes out from under it like the tablecloth trick, and the other 121 fall over and go off in a row to the horizon.
  • 847-civilizations becomes a plate of 847 planets in space with Earth, case #847, nearest the camera and sweating; next-page happens on Earth's top, on the same plate.
  • The cuckoo now lives in the lectern ("crazy", "craziness") before it pops out of the book; the one-line treaty unrolls from her hand at the lectern and comes back as the fourth scroll.
  • The objection "humans would never agree" and the answer "You already have" move to the lectern (more-than-sufficient), where she plunks the three signed treaties down; YOU signs them in the plate.
  • human-greed is a host shot with a guest: the fat cat runs a hamster wheel on the stage after a paper on a stick strapped to his own hat, and lights the studio.
