Part 9: The Unexplored Therapeutic Frontierv3

95% of human diseases have no approved treatment, and at the current rate testing the untested uses of 9,500 known safe compounds would take 443 years, far longer than any viewer will live.

1920 × 1080 · 1:04.6 · 7 scenes · audio index-manual.cbaf659d.mp3, 16:10.6 to 17:15.2

Changes from v2

  • The film look: the office, the wall screen, the cream paper and Wishonia's corner are gone. Wishonia is on screen only at her lectern: zero-treatments opens the argument and weirdly-calm ends it. Everything else happens on two plates.
  • 95-percent-untreated is split in two. Its 10 by 10 grid of tagged blobs (a busy diagram) becomes one disease blob at the lectern, still wearing the explorer's pith helmet from the title card, drinking Wishonia's coffee (zero-treatments). The shut window and the bearded knocker become back-soon.
  • The FDA window, the queue and the warehouse are one place, the-queue plate, and the camera travels over it. The knocker from back-soon is the human the window finally serves on "treatment", and the human whose nose the shutter flattens is the skeleton who gets the bottle 443 years later.
  • The warehouse is the building the window is cut into, drawn as a cutaway. The "KNOWN SAFE COMPOUNDS: 9,500" sign is gone (the shelves show it), and the scientist's errand ends in one framing at the window.
  • The queue's tickets are gone (the take-a-number deli is Part 8's): every human walks his own disease on a leash, and the last skeleton's disease holds up the 6,650 placard. The commuter in no-roads wears a fedora (the bowler is YOU's).
  • weirdly-calm moves from the wall screen to the lectern. The 443-year timeline is gone (the wait sign has just said 443). The YOU headstone lands like an anvil beside the lectern, and the reader slides in next to it in his armchair and leans on it.

    05 · queue-to-not-dieB · 16:31.2–16:48.6

    First: "Only" → back on the window: the bearded knocker at the shutter with his cobweb and spider.     cut (back to the-queue, on back-soon's last framing)
    Narration and moves

    "Only fifteen diseases get their first effective treatment each year. Six thousand, six hundred and fifty diseases are still waiting. There is a queue to not die, and it is longer than any queue humans have ever voluntarily stood in, which is saying something because you invented Disneyland."

    Moves: "Only" → back on the window: the bearded knocker at the shutter with his cobweb and spider. An enamel sign drops in on two chains over the window, both its lines blank. "fifteen" → its top line flips to "15 PER YEAR". "diseases" → its bottom line, "WAIT TIME FROM HERE:", starts spinning its digit wheels, and they do not stop. "first" → the shutter flies up. "treatment" → an arm comes out of the window and hands the knocker a pill bottle; his chest light goes out, his disease keels over on the end of its leash, and he skips off out of the frame dragging it behind him like a pull toy, beard flapping, cobweb trailing. "year." → the shutter slams down on the nose of the next human and flattens it; he stays put, nose flat, his disease at his heel (he is served in untested-warehouse). "Six thousand" → the camera pulls back and travels left along the queue: a single file of humans behind a velvet rope on brass posts, every one walking his own disease on a leash, every chest light blinking red; the diseases sit, scratch and sniff each other like dogs at the vet's. "hundred" → it travels faster. "waiting." → further back, the humans are grey, leaning on canes, beards down to their shoes, and their diseases are fat and glossy. "queue to not die" → further back still, the humans x-ray into grinning skeletons one after another as the camera passes, like dominoes, each still holding its leash, still in line; the diseases stay put, grinning. "longer" → the line winds through a graveyard; the headstones stand in line too, each with a disease leashed to it, waiting. "voluntarily" → the line climbs a hill and goes over it. "stood in," → the end of the line at last: one skeleton waves at the camera, bones rattling, and its disease beside it holds up a placard on a stick: "6,650". "Disneyland." → right beside it, a plain theme park (a roller coaster, striped flags) and its short line under its own sign, "WAIT: 45 MIN"; at the front of it the fat cat taps his pocket watch, goes red in the face and steams. after "Disneyland." → the steam blows his top hat off, high out of the frame.

    Sound: a click as the top line flips; a typewriter rattle under the spinning wheels; a ding for the bottle; a slam and a splat on the nose; a whoosh as the camera speeds up; a zap as the skeletons fall like dominoes; a steam whistle and a pop for the hat.

    On screen: "15 PER YEAR" / "WAIT TIME FROM HERE:" and its spinning wheels / "6,650" on the last disease's placard / "WAIT: 45 MIN".

    No: no castle or mascot in the theme park: a roller coaster and striped flags.

    Why: 6,650 waiting diseases as one line too long to see the end of, with the cost at the back.

    Narration and moves

    "There are nine thousand five hundred known safe compounds, and ninety-nine point seven percent of their potential uses have never been tested. At the current discovery rate, finding treatments for all of them will take approximately four hundred and forty-three years."

    Moves: "There" → the camera pulls back to the whole plate: the line on the left, from the theme park over the hill and through the graveyard to the ticket window, and on the right the warehouse, ten times the size of everything else, its front wall cut away right of the window. "nine thousand five hundred" → it travels right and pushes into the warehouse: aisles of shelves going back into the dark, packed with pill bottles up to the roof. "compounds," → it pushes in to one shelf: a row of bottles, thick with dust. "ninety-nine point seven percent" → a tag flips onto each bottle down the row, one after another: "NEVER TESTED FOR THIS"; one bottle in the row gets a check mark instead. "never" → dust puffs off the shelf in a cloud, and cobwebs string between the bottles. "current" → the camera pulls back to a tall rolling ladder: the scientist (lab coat, bun, glasses) starts up it, one slow rung at a time. "rate," → at the top she pulls one bottle and blows the dust off it; the cloud swallows her, and she comes out grey with dust. "finding" → the camera travels with her down the ladder and toward the window; on the way her bun goes white, her back bends, and a cane appears, tapping. "approximately" → the camera settles on the window from outside; behind the counter she hands the bottle out and x-rays into a grinning skeleton in her lab coat and glasses. "four hundred" → at the counter is the human whose nose the shutter flattened, now a skeleton, its disease still on the leash and twice the size; it takes the bottle and shakes it, delighted. "forty-three" → the wait sign's wheels slow down. "years." → they clank to a stop: "WAIT TIME FROM HERE: 443 YEARS". after "years." → through the window, the skeleton scientist shuffles back toward the ladder for the next bottle.

    Sound: a typewriter clatter as the tags flip on; a huff of dust; a squeak per rung; a zap for the x-ray; a clank as the wheels stop.

    On screen: "NEVER TESTED FOR THIS" / "WAIT TIME FROM HERE: 443 YEARS".

    No: no "9,500" or "99.7%" on screen (the shelves and the tags show them).

    Why: the candidates are already on the shelf, and testing them is the line everyone is standing in.
