# Part 7 storyboard: "Humanity's Death Wish"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "Humanity's Death Wish". It links no `knowledge/` pages and states no numbers.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The facts the pictures use:
  - *All Quiet on the Western Front* came out in 1930, between the First World War (1914 to 1918) and the Second (1939 to 1945). *Apocalypse Now* came out in 1979, between the end of the Vietnam War (1975) and the Gulf War (1990 to 1991).
  - Tolstoy wrote *War and Peace*.
  - Alfred Nobel, who endowed the Nobel Peace Prize, invented dynamite and owned the arms maker Bofors.
  - The Department of Defense was called the Department of War until 1947.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** humans already know war is stupid, say so in films, books and prizes, and pay for it anyway.
- **Plates:**
  - `museum`: one long gallery wall on aged paper, five exhibits in the order the narrator lists them, each in its own gilt frame or glass case. The camera travels right along it with one visitor.
  - `living-room`: a Victorian parlour, a card table under a hanging lamp, the board game on it.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** `tourist` (an everyday Victorian man in a checked suit with an audio-guide horn at his ear; he goes bumped, charred, shaved, then a skeleton), `tolstoy`, `nobel`, `prize-winner` (a woman with a dove), `politician` (existing), `weapons-maker`, `rube-goldberg`, `skeleton` (the tourist's), `bill-war`, `bill-plain`, `bomb`, `book` (WAR AND PEACE), `medal`, `dynamite`, `cinema-seat`, `soldier-helmet`, `pentagon`, `missile`, `germ`, `flask`, `display-case`, `snakes-board` (a snakes-and-ladders board with no ladders), `globe` (a tiny Earth game piece, made from existing `globe` scaled), `machine` (existing, the contraption's gears), `boot` (existing), `mushroom` (existing).
- **Callbacks:** the Gilliam `boot` from Part 11's soviet-plan kicks off Rube Goldberg's contraption. The board game box comes back in Part 24 (game-box), relidded as THE EARTH OPTIMIZATION GAME: draw the lid as its own piece.
- **New art:** the `museum` gallery wall with five empty frames and cases; the `living-room` parlour; every cut-out in the Cast that is not marked existing; a war timeline strip (an inked axis 1900 to 2000, war years as grey blocks) for the first exhibit. Titles, years and signs are cards in code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: an inked axis with year ticks and labels, and blocks or bars on it (the war timeline; the spending line in host-digress is drawn in the host kit, not here).
  - `march`: copies of one cut-out walking a path in single file (the war bills).
