# Part 5 storyboard: "The Sacred Order of Paper Distribution"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper with red (war money) and teal (medicine money). "PiP" means Wishonia talks from the round corner frame.

**Spine.** The scroll and one stream of papers. The five steps glow on Wishonia's scroll in sacred-scroll, and every step scene opens on its numbered card from that scroll. One stream of plain (uncolored) papers leaves the Rich Human's hill in Step 1 and passes through every step; in Step 5 the treaty's red papers go through the color-flip funnel and come out teal, and a tenth of them walk back up the hill.

**Callback.** From Part 4 (billionaire-pdf): the Rich Human who turned away from the PDF sits on the hill in step-1-dice-in-a-suit. Set up for later parts: the "I PREFER LIVING" button from 2800-top-hats stays on the Rich Human (style bible, the Rich Human).

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
- The papers in Steps 1 to 4 are plain paper, not red or teal; red and teal start with the treaty in Step 5.
- No real lobbying firms, Super PACs or campaigns; plain signs only.
