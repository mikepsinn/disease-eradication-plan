**Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "Step 2: The Great Clicking"; `knowledge/strategy/global-referendum.qmd` (the TikTok downloads, the referral bonuses).

**The numbers:**
- `global_population_activism_threshold_pct`: 3.5% of humanity. On the Earth beside the console it is a pie wedge of 12.6 degrees, a very thin slice.
- `global_population_2024`: 8 billion people.
