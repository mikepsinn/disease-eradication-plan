Changes from v2

  • No office, no wall screen and no corner: Wishonia is on screen only in the four host shots at her lectern (net-falls-short, less-crazy-goal, diagnostic-criteria, billionaire-pdf), and the humans' scenes are plates (living-room, the-body, game-show, padded-cell, rock-field).
  • New host shots: net-falls-short opens the argument after the title card (the net falls short of her, which "light-years" pays off), less-crazy-goal gives Part 2's hungry human a dashboard gag, and billionaire-pdf ends the part with the STOP sign and the Rich Human as her guests.
  • Labels, cards and narration text are cut: the LAWS OF NATURE chart is a TV weather forecast with a missile in it, the game show's cards are pictures, and the LESS CRAZY GOAL tag, BUILT BY ACCIDENT, the blueprint, the PHYSICALLY POSSIBLE stamp and LARGEST COORDINATED EFFORT are gone.
  • Gags that needed the office are replaced: the distance gauge and the receding office become a pull-back from the studio into space, the patient's fall from the trapdoor crosses the studio on its way to the cell, and "physically possible" is the STOP sign's dent popping back out.
  • The TV's bomb chars the sofa crowd, who nod along; the war wheel's gondolas come alive from crayon one by one and drain back to crayon on "NOT".
  • The wheel's village is busy hanging a WELCOME banner when the missiles land (waving at the missile is Part 2's family's beat).

"cascades," → the rows of centrifuges spin up, pipes branching across the frame, everything humming. "colored" → a rust-colored rock rolls up beside the first; a little heart floats up between them. "thousands" → the sun arcs over again and again, faster and faster; the counter spins up into the hundreds of thousands; moss and little flowers grow on both rocks, and a bird nests on the gray one. "years." → an everyday human strolls in, picks up the rust rock and hurls it off the right edge; the heart pops; after "years." the counter flips back to 0. The gray rock sits alone, with its bird.
