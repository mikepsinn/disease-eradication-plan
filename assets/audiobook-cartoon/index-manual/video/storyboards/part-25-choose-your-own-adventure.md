# Part 25 storyboard: "Choose Your Own Adventure"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("future-a-2035: hold the charred orderly longer"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. The part is a 1950s atomic-age instructional film. Layer A is the host's set (`KIT.studio`): Wishonia large at her navy lectern in front of the mustard wall, her mug and the case file "~~EARTH~~ DIRT: CASE #847" on the lectern, with Part 1's "MAY BE ELIGIBLE FOR OPTIMIZATION" and Part 12's "SUBOPTIMAL" on its cover. Layer B is an illustrated plate on a plain field that the camera travels over (`KIT.camera`), one framing per phrase; Wishonia is not in it, she is the voice. Scenes with the same plate name are one drawing, and the camera keeps traveling from one into the next with no seam. Money is green, and its seal says what it buys: a missile for war, a cross for medicine, none on plain pay. Part 2 (`../compositions/scenes/part-02/`) is the reference for every kind of shot.

**Spine. The fork and the two roads.** The part is a pick-your-path book. The fork is a wooden fingerpost with two arrow boards, "FUTURE A" pointing left and "FUTURE B" pointing right. It springs up beside Wishonia's lectern in fork-in-the-road, and it stands in the middle of the-roads, the plate both futures are told on: one long road across a plain field, seen from the side, with the fingerpost at plate x 0. Road A runs left from the fork, one milestone per year, and the sky behind it steps a shade darker at each one (cream, tan, orange, rust, slate, navy, with more smoke columns on the horizon and more cracks in the road); past the COLOMBIA sign it ends at a cliff, and 2043 to 2050 are on the dark valley floor below. Road B runs right from the fork, and its sky steps brighter at each milestone (a teal wash, and a mustard sun a little higher every year), until its last stack of papers reaches the moon. The road keeps the same height on screen while the camera travels, so the stretches read as one road. The plate constants and drawings (the road, the fingerpost, the milestones, YOU, the sky steps and every station's plate x) are in the sketch module, `part-25-choose-your-own-adventure.sketch.mjs`; the scene files copy them.

**Running gag: what happens to YOU.** The viewer is the busy human in the bowler from Part 1's title card (`{ tone: 2, outfit: "suit", hat: "bowler" }`, as in Parts 10, 12 and 13; the same options in every scene, so he is the same man).
