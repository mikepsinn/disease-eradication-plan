# Part 26 storyboard: "The Part Where Humanity Has No Choice"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("greed-ropes: make the bond bigger"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. The part is a 1950s atomic-age instructional film. Layer A is the host's studio set (`KIT.studio`): Wishonia large at her navy lectern, in front of the mustard wall and its spotlight pool, her coffee mug and the case file "~~EARTH~~ DIRT: CASE #847" on the lectern.
