# Part 26 storyboard: "The Part Where Humanity Has No Choice"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "The Part Where Humanity Has No Choice", through to "This is why aliens don't visit." The section links only warondisease.org; its numbers are parameters.
- **The numbers:**
  - `victory_bond_annual_return_pct`: 272% (the rich humans' returns).
  - `global_disease_deaths_per_minute`: 104 a minute, one every 0.58 seconds.
  - `voter_lives_saved`: 2.6 lives per vote.
  - `voter_suffering_hours_prevented`: 468,000 hours per vote, which is about 53 years of suffering, night and day.
  - The doubling (derived, not spoken): if every voter brings two friends, the total after each round is 1, 3, 7, 15, 31 and so on, and it passes `global_registered_voters` (4.13 billion) in the 31st round.
  - The death rate is the only toll here, and it gets a held frame (see countdown-104).
