- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "The Earth Optimization Game". The section links only warondisease.org. The Scoreboard's two numbers and the 2040 date are parameters from `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `destructive_economy_50pct_year`: 2040, the deadline (the year the destructive economy reaches half of GDP on its current path).
  - The Scoreboard's two numbers (not spoken): `global_hale_current`, 63.3 years of healthy life, and `global_median_after_tax_income_2025`, $2,138 a year.
