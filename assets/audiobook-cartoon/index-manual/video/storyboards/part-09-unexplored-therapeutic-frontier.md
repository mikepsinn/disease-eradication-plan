# Part 9 storyboard: "The Unexplored Therapeutic Frontier"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. The film look of Part 2 (`compositions/scenes/part-02/`): Layer A is the host at her lectern (`KIT.studio`), Layer B is an illustrated plate the camera travels over (`KIT.camera`), and the period film runs over every shot.

**Spine.** One plate, the-queue, and the wait sign that keeps score on it. On its right stands a warehouse ten times the size of everything else; a ticket window is cut into the left end of its front wall, with "FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION" over it. From the window a roped queue of humans runs left across a plain field, through a graveyard and over a hill, and ends beside a theme park. Every human in it walks his own disease on a leash: a knee-high disease blob, each its own lump and color, sitting at his heel like a dog at the vet's. Right of the window the warehouse's front wall is cut away (a cutaway, as in a 1950s instructional film), showing shelf on shelf of pill bottles. back-soon frames the shut window; queue-to-not-die starts there and travels left to the end of the line; untested-warehouse pulls back to the whole plate, goes into the warehouse and ends back at the window. The wait sign drops in over the window on "Only" with blank lines, reads "15 PER YEAR" over "WAIT TIME FROM HERE:" with its digit wheels spinning, and clanks to a stop on "443 YEARS" at the end of untested-warehouse.

**Running gag: waiting ages you, one step further each time.** The human knocking at the shut window grows a beard to the floor (back-soon). The queue goes grey, then turns into skeletons that keep their place and their diseases, which stay on the leash, fat and well (queue-to-not-die). The scientist goes grey fetching one bottle, turns into a skeleton as she hands it over, and the human it was for is a skeleton too by the time it comes (untested-warehouse). The viewer gets a headstone (weirdly-calm).

The mug sip (style bible, gag 3) goes to the disease in zero-treatments: it drinks Wishonia's coffee. The reader from falling-apart (Part 8) slides in beside the lectern in weirdly-calm, in his orange armchair under his floor lamp, still reading the manual, his red ticket for a bookmark (draw him, the chair and the lamp from READER, SEATED, armchair and lamp in Part 8's sketch module). **Set up here.** The ticket window, the wait sign on "443 YEARS" and the roped queue of humans walking their diseases on leashes (grey and bearded further back, then skeletons still holding the leash) come back in accordion-timeline (Part 14), where the line squeezes like an accordion to 36 years, and in future-b-2032 (Part 25), where the sign spins down to NOW.
