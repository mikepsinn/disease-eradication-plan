# Part 9 storyboard: "The Unexplored Therapeutic Frontier"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper with red (war money) and teal (medicine money). "PiP" means Wishonia talks from the round corner frame.

**Spine.** The wait sign. One plain enamel sign hangs over the ticket window at the front of the queue to not die. In queue-to-not-die its top line reads "15 PER YEAR" and its bottom line, "WAIT TIME FROM HERE:", spins without stopping. The window turns out to be the warehouse's, and at the end of untested-warehouse the digits stop on "443 YEARS". In weirdly-calm the same 443 years stands next to the viewer's own "80 YEARS". The part is one long wait, and the sign keeps score.

**Callback.** The reader from falling-apart (Part 8) sits on the wall screen in weirdly-calm, still reading, still calm.

**Callback.** The warehouse shelf from untested-warehouse comes back in someone-you-love (Part 12). The queue of disease blobs under its "WAIT TIME FROM HERE: 443 YEARS" sign comes back in Part 14, where it squeezes like an accordion.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
- No real theme-park characters, castles or logos; the theme park in queue-to-not-die is a plain, unbranded one.
