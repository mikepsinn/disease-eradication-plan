# Part 9 storyboard: "The Unexplored Therapeutic Frontier"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "The Unexplored Therapeutic Frontier", with its citation `95-pct-diseases-no-treatment`. It links no `knowledge/` pages.
- **The numbers:**
  - 95% of diseases have zero FDA-approved treatments (cited, not a variable).
  - 15 diseases a year get their first effective treatment (spoken; the rate inside `status_quo_queue_clearance_years`).
  - `diseases_without_effective_treatment` = 6,650 diseases.
  - `safe_compounds_count` = 9,500 compounds.
  - `unexplored_ratio` = 99.7% of their possible uses never tested.
  - `status_quo_queue_clearance_years` = 443 years. Derived: 6,650 / 15 = 443, so the line clears in about 2468.
  - "dead within eighty years": a viewer alive now is dead by about 2105, 363 years before the line clears.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** at 15 a year the line of untreated diseases takes 443 years to clear, and you are gone in 80.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-queue`: aged paper, a long engraved timeline laid along the ground. At its right end, a Victorian dispensary window under the card FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION, cut into the front of a huge warehouse whose wall is cut away to show shelves of bottles. From the window a roped queue runs left along a ruler on the ground: every 15 people in line is one year, and the ruler carries year marks (2030, 2050, 2105, 2200, 2300, 2400, 2468). Each person in line walks his disease on a leash, a germ the size of a dog.
  - `no-roads`: a Department of Transportation building on an open field with no road, ending at a cliff.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** `scientist` (the Scientist, existing `scientist`, in a pith helmet piece), `germ` (several, each its own lump), `leash`, `leash-human` (everyday Victorians, several), `beard`, `cobweb`, `spider`, `back-soon` (a card on a nail), `pill-bottle`, `skeleton`, `disneyland` (the castle, a coaster and a short queue), `fat-cat` (in mouse ears), `ladder`, `motorcar`, `motorist`, `dot-building`, `headstone` (existing).
- **Callbacks:** none from earlier parts. Set up here: the FDA window, the 443-year ruler and the leashed germs come back in Part 14 (accordion-timeline: the same ruler squeezed to 36 years) and Part 25 (future-b-2032). The warehouse shelf comes back in Part 12 (someone-you-love) and Part 14 (two-hundred-twelve-years-sooner).
- **New art:** the `the-queue` sheet (window, warehouse cutaway, the ruler on the ground, no words); the `no-roads` sheet; every cut-out in the Cast not marked existing. Year marks, signs, tags and the wait board are cards and digits in code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: a waffle grid of 100 squares that fill one by one (the 95%), and the ruler's year marks along a path.
  - `count`: digit wheels that spin and stop on a number (the wait board).
  - `march`: a queue of copies along a path, each copy varied by seed (the patients and their germs).
  - `xray`: a white flash and a swap to a skeleton, run down a line of cut-outs one after another like dominoes.
  - `age`: a cut-out greys and stoops over a set time (the knocker, the queue, the scientist), as a tint plus a swap to an older pose.
