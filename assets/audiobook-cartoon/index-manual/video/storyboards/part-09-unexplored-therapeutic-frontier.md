# Part 9 storyboard: "The Unexplored Therapeutic Frontier"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper; the money is green, and its seal says what it buys (a red missile for war, a teal cross for medicine). "PiP" means Wishonia in her Layer B corner: standing in the bottom right corner in 16:9 (cut off by the frame's bottom edge), in her flying saucer at the top right in 9:16, with her gesture body showing.

**Spine.** The wait sign and the queue under it. One plain enamel sign hangs over the ticket window at the front of the queue to not die. In queue-to-not-die its top line reads "15 PER YEAR" and its bottom line, "WAIT TIME FROM HERE:", spins its digit wheels without stopping. The window turns out to be the warehouse's, and at the end of untested-warehouse the wheels clank to a stop on "443 YEARS". In weirdly-calm the same 443 years lie on the wall screen as a timeline, with the viewer's headstone at year 80. The part is one long wait, and the sign keeps score.

**Running gag: waiting ages you, one step further each time.** The patient knocking at the shut FDA window grows a beard to the floor (95-percent-untreated). The queue goes grey, then turns into skeletons that keep their tickets (queue-to-not-die). The scientist goes grey on the way to the window, turns into a skeleton as she hands the bottle over, and climbs the ladder again for the next one (untested-warehouse). The viewer gets a headstone (weirdly-calm).

**Callback.** The cobweb and the spider from without-with-papers (Part 1) grow on the knocking patient in 95-percent-untreated.

**Callback.** The reader from falling-apart (Part 8) sits on the wall screen in weirdly-calm, in the same armchair under the same lamp, still reading, still calm.

**Callbacks set up here.** The warehouse shelf with its "NEVER TESTED FOR THIS" tags comes back in someone-you-love (Part 12). The roped queue of ticket-holding humans under the wait sign on "443 YEARS" comes back in accordion-timeline (Part 14), where it squeezes like an accordion to 36 years, and in future-b-2032 (Part 25), where the sign spins down to NOW.

**Held frame.** weirdly-calm, from after "this." to the end: only the reader's page turn moves.

**Transitions.** Layer B is the footage on Wishonia's wall screen. Going from the office to Earth zooms into the wall screen; coming back zooms out of it. Earth to Earth slides left. The title card has no wall screen, so it slides left into the first Earth scene, as in Part 1.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
- No real theme-park characters, castles or logos; the theme park in queue-to-not-die is a plain, unbranded one.
- No real FDA seal or logo (the window's sign is plain lettering) and no real car make or badge.
