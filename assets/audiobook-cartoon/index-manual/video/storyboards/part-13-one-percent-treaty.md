# Part 13 storyboard: "The Solution: A 1% Treaty"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd` "A 1% Treaty", and `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd`, which its heading links to.
- **The numbers:**
  - The 1%: of `global_military_spending_annual_2024` $2.72 trillion, which is `treaty_annual_funding` $27.2 billion a year to clinical trials, leaving `global_military_spending_post_treaty_annual_2024` $2.69 trillion. A stack of 100 papers loses one.
  - `nuclear_winter_warhead_threshold` 100 warheads ends civilization; `global_warhead_count` 12,241 warheads; so `nuclear_winter_overkill_factor` 122 apocalypses, of which `nuclear_winter_spare_apocalypses` 121 are spare. Drawn as a tower of 122 crates of 100. Derived: 1% of 12,241 is 122 warheads, about one crate.
  - Treaties already signed (the chapter, not parameters): chemical weapons, 1993, 193 countries; biological weapons, 1975, 187; landmines, 1997, 164. The 1% treaty: 0.
  - 847 civilizations on 847 planets.
  - Antibiotics: 200,000 years of humans next to bread mold. Penicillin was found in mold in 1928 by Alexander Fleming (not in the chapter; a history fact). Derived: on a 200,000-year timeline, everything since the pyramids (about 4,500 years) is the last 2%.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the whole plan is one sentence, every country moves 1% of its military budget to clinical trials at the same time, it still leaves them 121 apocalypses, and humans have already signed three treaties that asked for more.
- **Plates:**
  - host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass. A carved wooden cuckoo-clock bird lives in a hatch in the console and springs out on its zigzag spring whenever the narrator says "crazy".
  - globe: the big engraved globe on its stand, its top the ground, the seal-flip funnel on the axis pin at the North Pole and the Scientist's lab bench beside it. Leaders stand round it, each on a stack of 100 war papers.
  - fence: two yards split by a picket fence, a heap of 100 missiles in each, a tally board on a post in each.
  - crate-tower: a tan field to a low horizon; the tower of 122 crates, a tiny Earth beside it.
  - signing-table: a long table on a plain floor; the treaties unroll off its front edge into a bar chart of signatures.
  - planets: navy space; a line of 847 planets curving away from the camera, Earth the nearest and the 847th. Later the planets sort into a chart.
  - cave: Part 1's cave, its long back wall painted as a timeline, 200,000 years ago to 1928.
- **Cast:** existing cut-outs: `globe`, `scientist`, `politician`, `mushroom`. From Part 11: `kim-jong-un`, `putin`, `weapons-maker`, the warhead and the missile. From Part 1, the 122 of nuclear-winters (the tower of 122 crates), the cave of fire-on-fire, and split-the-atom's pose (two figures aiming atoms at each other, here as alien skeletons).
