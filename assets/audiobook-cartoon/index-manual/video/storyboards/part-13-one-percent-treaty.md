# Part 13 storyboard: "The Solution: A 1% Treaty"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. The part is a 1950s atomic-age instructional film. Layer A is the host's set (`KIT.studio`): Wishonia at her lectern, talking to the viewer. Layer B is a plate: one large illustration on a plain field that the camera travels over (`KIT.camera`), one framing per phrase; Wishonia is the voice and is not in it. Money is green; its seal says what it buys (a missile for war, a cross for medicine). Part 2 (`compositions/scenes/part-02/`) is the reference for every kind of shot.

**Spine.** The 1%. Every scene takes one small piece off something big: one paper off the top of every leader's stack of war papers (through the color-flip funnel, where the missile seal turns into a cross), one missile off each heap at the fence, one crate off the bottom of a tower of 122, and a fourth treaty that asks for 1% fewer. In 847-civilizations, the planets that listened are the ones whose papers flipped.

**The leaders.** Every leader is the politician (`KIT.castOpts("politician", { tone, flagPin: false })`: the toothy grin, the slick hair, the wide tie) with a plain sash across his chest, no emblem. L0 is tone 0 with an orange sash, L1 tone 1 mustard, L2 tone 2 periwinkle (the one who is late), L3 tone 3 tan; four more swap the sash colors. The two at the fence in enemies-will-invade are L1 and L3, with the same options, so they are the same men.

**YOU.** The viewer is the busy human in the bowler from Part 1's title card, as in Parts 10 and 12 (`{ tone: 2, outfit: "suit", hat: "bowler" }`, the same options in every scene). He walks the papers to the leaders in every-leader-at-once (the viewer does the persuading), slams the treaties down in treaties-you-signed, and is the one reader in next-page who turns to page 2.

**Running gag: the treaty is one line.** The 1% treaty is a scroll with one line of text on a great length of empty paper (`KIT.props.scroll({ text: "THE 1% TREATY", lines: ["Redirect 1% of military spending to clinical trials."], signatures: 0, wax: false })`, its paper drawn as long as the shot needs). It unrolls from Wishonia's hand in thats-the-treaty and comes back in treaties-you-signed as the fourth scroll, unsigned, next to three crowded with signatures.

**Running gag: "crazy".** A carved wooden cuckoo-clock bird lives inside the lectern. Each time the narrator says "crazy", it springs out on its zigzag spring and cuckoos: in sounds-crazy (she side-eyes it), on "craziness" in thats-the-treaty (she presses it back down without looking), and in next-page out of page 1 of the manual, where the reader snaps the book shut on it.

**Running gag: Earth is case #847.** The Earth case file on the lectern is Part 12's: the tab "~~EARTH~~ DIRT: CASE #847" (Part 1's earth-means-dirt), Part 1's "MAY BE ELIGIBLE FOR OPTIMIZATION" and Part 12's "SUBOPTIMAL" on its cover. In 847-civilizations Earth is the 847th planet, nearest the camera, neither lit nor burnt: it sweats, a wisp of smoke curls off its edge and stops, and the survey satellite flashes in its face. next-page is decided on its surface.

**Callbacks.** From Part 1: the 122 of nuclear-winters is the tower of 122 crates; the two humans of split-the-atom pointing their atoms at each other are the two grinning alien skeletons on the burnt planet; the cave and the two cavemen of fire-on-fire are bread-mold's. From Part 10 (warheads-in-the-cart): the 12,241 warheads, which the weapons maker heaped into the politician's cart. From Part 12 (good-bad-weird-news): "You'll get rich doing it" becomes "those papers" on YOU's leashes; and the case file, "~~EARTH~~ DIRT: CASE #847", with its two stamps.

**Set up here.** The bond certificate in human-greed (`KIT.props.bond({ text: "INCENTIVE ALIGNMENT BOND", value: "$1,000" })`) is the one Part 17 slaps down on the lectern in heres-one. The cuckoo bird is the bird Part 17's cuckoo clock replaces with the Grim Reaper (enemy-is-the-clock). **Held frame.** 847-civilizations, from after "themselves." to "The" in next-page: the two skeletons keep aiming and nothing moves. A HOLD is silent.

**Transitions.** The title card cuts in (the parts are rendered on their own and joined) and goes down to black; sounds-crazy irises in on her face. Host shots and plates cut to each other. next-page continues 847-civilizations' plate with no seam: the camera travels back to Earth. human-greed irises out on her face after "greed.".
