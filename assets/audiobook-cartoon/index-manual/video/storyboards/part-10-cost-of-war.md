# Part 10 storyboard: "The Cost of War"

- **Source:** `index-manual.qmd`, "The Cost of War", with its citation `chance-of-dying-from-terrorism-1-in-30m`. It links no `knowledge/` pages.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_military_spending_annual_2024` = $2.72 trillion a year.
  - `global_warhead_count` = 12,241 warheads. `nuclear_winter_overkill_factor` = 122x: about 100 warheads end civilization, so 12,241 is 122 crates of 100. `nuclear_winter_spare_apocalypses` = 121 spares.
  - `cumulative_military_spending_fed_era` = $170 trillion since 1913.
  - `war_deaths_since_1900` = 310 million.
  - The paycheck buys 97% less (spoken; a dollar of 1913 buys about 3 cents of goods now).
  - `military_to_government_clinical_trials_spending_ratio` = 604, so government trials get about $4.5 billion a year ($2.72T / 604). $170T / $4.5B = about 38,000 years.
  - `war_counterfactual_income_multiple` = 23.2x. `war_counterfactual_gdp_per_capita` = $333,636 against `global_avg_income_2025` = $14,375.
  - `global_hale_gap` = 10.1 years of life lost to disease.
  - Terrorism: 1 in 30 million (cited). Disease: 100%.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** humans spend $2.72 trillion a year on killing, $170 trillion since 1913, enough for 38,000 years of trials, and chose the killing.
- **Plates** (each on aged paper):
  - `murder-mart`: a Victorian grocer's aisle on a plain field: a bin of warheads, a robot shelf, an empty spot under a price tag, a little hospital on a stand, a crate.
  - `1913-press`: a printing press at the left, a big inked line chart (1913 to 2024, $0 to $170T) running right from it, and a small town under the chart's far end.
  - `checkout`: a shop counter and till; the receipt unrolls from the till across the field into the distance.
  - `two-roads`: a line chart of income per person from 1800 to 2025 that forks at 1900 into two lines, drawn as two roads.
  - `604-bar`: a two-bar chart on a plain field, one bar going up past the clouds.
  - `midway`: two carnival prize wheels, each a pie chart of the odds.
  - `desert`: cracked ground; a sea and a little island country on the right; a puddle.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** `uncle-sam` and `general` (existing; the shopper and the stocker), `shopping-cart`, `warhead`, `crate`, `robot`, `price-tag`, `hospital`, `space-guard` (a beanpole in a fishbowl helmet with binoculars), `porthole` (existing deck art, `assets/art/deck/porthole.webp`, shown in the binocular view), `quake-crate` (a crate with a crank and a "?"), `woodrow-wilson`, `printing-press`, `moth`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `bill-plain`, `skeleton`, `house`, `rubble` (existing), `you` (a busy Victorian in a fedora), `loaf`, `cash-register`, `receipt`, `pyramid`, `caveman`, `fat-cat`, `germ`, `satellite`, `prize-wheel` (two), `sandbags`, `missile` on a `leash`, `oil-derrick`, `george-w-bush` (flight suit), `banner`, `tank`, `island`, `submarine`, `mushroom` (existing).
- **Callbacks:** the leashed missile from Part 8 (fifty-nine-elevens), which walked past a germ with no oil, finds the oil on cancer's head here. The 604 bar is Part 6's bar chart. The missile is the Weapons Maker's from Part 6.
- **New art:** all seven plate sheets, with no words; every cut-out in the Cast not marked existing. Prices, years, axis labels, the receipt's lines, banners and stamps are in code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: inked axes, ticks, labels, a dashed reference line, and a line drawn left to right over a set time (the 1913 chart, the dollar line, the two roads).
  - `stack`: copies of one cut-out piling into a bar (the crates, the 604 bills, the skulls).
  - `unroll`: a strip (the receipt, the price tag's ribbon) that pays out along a path with lines of text on it, the camera following.
  - `count`: flip digits on a readout card (the Space Force's ALIENS: 0).
  - `xray`: a white flash and a swap to a skeleton.
