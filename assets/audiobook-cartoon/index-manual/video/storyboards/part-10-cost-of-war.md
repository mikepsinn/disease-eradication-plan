# Part 10 storyboard: "The Cost of War"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`, so do not edit them by hand; if the audio changes, rebuild the timings and the scenes follow. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, every move starts on the quoted word.

Scenes are named, not numbered. Use the name in feedback ("loved-ones: make the form smaller"). The number in each heading is only the order.

Look, characters and rules: `../../cartoon-style-bible.md`. Layer A is Wishonia's sunburst office; Layer B is the engraved Earth paper with red (war money) and teal (medicine money). "PiP" means Wishonia talks from the round corner frame.

**Spine.** Shopping. The part is one shopping trip: the cart fills with this year's purchases (murder-shopping-cart, warheads-in-the-cart, cart-gadgets), the printing press pays for 110 years of carts, the receipt in bought-the-other-thing says what humans chose, and the 604 bar shows the same choice today. Every weapon is a silly object with a price tag.

**Callback.** The 122 red snowflakes from nuclear-winters (Part 1) fill the warheads' tag.

**Callback.** The 604 bar from 604-papers (Part 1) comes back in 604-times-less.

**Callback.** The shopping cart comes back in official-murder-budget (Part 11), and the receipt comes back in murder-receipt (Part 12).

**Held frame.** nothing-papers, from "million" to "valuable". odds-of-dying, from "percent" to the end of the scene.

**Bans for this part.**
- No real people or logos.
- No slideshow (a new text card every scene) and no screensaver (motion that says nothing). Every Layer B scene has an actor that moves on the words.
- Captions keep-out: nothing important in the bottom 17% of the frame.
- No real banknotes, weapons models or company marks; weapons are silly engraved objects with price tags.
