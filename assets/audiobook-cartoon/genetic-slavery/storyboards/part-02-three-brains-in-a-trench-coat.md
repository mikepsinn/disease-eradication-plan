# Part 2 storyboard: "Three Brains in a Trench Coat"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/genetic-slavery.qmd`, "How Hydrogen Learned to Worry", from "But the vehicle wasn't built all at once" to the end of the section ("very, very out of hand.").
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The chapter's round figures (uncited): the reptilian brain is about 500 million years old; reflexes alone for 300 million years before fur; the limbic system about 200 million years ago; the neocortex expanded 2 to 3 million years ago; 13.8 billion years of repetition.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the brain is three layers from three eras, the oldest ones grab control first, and nobody designed it.
- **Plates:**
  - `head-house`: YOU, huge, in profile, his head cut away like a Gray's Anatomy plate; three brain layers in three tints, bottom to top, with a tangle of old brass pipes running through all of them. The camera climbs it floor by floor and pulls back to his body when it reacts.
  - `cliff-road`: a plain street that becomes a cliff road; at the foot of the cliff, the wreck and a pie chart on an easel.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `you`, `you-desk`, `citizen`, `knife-switch`, `hanky`, `balloon`, `desk-calendar`, `motorcar`, `atom`, `smallpox-cup`, `laptop`, `speech-bubbles`, `missile`, `plan-clipboard`, `chalkboard`. From Part 1: `gene`, `hydrogen`. New: `lizard`, `shrew`, `trench-coat`.
- **Callbacks:**
  - Part 1 (hydrogen-title, cosmic-timeline): the nervous hydrogen atom is the only suspect in the line-up.
  - Part 1 (the-copier): the gene on her console ledge copies itself onto the plan.
  - Episode 1, Part 1 (split-the-atom): the atom the trench coat splits is the atom emblem.
  - Set up for later parts: the blank PLAN: clipboard comes back filled in Part 4 (meat-puppet). The lizard, the shrew and tiny YOU (YOU scaled to a third) are the three tenants whenever the brain is opened again.
- **New art:**
  - `head-house`: the cutaway head as a plate, three empty layers and the pipes.
  - `lizard`: a scaly lizard with a blank stare, working a bank of big brass levers; cranking a heart crank; throwing a knife switch; grabbing a steering wheel; swallowing a hatchling from its eggs; charred.
  - `shrew`: a small furry Victorian-plate mammal, a mother with a litter, at a small fancy control panel with dials; pulling a chain; charred.
  - `trench-coat`: an empty tall trench coat and a fedora, open and closed, sized to fit the lizard, the shrew and tiny YOU stacked.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `flood`: a tinted water level that rises through a plate's layer and floats cut-outs on its surface.
  - `xray` (as in episode 1): to see the three tenants inside the coat.
