# Part 7 storyboard: "The Compassion Gradient"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/genetic-slavery.qmd`, "The Compassion Gradient (Or: Why You'll Buy Your Cat a Birthday Cake While Children Starve)", from the heading to "This is the software."
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The facts the pictures use:
  - Hamilton's Rule, rB > C (cited `hamilton-kin-selection`): r is relatedness, B the benefit to the one helped, C the cost to the helper.
  - Relatedness: a child shares 50% of your genes (the narrator's figure); a full sibling 50% on average; a first cousin 12.5%. Only the narrator's 50% goes on screen.
  - "Four billion years" and "two hundred thousand years": the chapter's round figures, uncited.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** your genes ration kindness by how much DNA you share, from all of it for yourself to none for strangers, and in-group bias is that rule doing its job.
