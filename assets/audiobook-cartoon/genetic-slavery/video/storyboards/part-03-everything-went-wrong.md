# Part 3 storyboard: "Everything Went Wrong"

Times come from the cue words. A move starts on the quoted word. `after "word"` is a beat after it ends.

## Decided

1. Three UNREMARKABLE stamps, each crossed out, and a nameplate that says DIRT.
2. Carbon bonds with a boot, a teapot, and a rubber duck, and with H, O, and N.
3. The molecule is on a photocopier. The banner EVERYTHING WENT WRONG is printed on the machine.
4. The page stays small. The footnote bursts it: 1. INCLUDING YOU.

**Bans for this part.**

- No periodic-table poster.
- The banner is ink on the copier, not a caption floating over the scene.
