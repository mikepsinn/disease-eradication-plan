# Part 3 storyboard: "Everything Went Wrong"

Times come from the cue words. A move starts on the quoted word. `after "word"` is a beat after it ends.

## Decided

1. Three "unremarkable" stamps, then the rock is labeled DIRT in the proudest letters on the plate.
2. Carbon bonds with whoever is standing nearby. The trick is a second copy popping out of the first.
3. The banner EVERYTHING WENT WRONG drops on that copy and never comes down.
4. Economy, politics, wars, and trials are four lines. You are the footnote, and the footnote is then enlarged until it is the frame.

**Bans for this part.**

- No periodic-table poster with every element filled in.
- No narrator caption repeating "this was the moment everything went wrong." The banner is that sentence.
