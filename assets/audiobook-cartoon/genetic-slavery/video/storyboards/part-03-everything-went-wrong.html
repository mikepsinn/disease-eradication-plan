Decided

  1. Three "unremarkable" stamps, then the rock is labeled DIRT in the proudest letters on the plate.
  2. Carbon bonds with whoever is standing nearby. The trick is a second copy popping out of the first.
  3. The banner EVERYTHING WENT WRONG drops on that copy and never comes down.
  4. Economy, politics, wars, and trials are four lines. You are the footnote, and the footnote is then enlarged until it is the frame.

The trick1 scene

03 · copy-themselvesB · 3:30.0–3:54.5

First: "Copy" → the group pulls a small lever. Drawn: two identical molecules, and a banner already down reading EVERYTHING WENT WRONG.

cut
Narration and moves

"Copy themselves. This was the moment everything went wrong. The molecules that were better at copying made more copies. The ones that were worse at copying made fewer. This is the entire plot of the next four billion years. It is also the entire plot of your economy, your politics, your wars, and your inability to fund clinical trials."

Moves: "Copy" → the group pulls a small lever. "themselves." → an identical group pops out beside it. "wrong." → a banner drops over both: EVERYTHING WENT WRONG. "more" → the pair becomes four. "fewer." → a faded pair at the edge shrinks. The banner stays.

Sound: a pop on "themselves." A slam on "wrong."

On screen: "EVERYTHING WENT WRONG". No: no family tree. Why: copying is the whole plot, and the banner says how the plot turned out.
