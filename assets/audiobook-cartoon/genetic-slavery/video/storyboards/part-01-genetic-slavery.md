# Part 1 storyboard: "Genetic Slavery"

Times are MP3 times. They come from each scene's cue word in `../../word-timings.json`. Do not edit the time or voiceover lines by hand. Each scene runs until the next one starts. Inside a scene, a move starts on the quoted word. `after "word"` is a beat after that word ends.

Look: `../../../index-manual/cartoon-style-bible.md`. This episode uses the atomic-age film (navy, cream, mustard, green money). Layer A is the title card. Layer B is a plate. Wishonia stays off the plates.

## Decided

1. The title card says the two words the narrator says, and nothing else.
2. The 604 is a pile of war-seal bills against one medicine bill. The plaque "POLITICAL PROBLEM" is crossed out a beat after "not."
3. The hardware is one stone computer. The rocks, the stranger, and the cloud are the same object getting worse.
4. The manual's cover turns to YOU on "kill."
5. After the bang, one spark sits still while the year counter runs. The joke is that nothing happens.

**Bans for this part.**

- No slideshow of text cards. The heading in universe-exploded leaves before the bang.
- No red money. The seals say war or medicine.
- No Wishonia on a plate.
- No caption burned into the 16:9 frame.
