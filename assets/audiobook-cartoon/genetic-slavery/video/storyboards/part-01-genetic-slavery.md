# Part 1: Genetic Slavery

Chapter audio from 100.24s to 143.34s of `genetic-slavery.680f0446.mp3` (after the theme song). Five scenes. The card is the only scene without captions.

## genetic-slavery-title

The period title card, up from black. GENETIC on "genetic", SLAVERY on "slavery". The atom emblem runs. A projector whirr on the first word. Down to black before "Your".

## six-oh-four

A navy plate, cream floor. On "604" a stack of war-seal bills climbs and the number 604 lands on it. On "curing" one medicine-seal bill and a 1. On "political" a plaque, POLITICAL PROBLEM. On "not" an X through the plaque. The plaque stays crossed out.

## hardware

A stone computer in the middle. On "hardware" it is there. On "software" the screen says SOFTWARE. On "200,000-year-old" a stamp: 200,000 YEARS. On "rocks" three rocks sit in front of it. On "strangers" a person steps in from the side and the machine leans away. On "nuclear" the rocks are gone and a mushroom cloud grows out of the machine.

## owners-manual

A brain, plain, on the same floor. On "manual" a book drops onto it: OWNER'S MANUAL. On "kill" the cover turns and says YOU.

## universe-exploded

The heading comes in on the words: HOW HYDROGEN LEARNED TO WORRY, then the small line A 13.8 BILLION YEAR RECAP. The heading leaves before "exploded". On "exploded" a white flash, and one spark is left in the dark. On "interesting" the spark does not move and a year counter starts. On "400" the counter hits 400,000,000 YEARS and holds. Nothing else happens. That is the joke.
