# Part 1 storyboard: "Collect Names First"

- **Source:** `knowledge/appendix/recruitment-and-propaganda-plan.qmd`: the opening, "Phase 0: Collecting Humans Before You Have Anything to Offer Them" and "The Bureaucratic Nesting Doll Problem" (the audio was read from an earlier draft: `recruitment-and-propaganda-plan.prepared.txt`, from the chapter title to the end of "The Bureaucratic Nesting Doll Problem"). The theme song plays before it in the MP3 (0:00 to 1:40) and is the existing music video, as made.
- **The numbers:**
  - 100,000 humans to recruit: the chapter's own figure (hardcoded in the chapter's description and shopping list, not a `_variables.yml` parameter).
  - The three lists of names, for the timeline (dates, not parameters): abolition, the 13th Amendment, 1865; suffrage, the 19th Amendment, 1920; civil rights, the Civil Rights Act, 1964. The gaps are 55 and 44 years; the screen shows the narrator's "every forty years" as the span marks, "~40 YEARS".
- **What the viewer must leave with:** before you can take a dollar you need permits that each need the other first, so you start with a list of names, which is how every big change started.
- **Plates:**
  - `phase-zero-field`: a plain tan field. At the left the pledge board (the `sign-board`); at the right the SEC building, and beside it an empty lot with a signpost.
  - `nesting-dolls`: a plain floor and a plain wall, with one door in the wall that the camera finds at the end.
  - `list-of-names`: a timeline running along the floor, inked marks at 1865, 1920, 1964 and a blank "20??".
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing names: `you`, `moth`, `reaper`, `bill-med`, `bond`, `share-certificate`, `paper-plane`, `hanky`, `patient` (and `patient-skeleton`), `tumbleweed`, `signpost`, `plan-clipboard`, `filing-cabinet`, `coin`, `citizen`, `bank`, `politician`, `balance-scale`, `calendar`, `treaty-scroll` (as the three laws), `scroll` (the three petitions), `megaphone`, `sign-board` (the pledge board), `door`. New: `sec-building`, `nesting-doll`, `key`, `frederick-douglass`, `susan-b-anthony`, `martin-luther-king`, `party-hat`.
- **Callbacks:** episode 1 Part 2's moth, which lived in the hungry man's empty wallet, flies out of YOU's empty pocket. The pledge board comes back in Part 3 as the shopping list's backdrop; the nesting dolls come back in Part 3, where YOU points the list at each one and it pops open; the lists of names come back in Part 4 (the Comforting Asymmetry).
- **New art:**
  - the SEC building: a 1930s granite federal building with a face (its columns are teeth, two windows are eyes), a service window with a long jointed arm that holds a rubber stamp; the stamp's words (NOT YET) are code
  - a set of five Russian nesting dolls, each painted as a frowning clerk in a different hat, each half separate so they can be twisted open; label strips are code
  - an iron key on a wall hook (the key can wave); the door is the existing `door`, its sign in code
  - Frederick Douglass, Susan B. Anthony and Martin Luther King Jr. as engraved portraits, each holding a petition, drawn straight (the joke is on everyone else)
  - a paper party hat and a tiny flag, for the Politician
- **Kit additions:**
  - `dotted`: a cut-out drawn as a dotted outline only, then filled in (the crowd YOU does not have yet).
  - `unroll`: a scroll that unrolls down to the floor and along it.
  - `nest`: a doll that twists open and lifts a smaller one out of itself, repeated.
