# Part 4 storyboard: "Twenty Minutes"

- **Source:** `knowledge/appendix/recruitment-and-propaganda-plan.qmd`, "Getting Started (It Takes Twenty Minutes)" and "The Comforting Asymmetry". Narration script: `recruitment-and-propaganda-plan.prepared.txt`, "Getting Started (It Takes Twenty Minutes)." to "...it starts with a list of names."
- **The numbers:**
  - 20 minutes to build the three boxes: the chapter's own figure, hardcoded.
  - The Department of Defense's name (history, not parameters): the National Security Act of 1947 renamed the Department of War the National Military Establishment, and the 1949 amendments renamed that the Department of Defense; about two years of signs.
  - 168: the hours in a week (the Heart Human's answer; derived, 24 x 7).
  - Comic Sans: designed in 1994 (fact, for the "didn't exist yet" card).
  - "3,247 registered investors representing $180 million in stated investment demand" and "2.3 centimeters": the chapter's example figures, hardcoded.
  - The 65-year head start: hardcoded in the chapter. Eisenhower named the military-industrial complex in his farewell address on 17 January 1961; 1961 + 65 = 2026, so the track runs 1961 to 2026.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the start costs twenty minutes, and the war industry's money has lost before to a correct argument with enough names.
- **Plates:**
  - `twenty-minutes`: YOU's desk with a laptop and a kitchen timer at the left; a plain cobbled street in the middle (Pankhurst, the two doctors); the Pentagon with a sign painter on its ladder at the right. Steps 1, 2 and 4 all happen here.
  - `dentist-office`: one dentist's chair and its lamp on a plain floor.
  - `outspent-chart`: a bar chart on aged paper, "MONEY", four pairs of bars: the war factory's 65-year head start against YOU, then the three movements.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass; a brass balance scale on the ledge.
- **Cast:** existing names: `you`, `laptop`, `egg-timer`, `fat-cat`, `heart-human` (Part 2), `nonprofit-building` (Part 2), `sec-building` (Part 1, its stamp arm), `pentagon`, `ladder`, `missile-fly`, `calendar`, `envelope`, `doctor`, `patient` (and `patient-skeleton`), `calculator`, `money-hill`, `factory-proud`, `lobbyist`, `megaphone`, `eisenhower`, `frederick-douglass`, `susan-b-anthony`, `martin-luther-king` (Part 1), `scroll` (Part 1's petitions), `balance-scale`, `headstone`, `plan-clipboard`, `painter` (the sign painter), `letter` (the pamphlet; its type is code), `xray`, `bundle-war` (the RICH pan). New: `emmeline-pankhurst`, `dentist`, `dentist-chair`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 1's three campaigners and their petition scrolls, which knock the big bars over; Part 2's three types, who fill in the three boxes (the nonprofit's sash). Episode 1 Part 7's Pentagon sign peel (DEPARTMENT OF WAR) is the same building, but the gag here is the two years of repainting, not the missiles.
- **New art:**
  - Emmeline Pankhurst, engraved, in her Edwardian hat and sash, holding out a pamphlet (its type is code: Comic Sans, a Victorian serif, and the WordArt version)
