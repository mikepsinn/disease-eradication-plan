# Part 5 storyboard: "The Questions You Can't Answer Wrong"

- **Source:** `knowledge/appendix/recruitment-and-propaganda-plan.qmd`, "The Question You Can't Answer Wrong", questions one to four; the spending figures from `knowledge/appendix/parameters-and-calculations.qmd` and the growth figure from `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd`. Narration script: `recruitment-and-propaganda-plan.prepared.txt`, "The Question You Can't Answer Wrong." to "...You would be bad at math."
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_military_spending_annual_2024`: $2.72 trillion a year on weapons.
  - `global_government_clinical_trials_spending_annual`: $4.5 billion a year on clinical trials.
  - `military_to_government_clinical_trials_spending_ratio`: 604 (the first over the second). Drawn to scale, if the weapons bar is 900 px tall, the trials bar is 1.5 px.
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1% of military spending, compounding for 20 years.
  - "Sixteen point five times richer": the narrator's figure, said twice (questions two and four), and the screen uses it ("16.5×"). It was the value of `treaty_trajectory_gdp_vs_current_trajectory_multiplier_year_20` when the audio was made; that parameter now reads 1.71x. For the chart: the treaty line ends 16.5 times as high as the current line at year 20.
  - Dr Pangloss: the tutor in Voltaire's *Candide* (1759), who holds that this is the best of all possible worlds through the Lisbon earthquake and everything else (fact, not a parameter).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** every question in the sequence has one sensible answer, and the answers add up to "this should be your top priority, because it makes you richer."
- **Plates:**
  - `five-turnstiles`: a long plain corridor of five brass turnstiles in a row, numbered 1 to 5 on little signs; Socrates and one column at its entrance; past turnstile 3 the treaty scroll on its stand and the suggestion box on a post; past turnstile 4 the Skeptic's to-do list on a clipboard and a little rowboat.
  - `sword-kitchen`: a 1950s kitchen with a turnstile at its door and a dresser of drawers; its back wall is the chart "SPENDING", racked floor to ceiling with swords against one small first-aid tin on a shelf.
  - `trajectory-chart`: a line chart on aged paper, "HOW RICH EVERYONE IS", 0 to 20 years, that becomes the city of tomorrow.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing names: `you`, `globe`, `factory-proud`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `bill-plain`, `scissors`, `family` (and `family-groceries`), `city-of-tomorrow`, `market-of-tomorrow`, `flying-car`, `moral-human` and `halo`, `history-book` (relabelled GDP TRAJECTORIES), `treaty-scroll`, `cobweb`, `moth`, `envelope`, `headstone`, `plan-clipboard`, `rowboat`, `dunce-cap`, `calculator`, `gold-star`, `pessimist` (the Skeptic, raincloud and all), `turnstile` (five copies), `teacup` (the hemlock), `sword` (copies racked up the kitchen wall), `ballot-box` (the suggestion box, BETTER PLANS in code).
