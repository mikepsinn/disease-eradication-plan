# Part 1 storyboard: "Treaty Feasibility and Cost Analysis"

- **Source:** `knowledge/appendix/treaty-feasibility.qmd` as narrated: the opening and "What Treaties Actually Cost". Its treaty facts are cited in the chapter (`icbl-ottawa-treaty`, `cluster-munitions-convention-2008`, `arms-trade-treaty-2013`, `control-arms-coalition`), not parameters.
- **The numbers:**
  - `chain_world_leader_count`: 195 countries (the objection's "one hundred and ninety-five countries").
  - Ottawa Treaty: 14 months; 1 staff member (Jody Williams); a coalition of over 1,000 organizations in 60 countries; 122 states signed; a Nobel Peace Prize (cited, not parameters).
  - Cluster Munitions Convention: about 2 years; 7 governments; 94 states. Arms Trade Treaty: a decade; 3 governments to start; 50+ Control Arms reports; 130 countries. Nuclear Ban Treaty: a decade; ICAN, 5 staff in Geneva; 122 states voted yes; a Nobel Prize (cited, not parameters).
