# Part 4 storyboard: "Pillar Three: The Forever Part" and "How the Money Gets Spent: 80, 10, 10"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/treaty-financial-plan.qmd`, "Pillar 3: The Forever Part" and "How the Money Gets Spent: 80/10/10"; the compliance mechanism in `knowledge/appendix/incentive-alignment-bonds-paper.qmd` and `knowledge/solution/incentive-alignment-bonds.qmd`; Wishocracy in `knowledge/solution/wishocracy.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year, "or more".
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1% of military budgets, from 100 or more nations (hard-coded "100+" in the chapter).
  - `dih_treasury_medical_research_pct`: 80%; `dih_treasury_to_medical_research_annual`: $21.8 billion a year.
  - `victory_bond_funding_pct`: 10%; `victory_bond_annual_payout`: $2.72 billion a year.
  - `iab_political_incentive_funding_pct`: 10%; `iab_political_incentive_funding_annual`: $2.72 billion a year.
  - The fixed 20% (10% + 10%).
  - The train: ten cars, 8 to trials, 1 to bondholders, 1 to political incentives, so each car is $2.72 billion a year.
  - "Six months about font choices": the narrator's joke, no parameter.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** once the treaty passes, the money comes every year from military budgets, and fixed rules split it 80% to trials, 10% to investors and 10% to rewarding the politicians who support it, so nobody can redirect it and politicians comply out of self-interest.
- **Plates:**
  - `pillar-three`: Part 1's column of bundles on its plinth, and the sky above it, up into space.
  - `the-volcano`: one long plate the camera travels along. The Capitol, the fat military pipe from it to the weapons factory, Part 1's 1% tap, and a pipe underground to the money volcano, a clock on a post on its slope; pulled back, a ring of a hundred little factories feeding it. From the volcano's foot a railway runs to one junction and VICTORY CORP.'s station house, where it splits three ways: wide to the lab and its trial door (a hospital beside it), narrow to the fat cat, and narrow to three fenced sheds.
  - `the-career`: one politician's country: his tap, a scoreboard above him, a poster wall, a padded door, and a bare room with a typewriter.
- **Cast:** existing: `bundle-med`, `plinth`, `cloud`, `goose`, `airship`, `earth-big`, `capitol`, `factory-pay`, `factory-proud`, `tap` (Part 1), `volcano`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `post-clock`, `calendar`, `halo`, `politician`, `scoreboard`, `super-pac`, `campaign-poster`, `challenger`, `padded-door`, `typewriter`, `book`, `moth`, `tumbleweed`, `greed-hand`, `train`, `rail-switch`, `binder`, `committee`, `sign-board`, `employee-portrait`, `economist` (Part 1, with a lunch napkin), `lab`, `germ`, `fat-cat`, `picket-fence`, `ballot-box`, `bank`, `auditor`, `builder`, `wrench`, `paste-brush`, `patient`, `trial-door`, `citizen`, `allocation-bar`, `hospital`. - **New art:**
