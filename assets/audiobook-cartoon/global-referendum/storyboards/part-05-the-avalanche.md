# Part 5 storyboard: "Implementation Roadmap"

- **Source:** `knowledge/strategy/global-referendum.qmd`, "Implementation Roadmap" and "Success Metrics". (The page has since loosened the timeline to "weeks to years"; the audio's 18 to 36 months is what the chart shows.)
- **The numbers:**
  - `three_point_five_percent_of_global_population`: 280 million; `global_population_activism_threshold_pct`: 3.5%.
  - Time to 280 million: "eighteen to thirty-six months" (narrated). The phases: Foundation, months 1 to 3; Scaling, 4 to 12; Full Campaign, 13 to 36; Treaty Pressure, 24 on.
  - Cost per verified participant: under one dollar (narrated; about $0.89 from `treaty_campaign_viral_referendum_base_case` $250 million ÷ 280 million).
  - Viral coefficient: above 1.0, "each person brings more than one person". Derived for the branching picture: if each brings two, 28 rounds from one person reach 268 million and the 29th passes 280 million (as in episode 1 Part 18).
  - Fraud detection above 99%, false positives below 1% (Part 3's figures, the page's text).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** it grows like an avalanche because each person brings more than one, and four numbers tell you whether the machine works; when they are right, small change becomes pressure politicians feel.
- **Plates:**
  - roadmap-chart: a line chart on aged paper, "VERIFIED HUMANS" up the side and "MONTHS" 0 to 36+ along the bottom, a dashed line at 280,000,000, a shaded band from month 18 to 36. The chart's floor is a snowy slope.
  - three-trees: a mountainside, a phone screen and a village square side by side, each with the same branching tree.
  - pressure-machine: a Victorian contraption of gears with a hopper and four gauges on its front, a fat pipe running from it to the Capitol.
  - Not counted: the title cards ("IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP", "SUCCESS METRICS") and the host scenes (Wishonia at her console, Earth in the window).
- **Cast:** existing: `politician`, `machine`, `laptop`, `reaper`, `scythe-bent`, `globe`, `hammer-man` (the builder), `light-switch-off` / `light-switch-on`, `airship`, `you`, `bond`, `bill-plain`, `treaty-scroll`, `desk`, `crowd`, `capitol`, `coin`, `manual`, `calculator` (host prop). - **Callbacks:** episode 1 Part 18's referral pyramid, which blew the Reaper off its top; here the avalanche rolls over him. Part 4's bond pays the bonuses and Part 4's gum sits on the cost gauge. Part 1's glass box and Part 2's bouncer are what the builder builds.
