# Part 7 storyboard: "The Guard Dog"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/unrepresentative-democracy.qmd`, "The Misdiagnosis" and "Regulatory Capture: How the Burglar Trained the Guard Dog" up to the war machine.
- **The numbers:**
  - Wishonia chose the public-choice option 4,297 years ago and agreed in 12 minutes; Earth has argued "for several centuries" (the narrator's).
  - 13,700 registered lobbyists in Washington (cited `lobbyist-statistics-dc`); half used to work in government (the narrator's).
  - `us_total_lobbying_annual`: $4.4 billion a year. `us_congress_member_count`: 535 members. Derived: $4.4 billion ÷ 535 = about $8.2 million per member, "over $8 million". A congressman earns less than a mid-level dentist (cited `congressman-salary`).
  - Citizens United (cited `citizens-united-ruling`, 2010).
  - Insulin: $6 to make, $300 to you (cited `insulin-history-and-pricing`). Derived: ($300 − $6) ÷ $6 = 4,900%. Drawn to scale, if the $6 bar is 10 px, the $300 bar is 500 px.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the people who regulate have been fed by the regulated, there is $8 million of persuasion for every congressman, and the result is a $6 vial of insulin sold for $300.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-clinic`: a doctor's exam table on a plain field with the Capitol lying on it; beside it a treadmill wired to a pump and a little hospital, and a long timeline along the floor.
  - `the-lobby`: the Capitol's door with a doghouse beside it, and inside, its marble lobby, packed, with a revolving door at the side.
  - `corporate-row`: the Supreme Court's window, the office tower, the pharma factory and a hospital labelled MEDICARE in a row on a plain field; a pharmacy counter at the end, and an alley behind it.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
