# Part 5 storyboard: "Your Congressman"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/unrepresentative-democracy.qmd`, "Your Congressman: A Professional Fundraiser Who Occasionally Dabbles in Legislation".
- **The numbers:** (all cited, not parameters)
  - A 10-hour day: 4 to 5 hours calling rich people, 4 hours of hearings, votes and meetings, about 1 hour to read; the bills need 6 hours (cited `congress-time-spent-fundraising`).
  - The 110th Congress: 7,441 bills, 125,000 pages, 294 days in session (cited `congress-bill-volume-reading-time`). Derived: 125,000 ÷ 294 = 425 pages a working day; 6 hours ÷ 1 hour = six times normal reading speed.
  - 2013: of a bill's 85 lines, 70 were written by Citigroup lobbyists; two words made plural (cited `lobbyists-write-bills-citigroup`). Derived: 82% of the lines.
  - Over eight years, at least 10,000 copied bills in state legislatures, more than 2,100 became law (cited `copycat-model-legislation-10000`). Derived: about 1 in 5.
  - The PATRIOT Act: 342 pages, voted on 45 minutes after release, 7.6 pages a minute (cited `patriot-act-passage`). The Affordable Care Act: 2,700 pages (cited `affordable-care-act-passage`); the Speaker was Nancy Pelosi.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** congressmen spend their day raising money, have an hour to read six hours of law, and pass bills written by the companies the bills regulate.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-workday`: a bar chart of a 10-hour day beside a congressman's desk.
  - `who-writes-it`: the bank and the Capitol on a plain field with one long page of 85 lines between them; a little house beside the road; further on, fifty little state capitols in rows.
  - `the-floor`: the House floor reduced to a lectern, a stopwatch and a pile of pages; beside it a little forklift, and the launch button.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `politician`, `telephone`, `fat-cat`, `desk`, `calendar`, `ledger` (the bill page, piled by the kit), `bank`, `capitol`, `lobbyist`, `typewriter`, `pencil`, `binder`, `house`, `office-building`, `paste-brush`, `stopwatch`, `podium` (the lectern), `gift-bow`, `jack-spring`, `rolodex`, `sign-board`, `yes-button` (the launch button), `manual`, `mushroom`. - **Callbacks:** Part 4's Politician with the gold phone. The `manual` from episode 1 (the orange book) is the one nobody reads at the button.
- **New art:** a long wooden nose piece for the Politician; the Politician's head as a rubber stamp on a wooden handle; Nancy Pelosi, caricatured, in a 2010 suit with a gavel; a little 1950s forklift.
