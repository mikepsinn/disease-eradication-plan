- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/unrepresentative-democracy.qmd`, "The Mathematics of Why Nobody Fixes Anything" and "Concentrated Benefits vs. Diffuse Costs" (narration script from that heading to "a casino represents financial planning.").
- **The numbers:**
  - Sugar subsidies: $3.5 billion a year (cited `sugar-subsidies-cost`, not a parameter); `sugar_subsidy_cost_per_person_annual` $10 per person.
  - 330 million people (the narrator's; `us_population_2024` is 335 million). Derived: $10 × 330 million = $3.3 billion, the same order as the $3.5 billion.
  - Pharma: $500 billion in profits (cited `pharma-profits-500b`), $1,500 per patient (the narrator's).
  - Banks, 2008: a $700 billion bailout (cited `tarp-bailout-700b`), $2,100 per taxpayer (the narrator's).
  - "Millions" spent by sugar companies to keep it (the narrator's, no figure).
