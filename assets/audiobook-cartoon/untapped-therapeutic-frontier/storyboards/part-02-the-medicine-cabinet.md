# Part 2 storyboard: "Your Medicine Cabinet"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/untapped-therapeutic-frontier.qmd`, "Your Medicine Cabinet (After 5,000 Years of Civilization)", "Ways Your Body Breaks" and "The Multiplication Problem".
- **The numbers:**
  - `fda_approved_products_count` = 20,000 products.
  - "three hundred ways to sell the same painkiller" (spoken, not a variable).
  - `fda_approved_unique_active_ingredients` = 1,650 compounds.
  - `phase_1_passed_compounds_global` = 7,500 compounds.
  - `fda_gras_substances_count` = 635 substances.
  - `safe_compounds_count` = 9,500 compounds. (The three piles add to 9,785; the book's 9,500 is the midpoint of its 7,000 to 12,000 range after overlap, so the pile is stamped 9,500, the narrator's number.)
  - `icd_10_total_codes` = 14,000 codes.
  - `trial_relevant_diseases_count` = 1,000 diseases.
  - `drug_disease_combinations_possible` = 9.5 million combinations (9,500 × 1,000).
  - "five thousand years of civilization", "cough syrup from 2019" (spoken).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** humans already hold 9,500 safe compounds and 1,000 diseases worth testing, so 9.5 million tests could start tomorrow.
- **Plates:**
  - `cabinet`: a 1950s bathroom medicine cabinet with a mirror door, filling the frame, on a tiled wall.
  - `the-grid`: a huge chart on aged paper, "DRUG-DISEASE COMBINATIONS": 9,500 bottles in a row along the top as the columns, 1,000 germs down the left side as the rows, the squares between them filling in. It is Part 1's `lamp-field` with its edges labelled, and it comes back in Parts 3 and 7.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** existing: `you`, `pill-bottle`, `rosette`, `baker`, `scroll` (the baker's list and the to-do list), `filing-cabinet`, `cobweb`, `loaf`, `cheese`, `milk-bottle`, `grape`, `donut`, `germ`, `scientist`, `claim-form` (the permission forms), `fda-building`, `chalkboard`, `girl` (episode 1, Part 12), `medicine-bottle`, `moth`. New: `medicine-cabinet`, `pill-box`.
- **Callbacks:** episode 1, Part 12 (child-scientist): the girl in the ankle-length lab coat is the child who does the sum. Part 1's lamp-field is the-grid's dark field.
- **New art:**
  - `medicine-cabinet`: a mirrored 1950s bathroom cabinet with three glass shelves; the door swings on its own hinge.
  - `pill-box`: one pill packet drawn once and recoloured in many colours; a single white pill that hops between them.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: the grid's two labelled edges (bottles along the top, germs down the side) and squares that fill in row by row, then all at once.
  - `pile`: copies of a cut-out tumbling into a heap (the boxes, the 9,500 stack, Wishonia's avalanche, the forms).
