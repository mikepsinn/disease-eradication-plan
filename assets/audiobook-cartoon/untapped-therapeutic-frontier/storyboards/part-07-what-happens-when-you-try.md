# Part 7 storyboard: "What Happens When You Actually Try"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/untapped-therapeutic-frontier.qmd`, "What Happens When You Actually Try" and "The Bottom Line", with its citation `recovery-trial-100-days-to-cure`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `recovery_trial_cost_per_patient` = $500 per patient; results in under 100 days (cited, not a variable).
  - `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` = $41,000 per patient. Derived: 82 times as much, so if the $500 bar is 10 px tall the $41,000 bar is 820 px.
  - `dfda_trial_capacity_multiplier` = 12.3x.
  - `dfda_trials_per_year_capacity` = 40,682 trials/year (the narrator says "forty thousand seven hundred").
  - `dfda_known_safe_exploration_years` = 234 years (9.5 million / 40,700).
  - `current_known_safe_exploration_years` = 2,879 years (the narrator's 2,880, from Part 6), for the squeeze.
  - `exploration_ratio` = 0.342%.
  - Chemical universe: one-hundred-quintillionth to one-hundred-tredecillionth (10^-20 to 10^-50) of what is possible (the chapter's estimate).
  - "the remaining ninety-nine point nine nine nine percent" (spoken).
  - "one hundred million dollars to introduce them", "seventeen years to make the introduction" (spoken, not variables).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** cheap, fast trials already work, and with 12 times as many of them the search for treatments among the drugs humans already have takes centuries instead of millennia; the hold-up is money, which is being spent on missiles.
- **Plates:**
  - `cost-bars`: a two-bar chart on aged paper, "COST PER PATIENT". The short bar is a little 1950s ward of iron beds with a stopwatch on its wall; the tall one is a tower of blocks.
  - `the-ruler` (from Part 6), with the city of tomorrow standing at the squeezed end mark and a trial machine at its heart; the committee's table at one edge.
  - `the-grid` (Parts 2 and 3), with the tested speck ringed in red in its corner.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.

  - Part 5's turnstile and its $100,000,000 window are the thing between the bottles and the patients.
  - Part 3's grid and its red-ringed speck, for the bottom line.
- **New art:**

  - `ransom-note` (shared with other chapters): a cream sheet for a ransom note; its cut-out magazine letters are code, in mismatched type.
