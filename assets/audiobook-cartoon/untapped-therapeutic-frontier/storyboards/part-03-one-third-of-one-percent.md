# Part 3 storyboard: "What You've Actually Tried"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/untapped-therapeutic-frontier.qmd`, "What You've Actually Tried" and "The Number That Should Keep You Awake".
- **The numbers:**
  - `approved_drug_disease_pairings` = 1,750 pairings.
  - `drug_repurposing_success_rate` = 30%.
  - Failed trials: "ten times as many" (the chapter's assumption, spoken, not a variable).
  - `tested_relationships_estimate` = 32,500 relationships.
  - `drug_disease_combinations_possible` = 9.5 million combinations.
  - `exploration_ratio` = 0.342%. Derived: 32,500 / 9,500,000 = 0.342%; the untried share is 99.66% (the narrator's number; `unexplored_ratio` displays it rounded as 99.7%).

- **Plates:**
  - `the-grid` (from Part 2): bottles along the top, germs down the side, 9.5 million squares between. The whole part happens on it.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
