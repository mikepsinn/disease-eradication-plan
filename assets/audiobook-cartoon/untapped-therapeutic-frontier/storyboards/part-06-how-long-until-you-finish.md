# Part 6 storyboard: "The Timeline (How Long Until You Finish Looking)"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/untapped-therapeutic-frontier.qmd`, "The Timeline (How Long Until You Finish Looking)" and "But Won't We Run Out of Easy Discoveries?".
- **The numbers:**
  - `current_trials_per_year` = 3,300 trials/year.
  - `drug_disease_combinations_possible` = 9.5 million combinations.
  - `current_known_safe_exploration_years` = 2,879 years (the narrator says "two point eight eight thousand" and "two thousand eight hundred and eighty"). Derived: from 2026 it ends about 4905, the narrator's "roughly the year five thousand".
  - `combination_therapy_disease_space` = 45.1 billion combinations.
  - `current_combination_exploration_years` = 13.7 million years. For scale: Homo sapiens is about 300,000 years old (spoken as "longer than Homo sapiens has existed"); the sun has about 5 billion years left.
  - Chemical space 10^23 to 10^60 molecules (cited, not a variable); the universe is 13.8 billion years old (spoken, not a variable).
  - Diminishing returns dominate only after about 37% of the space is explored (the chapter's analysis, not a variable). Derived: 37% of 45.1 billion is 16.7 billion combinations, which at 3,300 a year is about 5.06 million years, the narrator's "over five million years".
  - `exploration_ratio` = 0.342% (where humans are now, for the chart's YOU ARE HERE).
  - The orchard: a 47-page application, three letters, IRB approval, a 14-month wait, page twelve (spoken).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** at today's pace, finishing even the drugs already on the shelf takes until about the year 5000, and the search has barely started, so the easy finds are nowhere near used up.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-ruler`: a ruler of years laid along the ground on aged paper, starting at 2026, with year marks; it keeps getting longer: to 5000, back in time to the trees, forward under the sun, and off the end of the paper into Part 4's starfield of molecules.   - `orchard`: an orchard behind a high locked iron gate, a little desk outside it.
  - `returns-chart`: a line chart on aged paper, "WHAT EACH TRIAL TEACHES YOU" against "% EXPLORED".
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.

- **New art:**

- voiceover: "Diminishing returns kicks in after you've explored a meaningful fraction of the space. At less than one percent explored, every trial teaches you something new, improving your ability to predict what works next. The math: even with weak learning effects, diminishing returns only dominates after exploring roughly thirty-seven percent of therapeutic space. For combination therapies, that crossover is over five million years away at current pace (thirty-seven percent of forty-five point one billion combinations at three thousand three hundred trials per year). You cannot have diminishing returns when you haven't had any returns. See the formal analysis for the equations."
