# Part 5 storyboard: "Why You Are Still Sick (A Diagnosis)"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/untapped-therapeutic-frontier.qmd`, "Why You Are Still Sick (A Diagnosis)", with its citation `preferential-target-attachment`.
- **The numbers:**
  - "$100 million per introduction" (spoken; the top of the chapter's Phase II to III range).
  - A typical Phase II to III trial costs $30 to $100 million (cited in the chapter, not a variable).
  - `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` = $41,000 per patient (median).
  - "twelve targets" (spoken, not a variable).
  - Metformin: a 1950s diabetes drug, off-patent, costs pennies; the TAME trial on aging spent years seeking funding (cited, not variables).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the science is not the hold-up; each test costs so much that only drugs someone can patent, for diseases someone can sell to, ever get tried.
