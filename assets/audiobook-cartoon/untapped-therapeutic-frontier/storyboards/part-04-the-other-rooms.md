# Part 4 storyboard: "The Other 99.66% Is Not Useless"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/untapped-therapeutic-frontier.qmd`, "The Other 99.66% Is Not Useless" and "Beyond Your Cupboard (Where It Gets Embarrassing)", with its citations `disease-network-overlap`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `human_interactome_targeted_pct` = 12% (so 88% never targeted). Mapped from 350,000+ clinical trials (cited, not a variable). For the mansion: 100 rooms, 12 lit, 88 padlocked.
  - "three cures from two drugs" (from Part 1).
  - Lithium: prescribed for gout in the 1800s, later the standard treatment for bipolar disorder (cited, not a variable).
  - "a one hundred million dollar clinical trial" (spoken; the chapter's $30 to $100 million Phase II to III range, not a variable).
  - Chemical space: 10^23 to 10^60 drug-like molecules (cited, not a variable). For scale: about 7 × 10^27 atoms in a human body, about 10^11 stars in the Milky Way.
  - ZINC22: tens of billions of purchasable virtual molecules (cited, not a variable).
  - Synthesized: less than 10^-40 of possible molecules; tested over possible: about 10^-20 to 10^-50 (the chapter's estimates, not variables). Written out, 10^-50 is a decimal point, 49 zeros and a 1.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the untried pairs are not leftovers: most of the body has never been aimed at, drugs already hit things nobody looked for, and the molecules humans could make outnumber the stars.
- **Plates:**
  - `mansion`: a cutaway mansion, ten floors of ten rooms (a 10 by 10 chart of 100 rooms), with a long hall and a stairwell, on aged paper. Twelve of the rooms are bathrooms.
  - `disease-web`: a corkboard of germs on pins joined with red string, Grandma's armchair at one side.
  - `chemical-space`: a night starfield where every star is a little molecule; Part 2's medicine cabinet at one edge.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** existing: `you`, `anatomy-man`, `scientist` (several), `journals`, `padlock`, `germ`, `pill-bottle`, `gold-star`, `newspaper-man` and `patient` with Part 1's `hair` (the Viagra and Rogaine men), `push-pin`, `red-string`, `grandma`, `grandma-skeleton`, `armchair`, `price-tag`, `calendar`, `medicine-cabinet` (Part 2), `fda-sip`, `tally-board`, `laptop`. New: `molecule`, `shoe-box`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 1's tented newspapers and the man with the mane, behind two of the mansion's doors, for "Remember Viagra? ... Rogaine?". Part 2's medicine cabinet: the camera leaves it through its back wall into chemical space. The shoes come back as the shoes YOU is still lacing at the start line.
- **New art:**
  - `molecule` (shared with the FDA chapter): a ball-and-stick molecule with little grabbing hands at the ends of its sticks; the stars of chemical space, close up.
  - `shoe-box`: a shoe box, drawn once, recoloured, stackable into a heap.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: a 10 by 10 grid of rooms that light one by one; a number written out digit by digit along a path.
  - `pile` (Part 2's): the shoe boxes.
  - `string`: a red string drawn from one pin to another over time; it can sag and snap.
