# Part 3 storyboard: "The Four Dollar Pill"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/nih-fails-2-institute-health.qmd`, "The Translation Crisis", from "Then there's metformin" to "the nutritional content."
- **The numbers:**
  - Metformin: the COVID-OUT Phase 3 trial cut long COVID incidence by 41%; $4 a month; generic for decades (cited, not parameters). N.I.H. guideline: "insufficient evidence to recommend for or against".
  - Long COVID: $170 billion a year in lost U.S. wages; about 17 million Americans affected; a RECOVERY-style trial would have cost about $24 million; the U.S. spent $5 billion on the antibodies (cited, not parameters).
  - Derived: $170 billion ÷ $24 million = 7,083 (the narrator's "seven thousand and eighty-three to one").
  - Papers: 2.5 million funded papers a year (cited, not a parameter); `current_drug_approvals_per_year`: 50 new drugs a year. Derived: 50 ÷ 2.5 million = 0.002%, one drug per 50,000 papers.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** a cheap generic that works gets no trial money because nobody profits from it, and the N.I.H. makes 50,000 papers for every drug that reaches a human.
- **Plates:**
  - `four-dollar-pill`: a plain floor; one small bottle; a bar chart behind it, "LONG COVID CASES".
  - `the-scale`: a giant brass balance scale on a plain floor.
  - `paper-mountain`: the N.I.H. building on a plain field, a chute from its front door.
- **Cast:** existing: `pill-bottle`, `sign-board` (the bottle's SPONSOR WANTED sign), `balance-scale`, `money-bundle`, `money-hill`, `calendar`, `capitol`, `patient`, `citizen`, `scientist`. Shared new: `nih-building` (`nih-read`, `nih-stamp`), `pharma-proud` (the drug company as its building, a white pill-bottle tower, cap for a hat, window eyes), `antibody-fridge` (Part 2). New: `paper-snake`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 2's fridge of antibodies (the $5 billion) sits on the scale's pan. Set up for Part 6: the $4 generic comes back in the boxing ring against the $80,000 drug.
- **New art:**
  - `paper-snake`: a long snake made of stacked papers and journals, wearing pince-nez, mouth open; it bites its tail; holds a pencil.
  - The `four-dollar-pill`, `the-scale` and `paper-mountain` plates.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `stamp-long`: a stamp whose impression runs on past the frame and the camera pans to read it.
  - `ring`: a cut-out bent round a circle that turns (the snake).

## Scene 3 · publishing-house

- id: publishing-house

- voiceover: "The N.I.H. is, by its own metrics, a publishing house that occasionally dabbles in medicine. It publishes two point five million funded papers annually.
