# Part 9 storyboard: "Losing Composure"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/nih-fails-2-institute-health.qmd`, "The Preventable Deaths" and the callout "The $600,000 Grant That May Have Cost $16 Trillion", to "I am losing my composure."
- **The numbers:**
  - The misallocation: at least 10 million QALYs and 700,000 preventable deaths a year; upper bound 100 million QALYs and 7 million deaths (the book's estimate, hardcoded, not parameters).
  - `nih_traditional_trial_max_efficiency_pct`: 2.27%, traditional trials' efficiency against pragmatic ones.
