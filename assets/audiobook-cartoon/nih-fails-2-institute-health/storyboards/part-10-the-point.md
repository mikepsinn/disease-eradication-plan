# Part 10 storyboard: "The Point"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/nih-fails-2-institute-health.qmd`, the callout "The $600,000 Grant That May Have Cost $16 Trillion", from "The F.B.I., the Department of Energy, and the C.I.A." to "costing one trillion dollars a year."
- **The numbers:** all cited in the chapter, not parameters.
  - The FBI, the Department of Energy and the CIA (January 2025) and a two-year congressional investigation lean to a lab leak; four intelligence agencies still lean to natural origin.
  - If the assessments are right: $14 to $16 trillion of U.S. economic damage; 7 million confirmed deaths, 19 to 36 million by excess mortality; $1 trillion a year in global long COVID costs.
  - The N.I.H.'s whole budget since 1970, inflation-adjusted: about $1.1 trillion. Derived: $14 to $16 trillion ÷ $1.1 trillion ≈ 13 to 14.5 (the narrator: "roughly thirteen times").
  - $1.7 billion to the N.I.H. for long COVID, 85% spent on observation (the narration; Part 1's RECOVER had $1.665 billion).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** if the assessments are right, one grant nobody weighed cost thirteen times the N.I.H.'s whole history, because nobody's job is to ask whether research helps.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-fork`: a plain road on aged paper running from the N.I.H. building past a row of government agency buildings to a fork: left, SIDE QUEST, a little lab with a bat in its window and a till at its door; right, TESTING, Part 2's shelf of safe bottles. The receipt prints at the lab, the bars of the 13× chart rise at the fork, and in Scene 6 the same plate turns into a 1950s world of tomorrow.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, the COMPOSURE gauge.
  - `the-lobby`: the N.I.H.'s marble lobby, a bench, a door at the far end, a directory board on its wall.
