# Part 2 storyboard: "The Pipeline Is Full"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/nih-fails-2-institute-health.qmd`, "The Translation Crisis: All Theory, No Medicine", from its heading to "move on." (the monoclonal antibody story).
- **The numbers:**
  - `fda_approved_products_count`: 20,000 F.D.A.-approved drugs known safe in humans.
  - Over 3,000 marketed drugs that could be repurposed; in Alzheimer's, hundreds of safe candidates with under 10% in trials; zero trials for "your grandma in St. Louis" (cited, not parameters).
  - Fifty years of indoor rain: the N.I.H. since about 1970 (the narration).
  - Antibodies: 2023 case reports of remission within days, sustained over two years; the U.S. spent over $5 billion on millions of doses; the first Phase 2 trial for long COVID is scheduled for mid-2026, four years after the doses were available (cited, not parameters).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the research worked and the testing never happened; the government owned medicine that helped long COVID patients and let it expire rather than test it.
- **Plates:**
  - `pipeline`: one long glass pipe across a plain field, from the N.I.H. building at the left into a warehouse whose shelves of dusty bottles (Part 9's shelf) run to the horizon; at the warehouse's far end, a long wall with one tiny door, TESTING FACILITY. Scenes 2 and 4 travel it.
  - `indoor-rain`: the N.I.H.'s marble lobby, rain falling from its ceiling.
  - `antibody-fridge`: a walk-in refrigerated store, glass-fronted, stacked with doses; the Capitol beside it; a line of long COVID patients outside.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `pill-bottle`, `shelf`, `scientist`, `grandma`, `ticket`, `committee`, `gold-star`, `capitol`, `germ` (the spiky virus, and one in a sash for Omicron), `patient`, `hospital-bed`, `iv-pole`, `padlock`, `calendar`, `wastebasket`, `leash`, `sign-board` (the placards), `trial-door` (the testing facility's door), `skeleton`, `money-bundle`, `bill-med`, `cobweb`, `spider`, `beard`. - **Callbacks:** Part 1's opera glasses (the N.I.H. watching the rain). Episode 1 Part 9's warehouse shelf. Set up for this chapter: Wishonia's drawer of forbidden words opens again in Parts 7 and 10.
- **New art:**
  - `pipeline`: a long riveted glass pipe on trestles, full of little pill bottles and molecules; a bulge; a rivet that pops.
  - `umbrella`: an umbrella made of a stapled stack of papers, dry and soggy.
  - `antibody-fridge`: a walk-in fridge with a glass front and a thermometer, stacked with boxed doses; its door takes padlocks.
  - `antibody-dose`: a boxed infusion bag of antibodies; fresh, and green and furry when expired.
- **Kit additions:**
