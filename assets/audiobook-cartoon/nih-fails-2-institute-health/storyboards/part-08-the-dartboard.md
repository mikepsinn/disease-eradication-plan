# Part 8 storyboard: "The Dartboard"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/nih-fails-2-institute-health.qmd`, "The Patient Disconnect: Zero Correlation with Health Outcomes", "The Human Cost of Misallocation" and "Cost Per Quality-Adjusted Life Year".
- **The numbers:**
  - Correlation between N.I.H. funding and disease burden: 0.07 (cited, not a parameter); the N.I.H. employs 20,000 people; fifty years.
  - `nih_annual_budget`: $47 billion a year.
  - A QALY: one year of healthy life. Dying at 40 instead of 80 loses 40 QALYs (the narration).
  - `dfda_trial_cost_reduction_factor`: 44.1× cheaper per patient, from `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` $41,000 to `adaptable_trial_cost_per_patient` $929 (the ADAPTABLE trial). Derived: one $41,000 seat buys 44 pragmatic seats.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the N.I.H. spends its money almost at random against what kills people, because committees decide, while trials 44 times cheaper exist.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-street`: one long street on aged paper. The N.I.H. building, a big scatter chart on an easel on its steps (MONEY up the side, DEATHS along the bottom), a lamp post with a dartboard on the pavement outside, and the kerb in front of it; far down the street, over a river, a railway and a golf course, the patient's little house.
  - `the-slide`: a microscope slide seen down the eyepiece, a round field.
  - `the-ruler`: an 80-year ruler along a plain floor, running up to the gate of a graveyard where every headstone has a filing cabinet.
- **Cast:** existing: `committee`, `headstone`, `skeleton`, `filing-cabinet`, `ruler`, `you`, `you-skeleton`, `crutch`, `patient`, `sick-1` to `sick-6` (the patients on the kerb), `scientist`, `armchair` (the traditional trial's velvet seat), `sandwich`, `money-bundle`, `bill-med`, `coin`, `citizen`, `house`, `germ`, `shelf`, `pill-bottle`. Shared new: `nih-building`. From Part 4: `giant-microscope`. New: `drunk`, `dartboard`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 7's sandwiches sit in the traditional trial's meeting room. Episode 1's `skeleton` and grinning dead.
- **New art:**
  - `drunk`: a 1950s drunk in a crumpled suit and loosened tie, a bottle in his pocket, swaying, one eye shut; throwing.
  - `dartboard`: a pub dartboard whose rings are labelled with diseases (labels in code); darts.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `scatter`: dots on a chart, each a little cut-out, that can move.
  - `divide`: one cut-out splitting into two copies, the copies splitting again (the committees).
