# Part 6 storyboard: "The Old Calculus"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/incentive-alignment-bonds.qmd`, "The Setup" and "The Old Calculus (Without IABs)".
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_campaign_total_cost`: $1 billion raised.
  - `victory_bond_annual_return_pct`: 272% (what investors want; on screen as PROJECTED).
  - The referendum: 70% of Americans support moving 1% of military spending to pragmatic trials (the worked example's figure, not a parameter). `treaty_reduction_pct` 1%.
