# Part 7 storyboard: "Senator Smith Votes Yes"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/incentive-alignment-bonds.qmd`, "The New Calculus (With IABs)", "What Actually Happens" and "Post-Treaty".
- **The numbers:** all from the book's worked example, not parameters.
  - Yes: score 45 + 15 = 60, × the early-supporter multiplier = 72 (derived: a ×1.2 multiplier); reelection 55% → 62% (with $500,000 of independent support); tier 3 → 2; post-office income $200,000 → $300,000 a year.
  - No: score 45 − 15 = 30; reelection 55% → 48% ($2 million to her opponent); stuck at tier 3.
  - The swing at the vote: +14 points (62% against 48%) and +$2 million of lifetime post-office earnings (derived: $100,000 a year more for 20 years).
  - The vote: staff model 72 and +7 points; three major contractors back the treaty; Super PACs commit $50 million nationally, $500,000 to her at 72; 70% of Texans voted yes; she and 50 others vote yes (51, a Senate majority).
  - After: score 72 → 78 (45 → 78, "over seventy percent"; derived +73%); reelection 55% → 68% (+13 points); independent support $0 → $3 million; tier 3 → 1; income $200,000 → $500,000 a year ("more than doubles", ×2.5).
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1% (the contractors' market shrinks 1%).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** once the score pays, a yes vote is the better deal for the politician on every count, so she votes yes and does better still afterward.
- **Plates:**
