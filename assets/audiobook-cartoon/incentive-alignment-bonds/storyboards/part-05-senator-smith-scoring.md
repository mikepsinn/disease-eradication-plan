# Part 5 storyboard: "How Senator Smith Is Scored"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/incentive-alignment-bonds.qmd`, "Worked Example: How Senator Smith Votes Yes" and "The Scoring System".
- **The numbers:** all from the book's scoring table, not parameters.
  - Votes: raise the treaty share, yes +15 / no −15; protect funding, yes +10 / no −10; expand to more countries, yes +10 / no −10; cut funding, yes −20 / no +5.
  - Bands: 75+ gets +$2 million in PAC spending, Tier 1 (W.H.O. boards, Aspen fellowships), $500,000+ a year; 60 to 74 gets +$500,000, Tier 2 (Brookings, RAND, university chairs), $200,000 to $400,000; 50 to 59 gets $0, Tier 3, $150,000 to $300,000; 40 to 49 sends $500,000 to the opponent, Tier 3, $150,000 to $300,000; under 40 sends $2 million to the opponent, Tier 3, $150,000 to $300,000.
  - Drawn: the high striker's tower is a 0 to 100 scale, its bands painted at 40, 50, 60 and 75. Income ranges are small range bars beside each band (the book's own ranges).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** each vote moves a public number, and the number decides whether the campaign money goes to you or to your opponent, and which job you get afterward.
- **Plates:**
  - `lecture-hall`: a plain field with a chalkboard and a row of seated citizens; a big light bulb on a stand.
  - `high-striker`: a carnival high striker on a plain field, its tower as tall as the frame and taller (the camera climbs it), a bell at the top at 100, the bands painted up its side with their prizes hung beside them; iron beds of patients at its foot.
- **Cast:** existing: `economist`, `chalkboard`, `citizen`, `light-bulb`, `politician` (as Smith's opponent, purple tie), `super-pac`, `campaign-poster`, `door`, `armchair`, `patient`, `patient-well`, `iron-bed`. From Part 3: `revolving-door`. New: `senator-smith`, `high-striker`.
- **Callbacks:** episode 1's `moth` (Parts 1 to 19) is what Smith flies like. Part 3's W.H.O. BOARD door, fellowship armchair and revolving door are the prizes. Set up for Parts 6 and 7: Smith's bib and her trading card.
- **New art:**
  - `senator-smith`: a fictional senator (R-Texas): a brisk woman in a 1950s skirt suit, pearls, a cream Stetson; moth wings as a separate piece; at a desk, swinging a mallet, at a YES button, on skis, at a boardroom table. Her soul is the same cut-out, pale and see-through (in code).
  - `high-striker`: a carnival test-your-strength tower with a puck that slides, a lever to hit, a brass bell on top, and its big wooden mallet.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `slide`: a puck that slides up and down a track to a value, with a little overshoot.
  - `branch`: lines that grow out of a shape into a tree of glowing nodes (the skill tree), drawn in code.
