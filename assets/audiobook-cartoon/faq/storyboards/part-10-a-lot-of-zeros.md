# Part 10 storyboard: "A Lot of Zeros"

- **Source:** `knowledge/appendix/faq.qmd`, the follow-up "Won't AI drug discovery shrink that timeline anyway?" and "How Do You Prevent Waste?".
- **The numbers:**
  - `current_trial_slots_available`: 1.9 million patients a year.
  - `new_disease_first_treatments_per_year`: 15 diseases a year.
  - `dfda_trial_capacity_multiplier`: 12.3x capacity.
  - `dfda_pragmatic_trial_cost_per_patient`: $929 per patient.
  - RECOVER: $1.6 billion over four years, zero treatment recommendations (hardcoded in `faq.qmd`, not a parameter).
  - `recovery_trial_cost_reduction_factor`: 82x lower cost per patient, from `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` $41,000 ÷ `recovery_trial_cost_per_patient` $500. Drawn to scale, if the $500 bar is 10 px, the $41,000 bar is 820 px.
  - Derived: $1.6 billion ÷ $500 = 3.2 million patients ("millions of patients"); at $929, about 1.7 million.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the bottleneck is trial slots, not ideas, so AI needs this system; and the money spent the old way bought nothing that the same money would have bought many times over the new way.
- **Plates:**
  - `step-four`: YOU on step four (Part 1).
  - `the-jam`: a long plain floor to ep. 1 Part 12's long wall with its tiny trial door, "WAIT HERE"; the robot at the left end.
  - `nih-street`: the NIH building on a plain field, a calendar on a post, and beside it a two-bar chart, "COST PER PATIENT".
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck.
- **Cast:** existing: `you`, `robot`, `laptop`, `scientist`, `pill-bottle`, `patient`, `patient-skeleton`, `wrecking-ball`, `bundle-med`, `calendar`, `rosette`, `binder`, `sick-1` to `sick-6`. From ep. 1 (not drawn yet): `trial-door`, `beard`, `envelope`, `zero`, `ladder`, `citizen` (the administrators, in hats of many kinds), `committee`, `flask`. From Part 2: `cadillac`. New: `nih-building`, `gilt-frame`.
- **Callbacks:** ep. 1 Part 12's tiny trial door in the long wall, "WAIT HERE", is the bottleneck; here it is finally knocked out. YOU's Cadillac from Part 2 is the robot's faster car. Ep. 1 Part 16's big stamped digit is the zero. The chapter ends on a zero that is also the iris; YOU's beard reaches the floor.
- **New art:** the NIH as its building (a 1950s federal block with a face and stick arms, "N.I.H." on its frieze); a gilt picture frame, in two sizes.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `queue`: copies piling up nose to tail behind a gate.
  - `iris`: an iris-out whose shape is a cut-out (the zero).
