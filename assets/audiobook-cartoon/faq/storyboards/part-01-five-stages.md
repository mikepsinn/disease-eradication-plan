- **Source:** `knowledge/appendix/faq.qmd`, the opening and "We Need the Military Budget" (to the COVID paragraph).
- **The numbers:**
  - The treaty share: 1% (`treaty_reduction_pct`, the 1% treaty); `defense_sector_retention_pct` 99% kept.
  - `pentagon_unaccounted_funds`: $2.46 trillion; the narrator says "two point five trillion", so the screen says $2.5 TRILLION.

  - 147 presentations (hardcoded in `faq.qmd`, not a parameter).

This plate recurs in every part.
