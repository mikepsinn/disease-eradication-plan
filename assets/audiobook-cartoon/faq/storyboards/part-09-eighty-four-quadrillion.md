# Part 9 storyboard: "Eighty-four Quadrillion"

- **Source:** `knowledge/appendix/faq.qmd`, "Why Not Just Use Philanthropy?" and "Eighty-four Quadrillion Dollars Is a Made-Up Number"; `knowledge/appendix/parameters-and-calculations.qmd` for the chain.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_annual_daly_burden`: 2.88 billion DALYs a year.
  - `eventually_avoidable_daly_pct`: 92.6%.
  - `standard_economic_qaly_value_usd`: $150,000 per QALY.
  - `global_disease_economic_burden_annual`: $400 trillion a year (2.88 billion × 92.6% × $150,000 ≈ $400 trillion).
  - `dfda_trial_capacity_plus_efficacy_lag_years`: 212 years sooner.
  - `dfda_trial_capacity_multiplier`: 12.3x = `dfda_patients_fundable_annually` 23.4 million ÷ `current_trial_slots_available` 1.9 million.
  - `dfda_patients_fundable_annually`: 23.4 million = annual subsidies ÷ `dfda_pragmatic_trial_cost_per_patient` $929.
  - Annual subsidies: $21.8 billion = `dfda_annual_trial_funding` $21.8 billion − `dfda_annual_opex` $40 million.
  - `dfda_annual_opex`: $40 million = platform $15 million + staff $10 million + infrastructure $8 million (+ regulatory $5 million + community $2 million, which the narrator does not name).
  - `global_gdp_2025`: $115 trillion.
  - The title's 84 quadrillion: the narrator's multiplication, $400 trillion × 212 ≈ $84.8 quadrillion. The current `dfda_trial_capacity_plus_efficacy_lag_economic_value` is $7.53 quadrillion, not 84. The screen uses the narrator's 84 QUADRILLION.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** charity is too small to matter, and the giant number is four ordinary inputs multiplied, with only the 212 years open to debate.
