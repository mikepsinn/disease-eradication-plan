# Part 6 storyboard: "Poorer Than a Pharaoh"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/wishonia.qmd`, "The Peace Dividend Economy", from "Here's what that means for your paycheck" to "rather than the obvious thing." (narration script lines 117 to 128). It links no `knowledge/` pages for these numbers.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The narrator's, shown as said:
  - Productivity per person "roughly tripled since nineteen seventy"; median wages, adjusted for living costs, "barely moved". The chart draws productivity reaching 3× by today and wages flat.
  - The pharaoh: 10,000 servants; died at 40.
  - Better than the richest human before 1900; 2026 billionaires.
  - A 200,000-year-old algorithm (the narrator's age for the human brain's reward system).
  - "Pennies a day".
- **What the viewer must leave with:** your work already pays for a much richer life, the gap goes to weapons, paperwork and finance, and closing it makes the poorest person richer than a pharaoh.

  - `late-night-ad`: full frame, a realistic late-night charity commercial: soft focus, a white superyacht at dusk drawn into the plate, sad piano.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
