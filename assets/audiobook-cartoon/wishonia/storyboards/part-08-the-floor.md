# Part 8 storyboard: "The Floor"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/wishonia.qmd`, "The Peace Dividend Economy", from "But the UBI is just cash" to "You can too." (narration script lines 145 to 163). It links no `knowledge/` pages for these numbers; the healthcare waste is a parameter.
- **The numbers:**
  - The insurer's cut: "twenty percent" (the narrator's, not a parameter); on screen, 20%.
  - Aspirin: "fifty dollars", behind "seven layers" (the narrator's); on screen, $50 and seven boxes.
  - `us_gov_waste_healthcare_inefficiency`: $1.2 trillion a year wasted by the US healthcare system "on this arguing".
  - Education and healthcare as the two fastest-rising costs: the narrator's claim, no numbers spoken; the chart draws them rising and the other lines flat or falling, with no values on the axis.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** when the money follows the person and the price is visible, bad providers lose, and a floor under everyone is a setting you can choose.
- **Plates:**
  - `school-street`: a street of schoolhouses on aged paper, one per lot, a pavement in front; at its far end Part 7's trapdoor and the smart contract on its pedestal.
  - `doctor-road`: a road on aged paper from YOU's house past the insurer building's toll gate to a hospital window, then on along a row of clinic storefronts with price cards in their windows (drawn into the plate).
  - `elevator-shaft`: a cutaway elevator shaft on aged paper, two cars side by side.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `schoolhouse`, `human-ages` (the schoolboy), `bill-plain`, `bill-med`, `auditor` (the bureaucrat, from Part 7), `cobweb`, `parent`, `orphan`, `photographer`, `tax-office` (the IRS building, and recoloured as the Department of Education), `trapdoor`, `smart-contract` (from Part 7), `you`, `doctor`, `claim-form`, `hospital`, `gift-bow`, `pill-bottle`, `patient`, `patient-skeleton`, `iron-bed`, `gauge` (with its needle), `calendar`, `ray-machine` (from Part 1), `citizen`, `speech-bubbles`, `gloved-hand`, `wallet` (from Part 6), `telephone`, `flames`, `price-tag`, `gold-star`, `economist`, `pull-down-chart`, `committee`, `hungry-man`, `skeleton`, `armchair`, `sandwich`, `knife-switch`. New: `insurer-building`, `elevator`.
- **Callbacks:**
  - Part 7 (shredder): the IRS building fell through the trapdoor; the Department of Education follows it.
  - Part 7 (trickles-up): the same glass machine pays the education and health credits.
  - Part 6 (host-not-robbed): the gloved hand in the wallet is "you, right now, but worse".
  - Part 1 (same-machine): Wishonia's little ray machine fixes the patient.
  - Part 5 (host-economist): the economist with the charts is wrong again, and gets the plaster.
- **New art:**
  - `insurer-building`: the insurance company as its building, a tall marble office with a face, a striped toll-gate pole for an arm across the road, a long stamping arm (NO), and a snipping arm with scissors.
  - `elevator`: a cutaway 1950s elevator car with a brass floor dial above its door; used twice.
