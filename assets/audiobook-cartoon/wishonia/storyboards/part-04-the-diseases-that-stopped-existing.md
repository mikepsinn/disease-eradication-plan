# Part 4 storyboard: "The Diseases That Stopped Existing"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/wishonia.qmd`, "The Diseases That Stopped Existing" (narration script lines 76 to 92). It links no `knowledge/` pages for numbers.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`; all are the narrator's, shown as said:
  - Most cancers caught at stage 0 for millennia; the last oncology wing became a trampoline park 3,800 years ago.
  - Alzheimer's caught 20 years before symptoms.
  - Aging solved around generation two. Generation one after the treaty: aging 40% slower, so the midlife crisis comes at 70 instead of 40, "thirty extra years".
  - Death at 150 after a good life, against 75 today.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** after the treaty the big killers are caught early and fixed, people live to 150, and the generation after that stops dying.
- **Plates:**

  - `age-line`: one long line chart on aged paper, "AGE", 0 to 150 along the bottom, with a green lawn past its far end. The camera travels it for the last three scenes.
