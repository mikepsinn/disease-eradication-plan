- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/wishonia.qmd`, the opening and "What the Optimized World Looks Like" to "Although the evidence is mixed." (the narration script `wishonia.prepared.txt`, lines 1 to 25). It links `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd` for the treaty.
