- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/wishonia.qmd`, "Your Corporate Heroes" and "Our Current Problems" (the narration script `wishonia.prepared.txt`, lines 165 to 196). It links `knowledge/solution/incentive-alignment-bonds.qmd` for the bonds.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`; all are the chapter's own, hardcoded in the prose, and the narrator says them:
  - Lockheed Martin Health stock: $12,000 a share. On screen: a line chart that runs out of the top of the paper at $12,000.
  - "seventy years of weapons manufacturing": the chart's flat stretch before the bend, 70 YEARS.
  - Raytheon Wellness: 50,000,000 surgeries, 0 villages destroyed (counter board).
