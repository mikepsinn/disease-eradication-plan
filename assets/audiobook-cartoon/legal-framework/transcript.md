**[00:13:15]** Securities Law (A Section Named After Oranges).

**[00:13:19]** VICTORY Incentive Alignment Bonds are securities under the Howey Test, a legal test named after a citrus company. Your entire securities regulation framework traces back to a man selling oranges in Florida. This is, genuinely, how the most powerful economy on Earth decides what money is. Your species finds this charming in the way that you find a child's drawing charming: it's technically wrong but it clearly means well.

**[00:13:47]** The plan for securities is boring on purpose: use actual exemptions, actual filings, actual disclosures, and actual separation between investment money, charitable money, and election money. Regulation D covers private accredited capital. Regulation C.F. and Regulation A can reach ordinary investors with the required portal, offering, and disclosure machinery. A filing or exemption is not S.E.C. approval. It is permission to offer under a rule while antifraud law still stares at you from the corner.

**[00:14:21]** Regulatory environment, twenty twenty-six. The current environment is friendlier to novel capital formation than the twenty twenty-four environment, but it does not repeal the nouns. A tokenized security is still a security. A Prize deposit with pooled investment upside is still a security. A private equity offering is still a securities offering. The S.E.C. crypto interpretive release helps classify crypto assets and transactions; it does not turn an investment contract into a video-game coupon. FinCEN delayed the investment-adviser anti-money laundering and countering the financing of terrorism, or A.M.L. C.F.T., program rule to January first, twenty twenty-eight, which buys time but not immunity. The S.E.C. withdrew multiple pending investment-management proposals in June twenty twenty-five, including E.S.G. fund-disclosure proposals, proposed rescinding the twenty twenty-four climate disclosure rules in May twenty twenty-six, and replaced S.L.B. fourteen L with S.L.B. fourteen M for shareholder proposals. Translation: cheaper and more permissive in places, less friendly to some E.S.G. or proxy strategies in others, and still very capable of ruining your week if the disclosure is sloppy.

**[00:20:31]** Why it cannot be the Referee: The entity lobbying for the outcome cannot measure whether the outcome occurred. This is the most obvious conflict of interest in the system and the one your species would notice first.

**[00:20:44]** On your planet, the equivalent would be letting the military contractor grade the weapons test. You already do this, actually, which is why your weapons cost ten times what they should and work half as well as advertised, but that is a different chapter.

**[00:20:56]** Why it cannot be the Vault: The I.R.S. requires charitable funds and political funds to stay separated. The S.E.C. requires investment funds and political funds to stay separated. The F.E.C. requires foreign funds and domestic political funds to stay separated.

**[00:21:12]** Three regulatory agencies, three firewalls, zero overlap. Your species built these walls because every time it didn't, the money ended up in the wrong place and someone went to prison. The walls are annoying but they work.

**[00:21:27]** How it interfaces with the Scoreboard: The Sword's operational tempo is driven by the Scoreboard numbers. **[00:21:47]** Above one hundred million: full-scale political campaign in every major democracy. **[00:22:08]** Redundancy: The Sword is naturally multi-node because campaign law is jurisdictional. You need a different legal entity in every country: a U.S. five hundred and one c four, an E.U.-equivalent advocacy organization, separate entities in every major democracy.

**[00:22:25]** Multiple Swords compete for bondholder capital to run the most effective campaign in their territory. Each Sword owns its jurisdiction clearly; its revenue depends on treaty passage in that territory, so the incentive to perform is direct and the ownership is unambiguous.

**[00:22:42]** A bad Sword loses bondholder capital to a more effective one in the same jurisdiction, the way a bad advertising agency loses clients. No tragedy of the commons because nobody is responsible for "the whole world." Everyone is responsible for their own country.

**[00:22:56]** Succession: If a Sword disappears, a replacement political advocacy organization in that jurisdiction can pick up the bond proceeds, the voter data (public on the Scoreboard), and the politician scoring methodology (published). The legal entities are separate from the people running them.
