# Part 4 storyboard: "The General Staff and the Oranges"

- **Source:** `knowledge/legal/legal-framework.qmd`, "The General Staff" and "Securities Law (A Section Named After Oranges)". The narration is from an earlier draft (the Victory Corporation and the Foundation; the book now says Earth Optimization Services and the mission-lock trust). The screen follows the narration.
- **The numbers:**
  - None from `_variables.yml`. The facts the pictures use: the staff sections G-1 to G-6 as the narrator lists them; the Howey Test comes from *SEC v. W. J. Howey Co.* (US Supreme Court, 1946), about a Florida company that sold strips of orange grove to buyers who let the company farm them and waited for the profit, which the Court called an investment contract, a security.
  - Regulation D (accredited investors only), then Regulation Crowdfunding and Regulation A+ (everyone), as the narrator says; KYC and AML throughout.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the company borrows the army's way of organising people and points it at disease, a nonprofit owner keeps its mission from drifting, and its bonds are real securities, sold first to the rich and then to everyone, with every buyer checked so nobody launders money through them.
- **Plates:**
  - `classroom`: two school desks side by side on a plain floor, a window behind; the camera pushes into the homework book's open page, a long plain field to a low hill.
  - `org-chart`: a wall-size org chart on aged paper: the owner's box at the top (the Soul), the company's box under it (the Engine), and six boxes in a row, G-1 to G-6, each a little room with its clerk; the camera travels down and along.
  - `bond-street`: one plain street, left to right: the Howey orange grove with a porch; the SEC building with an orange in a glass case at its door; YOU's bond counter in an old laundromat (its wash-tub and mangle padlocked), behind a club door and a velvet rope.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `you`, `school-desk`, `gold-star`, `pentagon`, `soldiers`, `skeleton`, `mushroom`, `citizen` (the clerks, the brokers and the grove buyers), `war-table`, `germ`, `trial-door`, `patient`, `crutch`, `money-bundle`, `bell-jar`, `alarm-light`, `coin`, `calendar`, `megaphone`, `manual`, `armchair`, `flames`, `bond`, `fat-cat`, `hungry-man`, `moth`, `prize-wheel`, `cloud`, `wash-tub`, `padlock`, `display-case`, `medicine-bottle`, `fed-crank-up`, `fed-crank-down`, `sign-board`. From Parts 1 to 3: `engine-building`, `soul-chalet`, `cuckoo`, `sec-building`, `wall-street`. New: `homework-book`, `orange`, `orange-grove`, `howey` (the grove salesman), `velvet-rope`, `smoke-pharma` (a building that is half cigarette factory, half pharmacy).
- **Callbacks:** episode 1's hungry man and the moth from his empty wallet (Parts 1, 2) are the ones kept out of the club. Episode 1's war-map table (Part 1) comes back with germs for soldiers. From this chapter: the Engine's leash (Parts 1 and 3) and the Brain's manual (Part 1), which G-6's megaphone fires into YOU's lap.

  - a homework book as thick as a wardrobe, its pages battle diagrams
