# Part 1 storyboard: "A Decentralized F.D.A."

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dfda.qmd`, its opening, "The Solution: Consumer Reports for Drugs", and its first paragraphs.
- **The numbers:**
  - `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` $41,000 a patient today; `dfda_pragmatic_trial_cost_per_patient` $929 a patient; `dfda_trial_cost_reduction_factor` 44.1×. Drawn as bars, the $929 bar is 1/44 of the $41,000 bar.
  - `current_trial_slots_available` 1.9 million patients a year; `dih_patients_fundable_annually` 23.4 million patients a year; `dfda_trial_capacity_multiplier` 12.3×.
  - `current_disease_patients_global` 2.4 billion people with chronic disease.
  - `patient_willingness_trial_participation_pct` 44.8% would volunteer: `willing_trial_participants_global` 1.08 billion people.
  - The participation rate: the narrator says 0.06%; `current_patient_participation_rate` in `_variables.yml` is now 0.0792% (1.9 million ÷ 2.4 billion). The screen uses the narrator's "0.06%".
  - `antidepressant_trial_exclusion_rate` 86.1% of real patients turned away: about 6 of every 7.
  - Spoken, not parameters: 90 to 99% of side effects missed, 1 to 10% of adverse events reported (cited, `adverse-event-underreporting`); disease eliminated on Wishonia 4,000 years ago; "over three decades" since the internet (about 1990).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** almost half of the 2.4 billion sick would join a trial, but there is room for 1.9 million, so the fix is capacity: an open protocol that plugs every treatment into every patient and writes down what happens.
