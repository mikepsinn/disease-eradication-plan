# Part 9 storyboard: "The Mathematical Impossibility of Committees" and "The Future"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dfda.qmd`, "Why This Works: The Mathematical Impossibility of Committees", "The Future: Where Death Becomes Embarrassing" and "How Your Decentralized FDA Actually Works".
- **The numbers:**
  - About 200 FDA reviewers (cited `fda-reviewer-count`); `global_population_2024` 8 billion people.
  - `current_drug_approvals_per_year` 50 new drugs a year; 10,000 known diseases (spoken, hardcoded in the chapter; `rare_diseases_count_global` is 7,000, with 10,000 its upper bound). Derived: 10,000 ÷ 50 = 200 years, so the last disease waits until about 2226.
  - The future: 2027, 2030, 2035, 2050 (spoken). In 2030: pills sold for $10,000 that cost $1 to make. The internet since 1990, computers since 1950; "five thousand years" of paperwork (writing, about 3000 BC).
