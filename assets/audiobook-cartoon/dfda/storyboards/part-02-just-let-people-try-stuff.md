# Part 2 storyboard: "How It Works: Just Let People Try Stuff (Carefully)"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dfda.qmd`, "How It Works: Just Let People Try Stuff (Carefully)"; the Type II ratio from `knowledge/appendix/invisible-graveyard.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - 95% of rare diseases have zero approved treatments (cited, not a parameter); drawn as a grid of 100 germs, 5 with a bottle.
  - "over eighty-five percent" barred: `antidepressant_trial_exclusion_rate` 86.1%; the narrator's "about fourteen percent" qualify (the book's table says 13.9%): about 1 in 7.
  - Sample sizes (cited, `us-trial-enrollment-targets-2009`, not parameters): 275 for a heart drug, 20 for a cancer drug, 100 for a diabetes drug. A kindergarten class is about 20 to 25 children.
  - For the scale in medicine-show (not spoken): `type_ii_error_cost_ratio` 3,389 people die waiting for a safe drug for every 1 protected from a dangerous one.
  - History for the pictures (not spoken): the 1906 Pure Food and Drug Act against patent medicines; thalidomide was kept off the US market by the FDA's Frances Kelsey in 1960, and the 1962 amendments that followed added the efficacy requirement.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** trials keep out the real sick and test on a few hundred perfect ones; the method that works is three steps: try it, write it down, show the next person.
- **Plates:**
  - `medicine-show`: a plain field, a patent-medicine wagon, and later the FDA building stacked on top of it; a big brass balance scale; along the field, three sick people in a row, and the US Capitol small in the distance.
  - `terrarium`: episode 1's lab bench and glass tank of tidy humans (Part 14), a little door in its side with a long checklist on a post beside it, the sick crowding the glass outside; a grid chart of rare diseases on the wall behind.
  - `sample-sizes`: three small batches of tidy humans on aged paper, then the world's crowd behind them; a kindergarten class photo.
- **Cast:** existing: `fda-sip`, `frances-kelsey`, `pill-bottle`, `hard-hat`, `balance-scale`, `skeleton`, `patient`, `patient-skeleton`, `citizen`, `medal`, `capitol`, `thought-bubble`, `terrarium`, `tidy-human`, `lab-bench`, `germ`, `ledger` (the checklist), `crowd`, `tank`.
