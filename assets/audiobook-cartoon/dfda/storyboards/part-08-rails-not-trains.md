# Part 8 storyboard: "The Money Shot", "The Partnership Approach" and "Federated, Not Centralized"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dfda.qmd`, "The Money Shot: How to Save 95% on Not Killing People", "The Itemized Receipt of Eliminated Stupidity", "The Partnership Approach: Building Rails, Not Trains", "The Players Already in the Game" and "Federated, Not Centralized"; the funding rule from `knowledge/solution/wishocracy.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - Timeline: "over a decade" (`fda_phase_1_to_approval_years` 10.5 years) to two to three years; about seven years saved and about 50,000 lives a year (spoken, hardcoded in the chapter).
  - Cost per trial: $57 million (cited `clinical-trial-cost-breakdown`) to $2 million; $55 million saved.
  - Who can take part: 13.9% of patients (cited `antidepressant-trial-exclusion-rates`) to 100%; `antidepressant_trial_exclusion_rate` 86.1% excluded today.
  - Rare diseases with treatments: 5% to "eventually" 100% (cited, spoken).
  - Federated networks already query 300 million plus patient records (spoken).
  - Funding: the protocol is one campaign competing for the 1% Treaty Fund (`treaty_annual_funding` $27.2 billion a year) through Wishocracy; it has no budget of its own.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the protocol is shared rails for the trial companies that already exist, it gets money only while it delivers, and nobody's data has to move.
- **Plates:**
  - `four-rows`: four before-and-after charts on aged paper, one under the other: TIMELINE, COST PER TRIAL, WHO CAN TAKE PART, RARE DISEASES WITH TREATMENTS; CURRENT INSANITY on the left of each, NEW REALITY on the right; episode 1's cash register at the bottom.
  - `rail-yard`: a plain field with little hospitals and labs scattered across it for stations, three trial-company office buildings, and later rails between them; along its edge, the treaty fund's bank and a row of campaigns.
  - `record-row`: three record buildings in a row on a plain field.
- **Cast:** existing: `pill-bottle`, `price-tag`, `patient-skeleton`, `headstone`, `calendar`, `patient`, `bill-med`, `scissors`, `gauge`, `needle`, `trial-door`, `picket-fence`, `germ`, `cash-register`, `receipt`, `capitol-legs`, `train`, `office-building`, `hospital`, `lab`, `bank`, `citizen`, `allocation-bar`, `lobbyist`, `briefcase`, `fda-sip`, `glowing-rectangle`, `shopping-cart`, `filing-cabinet`, `pigeon`, `shelf`, `padlock`, `hacker`, `laptop`, `plinth`, `cloud`, `trapdoor`.
