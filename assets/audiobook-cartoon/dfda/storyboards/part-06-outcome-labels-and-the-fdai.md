# Part 6 storyboard: "Outcome Labels", "The F.D.A.i." and "Making It Legal to Not Die"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dfda.qmd`, "Outcome Labels: Nutrition Facts for Drugs", "Your Personal Death-Prevention Assistant: The FDAi" and "Making It Legal to Not Die".
- **The numbers:** all spoken; the depression label is the book's example, not parameters:
  - OUTCOME LABEL: Depression Severity, 47,832 participants. 1. Bupropion −28.3%, 2,847 people, 300 mg. 2. Sertraline −24.7%, 5,123 people, 100 mg. 3. Venlafaxine −21.2%, 1,892 people, 150 mg.
  - The F.D.A.i.'s example: 50,000 similar cases; 60% reattachment surgery plus Drug A, 30% prosthetic foot plus Drug B, 10% hopping lessons plus prayer, 0% essential oils.
  - Precision dosing: best sleep after 400 mg of magnesium in the previous 24 hours, against a study of 36 college students in 1997.
  - "nearly two decades" of the technology (smartphones, about 2007); "three thousand years" on Wishonia.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** a drug's label should rank every treatment by what happened to real people, and your phone can read your own results to find what works for you and how much, once one law allows it.
- **Plates:**
  - `living-room`: YOU's living room from Part 5, where the patient sits in the armchair with a pill bottle.
  - `podium`: a plain field: a three-step winners' podium drawn as a bar chart (each step as tall as its effect), a grandstand behind it, then a running track.
  - `pocket`: a plain field with YOU standing in the middle, his phone big beside him; later his bed beside it.
  - Host scenes (host-at-birth, host-legal-to-not-die): Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** existing: `patient`, `armchair`, `cereal-box`, `cigarette-pack`, `pill-bottle`, `insert`, `outcome-label`, `miracle-sticker`, `lawyer`, `typewriter`, `crowd`, `medal`, `boot`, `money-bundle`, `you`, `you-walk`, `you-bed`, `glowing-rectangle` (the phones and the step counter), `doctor`, `wiki-page`, `halo`, `medicine-bottle`, `filing-cabinet`, `binder`, `germ`, `nurse`, `frances-kelsey`, `caveman-scientist`, `scientist`. Part 2's depressed line, each under a rain cloud, fills the grandstand. Part 3's turmeric jar on the race track (supplements). Part 5's banana costume is gone; YOU is in his tweed.
