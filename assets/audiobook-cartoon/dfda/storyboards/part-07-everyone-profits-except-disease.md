# Part 7 storyboard: "The Business Model: How Everyone Profits Except Disease"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dfda.qmd`, "The Business Model: How Everyone Profits Except Disease" with "How Companies Register Treatments", "The Payment Flow" and "Why This Creates Vastly More Research Capacity".
- **The numbers:**
  - Registration: five minutes, zero approval, net cost to the company $0 (spoken).
  - `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` $41,000: the data-collection cost each patient's reports replace.
  - The migraine example (spoken, not parameters): treatment $100 a month; deposit $50; pays $150; subsidy $125; out of pocket $25; gets back $50; net +$25. The company: $100 a month in, $20 manufacturing, $80 profit per patient per month, plus data worth $41,000 a patient.
  - The subsidy comes from the 1% Treaty Fund (`treaty_annual_funding` $27.2 billion a year; `dih_treasury_trial_subsidies_annual` $21.7 billion of it to trial subsidies; not spoken here).
  - $57 million per trial the old way (cited `clinical-trial-cost-breakdown`); about 10 trials a year per company against thousands at once; 95% of rare diseases with no treatment (cited); 100 patients worldwide is enough to run a trial (spoken).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the patient pays for the pills and the treaty subsidy pays the patient, so the company's trial costs it nothing and earns it money, and any treatment for any disease can be tested.
- **Plates:**
  - `medicine-loop`: episode 1 Part 2's loop road on aged paper (the-machine), rebuilt with four stops: the treaty fund's bank, a patient's house, the switchboard from Part 1 with the ranked board on a post, and the drug factory with a cash register in its door. The camera works close at one stop at a time and shows the whole loop only at the end.
  - `two-lanes`: two lanes side by side on aged paper, TRADITIONAL and DECENTRALIZED, from IDEAS at the left to TRIALS at the right; at the right end, Part 3's money hill above and a long shop shelf below.
  - `rare-globe`: the classroom globe on its stand, big, on a plain field.
- **Cast:** existing: `germ`, `moth`, `patient`, `scientist`, `bill-med`, `road`, `bank` (relabelled 1% TREATY FUND), `rank-board`, `egg-timer`, `swivel-chair`, `cobweb`, `spider`, `pill-bottle`, `loaf`, `boot`, `price-tag`, `hard-hat`, `ledger` (the bill), `glowing-rectangle`, `ice-pack`, `cash-register`, `filing-cabinet`, `gift-bow`, `light-bulb`, `committee`, `bucket`, `turnstile`, `money-bundle`, `globe`, `money-hill`, `shelf`, `cheese`, `medicine-bottle`, `lab`. Part 2's hard-hat bottle is the insured one. Part 3's money hill and its lone bottle. Part 5's ranked list, on the board.
