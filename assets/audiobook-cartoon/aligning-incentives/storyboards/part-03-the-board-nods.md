# Part 3 storyboard: "The Board Nods"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/aligning-incentives.qmd`, the end of "Why They Take It", "What You're NOT Asking" and "Why This Works" (to "This is, again, the design parameter").
- **The numbers:**
  - `victory_bond_funding_pct`: 10% (the bond share the chapter puts in a pension portfolio).
  - `average_market_return_pct`: 10% (the market's return).
  - `victory_bond_annual_return_pct`: 272% a year, projected.
  - `defense_sector_retention_pct`: 99% of their current income kept.
  - Derived for the sign on the bank: 90% × 10% + 10% × 272% = 36.2% blended return.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the bonds pay so well that every pension fund and weapons maker that holds them ends up lobbying for the treaty, and nothing is asked of them but taking the money.
- **Plates:**
  - `bond-street`: a plain street, the pension fund (a little bank with grandmas in rocking chairs on its porch, "RETURN: 10%" on a sign over its door) and two weapons factories side by side, LOCKHEED and RAYTHEON on their signs.
  - Host scene (host-the-plan): Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
  - `boardroom`: a long table, the weapons factory in a big swivel chair at its head, a chalkboard on legs beside it.
  - `legal-machine`: a fat law book on a podium that is the control panel of a run of brass pipes from the Capitol to the factory's door.
- **Cast:** existing: `bank`, `grandma`, `calculator`, `claims-clerk` (in his eyeshade, here the pension clerk), `factory-proud`, `factory-pay`, `factory-sob`, `committee`, `you`, `bond`, `lobbyist`, `sign-board`, `swivel-chair`, `chalkboard`, `podium`, `capitol`, `lab`, `book`, `spider`, `cobweb`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `money-bundle`, `top-hat`. New: `pipework` (a run of brass pipes with a swinging outlet and one dusty lever).
- **Callbacks:** the Calculator from episode 1 (Parts 16, 19, 22), which screams at big numbers. The committee's bobblehead nod from episode 1 Part 12 is the board.
- **New art:** the brass pipework with its lever and swinging outlet. The factory signs, the picket signs and the host blueprint are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `pass`: a cut-out handed along a row (bonds from YOU to each factory).
  - `strike`: a chalk line struck through a list item, one per beat.
