# Part 10 storyboard: "The Coordination Problem"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/aligning-incentives.qmd`, "Why This Can Work: The Coordination Problem Has a Price Tag" (to "has never been wrong"); the bonds are `knowledge/solution/incentive-alignment-bonds.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `concentrated_interest_sector_market_cap_usd`: $5 trillion (the military industry and fossil fuels).
  - `global_household_wealth_usd`: $454 trillion. Derived: 454 / 5 = 90.8, the narrator's "ninety times" and "ninety to one".
  - Prose, not parameters: $100 million to $1 billion a year on political influence against approximately $0; ten lobbyists making $10 million each against ten million patients losing $10 each. Derived for the picture: each side has $100 million at stake (10 × $10 million = 10,000,000 × $10).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** a few people with a lot to gain beat millions with a little to lose, even when the millions are far richer, and the bonds fix that by putting investors' money, which pays only if cures come, on the patients' side.
- **Plates:**

  - `two-mountains`: a two-bar chart, "MONEY", whose bars are stacks of bills big enough to stand on; a second, smaller chart beside it, "SPENT ON POLITICAL INFLUENCE".
