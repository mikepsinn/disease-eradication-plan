# Part 7 storyboard: "Volume Over Blockbusters"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/aligning-incentives.qmd`, the end of "The Math That Converts Them", "Pharma's New Business Model: Volume Over Blockbusters" and "How to Get Them Onboard".
- **The numbers:**
  - `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` $41,000 per patient; `dfda_pragmatic_trial_cost_per_patient` $929 per patient; `dfda_trial_cost_reduction_pct` 97.7%.
  - `pharma_drug_development_cost_current` $2.6 billion per attempt; `dfda_trial_capacity_multiplier` 12.3 times the capacity.
  - Prose, not parameters: 90% of drugs fail; 10 to 17 years against 2 to 3 years; $1 billion-plus blockbusters only; about 10 attempts a year; $500 million per success becomes profitable; thousands of attempts; 7 years for smartphones, 12 for social media.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** when a trial costs $929 a patient, failing is cheap, so companies try thousands of drugs instead of ten, rare diseases get studied, and the industry follows the money in order.
- **Plates:**
  - `two-lanes`: a two-lane race track on a plain field, "CURRENT" and "DECENTRALIZED", a calendar post at the finish.
  - Host scenes (host-definition, host-adoption): Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
  - `shooting-gallery`: a carnival shooting gallery on a plain field, a row of germ targets on a rail.
  - `adoption-curve`: an S-shaped line chart, "ADOPTION", big enough to climb, a billboard at its top.
- **Cast:** existing: `train` (big and gilded, the CURRENT trial engine), `motorcar` (the DECENTRALIZED trial cart), `fax-clerk`, `auditor`, `doctor`, `germ`, `germ-giant`, `top-hat`, `money-bundle`, `calendar`, `pharma-proud` (new in Part 1), `pharma-sweat` (new in Part 6), `factory` (plain, GENERIC on its sign), `scientist`, `flask`, `book`, `bill-med`, `tv-big`, `scoreboard`, `glowing-rectangle`, `lab`, `citizen`. - **Callbacks:** the motorcar from the race putts across Wishonia's console in host-definition. Episode 1's drug ads are realistic; this one is the billboard that turns into an outcome scoreboard.
- **New art:** the shooting gallery with its two guns; a garage biotech. The germ targets are episode 1's germs, the rare ones in top hats, flipping down when hit.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `race`: two cut-outs along parallel tracks at different speeds, with a calendar at the finish of each.
  - `stretch`: a plate that widens sideways by a factor, its repeated pieces filling in (the gallery, 12.3 times).
