# Part 2 storyboard: "The Waiting List"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/health-dividend.qmd`, "The Waiting List"; it links `knowledge/problem/untapped-therapeutic-frontier.qmd` (the queue) and `knowledge/appendix/dfda-impact-paper.qmd` (the 204 and 212 years).
- **The numbers:**
  - `diseases_without_effective_treatment` 6,650 diseases; `new_disease_first_treatments_per_year` 15 a year; `status_quo_queue_clearance_years` 443 years. Derived: from 2026, the line clears in about 2469.
  - Derived: 443 years before 2026 is 1583. Shakespeare's plays date from about 1590 and Galileo's first telescope from 1609, so "for context" is approximate (spoken, not parameters).
  - `dfda_trial_capacity_multiplier` 12.3×; `dfda_first_treatments_per_year` 185 a year; `dfda_queue_clearance_years` 36 years. Derived: done by about 2062. On the plate, 12.3 is the window plus eleven trial doors and a cat flap.
  - `dfda_trial_capacity_treatment_acceleration_years` 204 years earlier for the average disease (`status_quo_avg_years_to_first_treatment` 222 years falls to about 18).
  - `efficacy_lag_years` 8.2 years between proving a drug safe and letting patients take it.
  - `dfda_trial_capacity_plus_efficacy_lag_years` 212 years (204 + 8.2).
  - Spoken, not parameters: "split atoms in six years during a war" (the Manhattan Project, about 1939 to 1945); an elephant's pregnancy is about 22 months, so three in a row is about 5.5 years; the "seventy-eight-page chapter".
- **What the viewer must leave with:** at today's 15 a year the last untreated disease waits until about 2469; 12.3 times the trials makes it about 2062, and treatments reach people 212 years sooner.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-queue`: aged paper, a year ruler printed along the ground from 1583 at the far left, through 2026 at the FDA window in the middle, to 2469 at the far right. The window has a NOW SERVING board over it and a long blank wall to its right with room for eleven trial doors and a cat flap. A roped queue of humans, each walking a disease on a leash, runs right from the window to 2469. The camera travels along it. At the end the city of tomorrow rises along the whole ruler.
  - `the-bedside`: a plain field; an atom and a tear-off calendar at the left; at the right a patient in an iron bed, with a SAFE bottle on a stand beyond a rope.
  - host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass, the receipt still printing.
- **Cast:** existing: `snail`, `fda-window`, `now-serving`, `ticket`, `leash-human` (and grey-bearded and skeleton versions), `germ`, `pill-bottle`, `trial-door` (the cat flap is one at three-tenths the height), `space-guard` (the human of 2469), `headstone`, `arrow-sign`, `boot`, `city-of-tomorrow`, `family-groceries`, `flying-car`, `grandma`, `city-pigeon`, `you`, `binder`, `calculator` (host prop), `patient`, `patient-skeleton`, `iron-bed`, `atom`, `mushroom`, `calendar`, `fda-sip`, `hourglass`.
