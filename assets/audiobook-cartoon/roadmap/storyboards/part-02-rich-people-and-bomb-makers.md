# Part 2 storyboard: "Rich People and Bomb Makers"

- **Source:** the chapter `roadmap` (narration script `roadmap.prepared.txt`; the page `knowledge/strategy/roadmap.qmd` was removed in commit 8aa6b135b), "The Sacred (Legal) Bribery Sequence", "(Legal) Bribe Category number one: Rich People" and "The Specific Offer to Bomb Makers".
- **The numbers:**
  - `victory_bond_annual_return_pct`: 272% a year, projected (`victory_bond_annual_payout` $2.72 billion a year over `treaty_campaign_total_cost` $1 billion). On screen it is always "PROJECTED".
  - World War II war bonds: 4% (the book's text, cited `wwii-war-bonds`; not a parameter). Derived: 272% is 68 times 4%, so if the 4% bar is 12 px, the 272% bar is 816 px, most of the frame.
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1%, the share of revenue the weapons makers lose.
  - `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year, the new health market.
  - Operating margin 8%: the narrator's figure, not a parameter (the book's `defense_net_profit_margin` is 4.99%, a net margin). The screen uses the narrator's 8%.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** appeals to morality and logic fail, so you pay; rich humans get a projected 272% (war bonds paid 4%) and their money back if the treaty fails, and the weapons makers lose 1% but get offered a new $27.2 billion-a-year market whose customers survive.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-holdout`: a plain field, one stubborn human in the middle (papers-work).
  - `money-hill`: the fat cat's hill of plain bills on a green mound (from episode 1, Part 5), with the RETURNS chart and the escrow jar on the flat ground at its foot (several-lifetimes, war-bonds-backwards, money-back).
  - `factory-yard`: the weapons factory and its conveyor, its revenue as a stack of war bills beside it, an empty lot with a tumbleweed, a patient's table and a little island at the conveyor's far end, and room for a spreadsheet on an easel (new-market, offer-to-bomb-makers).
  - The title card and the host scene (Wishonia at her console, Earth in the glass) are not plates.
- **Cast:** existing names: `citizen`, `crowd`, `moral-human`, `halo`, `economist`, `chalkboard`, `bill-plain`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `pull-down-chart`, `fat-cat`, `fat-cat-chair`, `fat-cat-skeleton`, `money-hill`, `grandma-skeleton`, `war-bond-poster`, `ledger`, `scroll-rolled`, `jar`, `reaper`, `germ`, `you`, `factory-proud`, `factory-sob`, `factory-kiss`, `bundle-war`, `scissors`, `tumbleweed`, `farm-stand`, `conveyor`, `missile`, `mushroom`, `patient`, `patient-well`, `island`, `skeleton`, `receipt`, `gold-star`, `spreadsheet`, `telephone`, `signpost`. New: `pacemaker`.
- **Callbacks:** episode 1 Part 5's pull-down chart and the fat cat's money hill. Episode 1 Part 17's Grandma and her war bond (she died in 1987, so her skeleton holds the poster). The weapons factory kisses the spreadsheet as it kissed its missile. The saint's halo from papers-work lands on the factory's roof in offer-to-bomb-makers. Set up for this chapter: the health stall comes back in Part 6; the feedback slips in Part 3.
- **New art:** a 1950s pacemaker in a little box. The dangling bill hangs on a drawn line; the chart, the spreadsheet's cells and the stars on the feedback slips (`receipt` with `gold-star`s) are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `xray`: the white flash and swap to a skeleton (as in episode 1).
  - `chart`: bars (as in episode 1).
  - `march`: copies of one cut-out walking a path in single file (the conga line, the feedback queue).
