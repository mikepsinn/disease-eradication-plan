# Part 7 storyboard: "The Endgame"

- **Source:** the chapter `roadmap` (narration script `roadmap.prepared.txt`; its page was removed in commit 8aa6b135b), "Here's Why It Keeps Growing" and "The Endgame", to the end of the chapter (the podcast outro after it is not in this script).
- **The numbers:**
  - `victory_bond_funding_pct`: 10% of all treaty funds to bond investors. `victory_bond_annual_return_pct`: 272% a year at 1%, projected. Derived: 544% at 2% ("it doubles"), 2,720% at 10% ("ten times their original payout"). Drawn to scale: if the 272% bar is 60 px, the 544% bar is 120 px and the 2,720% bar is 600 px.
  - `iab_political_incentive_funding_annual`: $2.72 billion a year at 1%; at 10%, $27.2 billion a year (the narrator's "Stage four" pool). "All current global lobbying combined" has no parameter (the nearest are `us_total_lobbying_annual` $4.4 billion, US only, and `defense_lobbying_annual` $198 million), so its bar carries no number and only has to be shorter.
  - The endgame (by 2075, at 50% of `global_military_spending_annual_2024` $2.72 trillion): $1.36 trillion a year or more to health; 10% of it (`iab_political_incentive_funding_pct`), $136 billion or more, to political incentives; 80% of it (`dih_treasury_medical_research_pct`), $1.088 trillion or more, to pragmatic clinical trials. The investors' 10% ($136 billion) is not said, so it is drawn but not labelled.
  - The year 2075 is the narration's.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** investors and politicians both earn more as the treaty grows, so they keep growing it without being asked; at 50% that is $1.36 trillion a year for health, more than a trillion of it for trials; and it should win, since diseases kill nearly everyone, but humans are humans.
- **Plates:**
  - `two-charts`: one long sheet of aged paper with two charts the camera travels between: "BOND RETURNS · PROJECTED" (three bars, the fat cat in his swivel chair beside it with a rotary telephone) and "ALL CURRENT GLOBAL LOBBYING" against "POLITICAL INCENTIVE POOL" (the Politician at his podium beside it); along the bottom edge, the weapons factory and a hospital across a road (keeps-growing, most-profitable-sentence).
