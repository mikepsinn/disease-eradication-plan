# Part 1 storyboard: "The Recipe"

- **Source:** the chapter `roadmap` (its page, `knowledge/strategy/roadmap.qmd`, was removed from the book in commit 8aa6b135b; the audio was read from the narration script `roadmap.prepared.txt`), from the chapter title to the end of "Step Three". Numbers from `_variables.yml`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `military_to_government_clinical_trials_spending_ratio`: 604 (the narrator's "six hundred and four times more on weapons than testing which medicines work"). Drawn to scale: if the medicine bar is one flask as tall as YOU's shoe (about 40 px), the weapons bar is 24,160 px, about 22 frames high, so the camera tilts up a long way and never shows the top in one frame.
  - `three_point_five_percent_of_global_population`: 280 million people, which is `global_population_activism_threshold_pct` 3.5% of `global_population_2024` 8 billion.
  - `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year, which is `treaty_reduction_pct` 1% of `global_military_spending_annual_2024` $2.72 trillion.
  - The bond's return (`victory_bond_annual_return_pct`, 272%) is not said in this part, so the bond here reads only "MORE PAPERS · PROJECTED". The narrator says 272% in Part 2.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the plan is a recipe with three steps (greedy investors, 280 million paid clicks, campaign money for politicians), and the result is $27.2 billion a year moving from weapons to medicine for good.
- **Plates:**
  - `kitchen`: a 1950s kitchen counter on aged paper, one mixing bowl as tall as half the frame, a recipe card on a stand, and a window over the sink with the weapons factory across the street (not-a-protest, step-one-rich-people).
  - `604-tower`: a plain field; YOU at the foot of a two-bar chart, "SPENT ON WEAPONS" and "SPENT TESTING WHICH MEDICINES WORK" (humans-love-steps).
  - `slider-globe`: the engraved globe on its stand, big, on a plain field, the sun on a brass arm round it, the Capitol and the funnel at its foot (step-two-referendum, step-three-treaty).
  - The title card and the host scenes (Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass) are not plates.
- **Cast:** existing names: `you`, `hard-hat`, `fat-cat`, `factory-proud`, `factory-pay`, `politician`, `super-pac`, `reaper`, `germ`, `citizen` (the protester, and the tiny voters), `sign-board` (his placard), `loud-human`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `bill-plain`, `bond`, `treaty-scroll`, `globe`, `sun`, `capitol`, `gauge`, `needle`, `funnel`, `calendar`, `missile`, `flask`, `slider-rail`, `slider-knob`, `yes-button`, `loaf`, `lab`, `trapdoor`, `desk`, `mushroom`, `hospital`. New: `mixing-bowl`, `recipe-card`, `tinfoil-man`, `monk-astronomer`.
- **Callbacks:** episode 1 Part 2's loaf (the economy was a machine for getting one loaf of bread; this recipe makes bread too). Episode 1 Part 5's Loud Humans with megaphone heads run the marketing campaign. The orrery sun that clonks the astronomer is the sun that explodes in step-three-treaty. Set up for this chapter: the protester comes back in Parts 3 and 5, the kitchen and the recipe card in Parts 3 and 5, the funnel and the sun in Part 7, the globe in Part 3.

  - `pour`: many small copies of a cut-out flowing into one place (the voters into the bowl, the sliders into the giant slider).
  - `rise`: a shape that swells over time (the dough).
