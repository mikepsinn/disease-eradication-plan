# Part 3 storyboard: "True Believers and Regular People"

- **Source:** the chapter `roadmap` (narration script `roadmap.prepared.txt`; its page was removed in commit 8aa6b135b), "(Legal) Bribe Category number two: The True Believers" and "(Legal) Bribe Category Number Three: Regular People".
- **The numbers:**
  - `campaign_phase1_budget`: $200 million to start the referendum.
  - `treaty_campaign_viral_referendum_base_case`: $250 million, the whole referendum. Derived: the last $50 million comes from the people who jump on.
  - `victory_bond_annual_return_pct`: 272% a year, projected.
  - `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year (the pie). `victory_bond_funding_pct` 10% of it is the investors' slice, `victory_bond_annual_payout` $2.72 billion a year ("at one percent, bondholders split two point seven two billion dollars"). Derived: at 5%, five times that, $13.6 billion a year.
  - `iab_political_incentive_funding_pct`: 10%, the politicians' allocation, which scales the same way.
  - `three_point_five_percent_of_global_population`: 280 million people, `global_population_activism_threshold_pct` 3.5% of `global_population_2024` 8 billion. Drawn as a pictogram of 200 dots, 7 of them lit.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** early investors start the referendum and get projected 272% returns that rise as the treaty grows, so they defend and expand it for free; 280 million voters get referral pay and a stake in it, which makes them dangerous to anyone who tries to take it back.
- **Plates:**
  - `slider-globe`: Part 1's globe on its stand, with a muddy road running up to it, the Politician's desk and the lab at its foot (true-believers, the-bandwagon, what-they-fund, critical-mass, pyramid-scheme).
  - `kitchen`: Part 1's kitchen counter (first-in-line).
  - `treaty-plinth`: the treaty scroll on a stone plinth on a plain field, a brass dial on the plinth, "TREATY %" (influence-comes-free).
  - The host scenes (Wishonia at her console, Earth in the glass) are not plates.
- **Cast:** existing names: `you`, `citizen`, `crowd`, `politician`, `super-pac`, `armchair` (with its FELLOWSHIP plaque), `bond`, `bill-plain`, `bill-med`, `bundle-med`, `treaty-scroll`, `globe`, `capitol`, `newspaper`, `tv-set`, `pencil` (the giant eraser end), `price-tag`, `gauge`, `needle`, `mushroom`, `patient`, `patient-well`, `reaper`, `germ`, `lab`, `desk`, `hoop`, `yes-button`, `podium`, `push-pin`, `egg-timer`, `slider-rail`, `slider-knob`, `thought-bubble`, `heart`, `receipt`, `sign-board`. New: `believer` (an earnest man in a yachting blazer and captain's cap, with a CURE pennant; with a bib and fork; with marionette strings on his fingers), `yacht` (one model yacht, scaled up in code to an ocean liner), `bandwagon`, `pie`, `toupee`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 1's kitchen, its slider-globe and its protester (here as the man who gets the paper cut). Episode 1 Part 18's referral pyramid ends on a living patient instead of a billionaire. Set up for this chapter: the treaty plinth's dial returns in Part 7; the believer and his yacht in Parts 4 and 7.
- **New art:** the believer (three poses as in the Cast); a model yacht; a circus bandwagon with a brass band on top and wheels stuck in mud; a giant pie with a crust of medicine bills, and one wedge; a toupee. The specimen label, the pictogram grid and the ballots are code.
- **Kit additions:**

  - `strings`: lines from a hand to several cut-outs that tug them when the hand moves.
  - `grow`: a cut-out scaled up in steps as a dial turns.
