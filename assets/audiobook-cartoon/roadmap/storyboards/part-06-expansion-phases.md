# Part 6 storyboard: "The Expansion Phases"

- **Source:** the chapter `roadmap` (narration script `roadmap.prepared.txt`; its page was removed in commit 8aa6b135b), "Phase four plus: The Expansion Phases" and "Why Expansion Is Built Into The System".
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_military_spending_annual_2024`: $2.72 trillion a year, the pie.
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1%, the first dose.
  - The split: `dih_treasury_medical_research_pct` 80% to clinical trials, `victory_bond_funding_pct` 10% to investors, `iab_political_incentive_funding_pct` 10% to political incentives.
  - The political incentive pool: `iab_political_incentive_funding_annual` $2.72 billion a year at 1% (10% of `treaty_annual_funding` $27.2 billion). Derived, each 10% of the treaty share of $2.72 trillion: $5.44 billion at 2%, $13.6 billion at 5%, $27.2 billion at 10%, $68 billion at 25%, $136 billion at 50%. Drawn to scale: if the 50% bar is 800 px tall, the 1% bar is 16 px, the 2% bar 32 px, the 5% bar 80 px, the 10% bar 160 px and the 25% bar 400 px.
  - The phases' years (1 to 3, 4 to 7, 8 to 12, 13 to 20, 21 to 35, 36 to 50) are the narration's, not parameters; they label the bars.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the 1% is a first dose; the politicians' slice grows every time the treaty grows, from $2.72 billion a year to $136 billion, so they keep expanding it until most military spending goes to health.
- **Plates:**
  - `military-pie`: one pie chart as tall as the frame on a tripod, "WORLD MILITARY SPENDING · $2.72 TRILLION A YEAR" (small-dose).
  - `the-split`: Part 4's banknote cut into ten strips, with its two bell jars (eighty-ten-ten).
  - `pool-chart`: one bar chart on aged paper, "POLITICAL INCENTIVE POOL · PER YEAR", six bars labelled by phase, percent and years. Behind the chart, a skyline that starts as smoking weapons factories and turns, bar by bar, into the 1950s city of tomorrow (phases-three-and-four).
