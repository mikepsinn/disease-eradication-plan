# Part 4 storyboard: "Politicians and the Success Spiral"

- **Source:** the chapter `roadmap` (narration script `roadmap.prepared.txt`; its page was removed in commit 8aa6b135b), "(Legal) Bribe Category Number Four: Politicians", "The Package", "The choice you give them" and "The Success Spiral".
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_campaign_budget_lobbying`: $650 million, the lobbying budget behind the Super PAC.
  - `three_point_five_percent_of_global_population`: 280 million voters.
  - `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year, the "one percent Treaty Fund" (`treaty_reduction_pct` 1%).
  - The split: `dih_treasury_medical_research_pct` 80% to clinical trials, `victory_bond_funding_pct` 10% to investors, `iab_political_incentive_funding_pct` 10% to political incentives. Derived: the capped share is 10% + 10% = 20%. On the banknote, the 80% is eight strips and each 10% is one strip.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** politicians follow reelection, so you offer more of it than the weapons makers do (campaign money, voters, jobs after office, clinics for their districts); each reward creates the next group's, and the payouts are capped at a visible 20%, apart from the 80% for trials.
- **Plates:**
  - `dog-street`: one plain street: the weapons factory and its garbage can at one end, the insurance company's building at the other (simple-organisms).
  - `gift-box`: a plain floor with one huge gift box, and behind it two doors, A and B, and a podium with bunting (the-package, the-choice).
  - `the-split`: one big sheet of aged paper: on the left, the Capitol wound in a tangle of string; on the right, one banknote as wide as half the frame, "$27.2 BILLION A YEAR", ruled into ten strips, with the success spiral inked across its face and two glass bell jars beside it (success-spiral, disentangled).
  - The host scene (Wishonia at her console, Earth in the glass) is not a plate.
