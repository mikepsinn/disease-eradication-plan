# Part 5 storyboard: "The Timeline and the Legal Machine"

- **Source:** the chapter `roadmap` (narration script `roadmap.prepared.txt`; its page was removed in commit 8aa6b135b), "The Timeline: From zero dollars to Ending Death" (Phases one to three and the feasibility check) and "The Legal Architecture".
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_campaign_total_cost`: $1 billion, the whole campaign, raised by the end of Phase three. The thermometer's scale runs $0 to $1,000,000,000.
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1% of the military budget, the referendum's question.
  - The phases' months (1 to 3, 4 to 18, 19 to 36) are the narration's, not parameters. Drawn along the thermometer's base as a 36-month timeline.
  - The feasibility check gives no number for the diplomatic phase ("much cheaper"), so its bar carries none.

  - `workbench`: a workbench on a plain field where YOU builds the robot; the lawyers' board game spread on it first; three crowds round it (three-fronts, four-part-machine).
  - The host scene (Wishonia at her console, Earth in the glass) is not a plate.
