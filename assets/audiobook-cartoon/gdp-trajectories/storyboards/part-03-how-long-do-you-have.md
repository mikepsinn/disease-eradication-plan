- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd`, "So How Long Do You Have?" and "You Already Know What This Looks Like" up to "there is no Colombia". Narration lines 41 to 63.
- **The numbers:**
  - `destructive_economy_25pct_year`: 2033 (the narrator's "in eight years"); `destructive_economy_50pct_year`: 2040 ("fifty percent in fifteen years").
  - `global_destructive_economy_pct_gdp`: 11.5% today. On the same curve it crosses 15% in about 2028 ("within a decade").

  - None of the country figures are parameters; they are cited in the chapter.
