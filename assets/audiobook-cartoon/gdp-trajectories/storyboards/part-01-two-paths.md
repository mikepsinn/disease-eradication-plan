# Part 1 storyboard: "Two Paths: G.D.P. Trajectories"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd`, the opening and "Your Current Trajectory" up to the nuclear arsenals. Narration: `assets/audiobook/manual-paperback/narration-txt-chapters/gdp-trajectories.prepared.txt`, lines 1 to 19.
- **The numbers:**
  - `gdp_baseline_growth_rate`: 2.5% a year, the economists' forecast (spoken).
  - `global_destructive_economy_pct_gdp`: 11.5% of GDP (military plus cybercrime, `global_destructive_economy_annual_2025` $13.2 trillion of `global_gdp_2025` $115 trillion).
  - The Soviet Union: 15 to 18% of GDP on the military before it collapsed (cited in the chapter, not a parameter).
  - Treaty-only average income at year 20: the narrator says $339,000. `treaty_trajectory_avg_income_year_20` now reads $34,972; the audio's figure goes on screen.
  - "Eight years and fifteen years": `destructive_economy_25pct_year` 2033 (25% of GDP, 8 years from 2025) and `destructive_economy_50pct_year` 2040 (50%, 15 years, where destroying passes making).
  - Military spending: `global_military_spending_annual_2024` $2.72 trillion; up 9.4% in 2024, the tenth rise in a row (SIPRI, cited, not a parameter); `global_military_spending_real_cagr_10yr` 3.4% a year.
  - For the dice: twenty year ticks, 2025 to 2045, one die each.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the 2.5% forecast is a best case that needs twenty lucky years, the destructive economy is already 11.5% and growing faster, and military spending has gone up ten years running.

  - `military-stairs`: a bar chart, "GLOBAL MILITARY SPENDING", ten bars 2015 to 2024, each a stack of plain missiles, the last one much taller.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass. The theme song is the existing music video.
