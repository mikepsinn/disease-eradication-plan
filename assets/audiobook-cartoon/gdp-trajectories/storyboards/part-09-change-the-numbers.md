# Part 9 storyboard: "Go Ahead, Change the Numbers"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd`, the end of "What This Means Per Person" and "Go Ahead, Change the Numbers". Narration lines 162 to 175, then the podcast outro (`knowledge/appendix/podcast-outro.qmd`), the same words as episode 1's Part 27.
- **The numbers:**
  - `wishonia_disease_cure_fraction_20yr_full`: 100% (from trial capacity, `dfda_max_trial_capacity_multiplier_physical` 566×, up to the physical ceiling of willing participants).
  - `rd_spillover_multiplier`: 2×.
  - Halves and quarters: 100% → 50% → 25%; 2× → 1× → 0.5× (the narrator's "cut them all in half, cut them in quarters"; the dials show the cuts, not new results).
  - Three compounding mechanisms (research spillover, cured diseases, governance) for twenty years.
  - The outro's `voter_lives_saved` 2.6 and `voter_suffering_hours_prevented` 468,000, as in episode 1.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** every input is public and can be changed; even cut to a quarter, the three mechanisms compounding together still beat collapse, and the choice comes down to preferring to be alive.
- **Plates:**
  - `two-paths-chart` (from Part 1), with a brass control panel of named dials standing beside it.
  - `two-doors`: a plain wall with two doors, ALIVE and DEAD, and a question card on a board.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass. The outro is episode 1's Part 27 scenes.
