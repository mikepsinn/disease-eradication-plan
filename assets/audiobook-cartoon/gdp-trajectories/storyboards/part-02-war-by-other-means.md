- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/gdp-trajectories.qmd`, "Your Current Trajectory", from "War funds cybercrime" to the weight-loss line. Narration lines 21 to 39.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_cybercrime_cost_annual_2025`: $10.5 trillion a year; $6 trillion in 2021 (Cybersecurity Ventures, cited, not a parameter).
  - `global_cybercrime_cagr`: 15% a year. Derived: 10.5 × 1.15^10 ≈ $42 trillion by 2035, the narrator's "forty-two trillion dollars within a decade".
