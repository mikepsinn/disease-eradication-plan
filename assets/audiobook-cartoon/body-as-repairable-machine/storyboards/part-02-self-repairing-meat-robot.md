# Part 2 storyboard: "You Are a Self-Repairing Meat Robot"

- **Source:** `knowledge/proof/body-as-repairable-machine.qmd`, "You Are a Self-Repairing Meat Robot". Its figures are cited (`human-genome-and-genetic-editing`, `cellular-turnover-rates`); none is a `_variables.yml` parameter.
- **The numbers:**
  - DNA: 3 billion letters, cited (`human-genome-and-genetic-editing`), not a parameter. Stamped as 3,000,000,000 LETTERS.
  - 330 billion cells replaced a day, cited (`cellular-turnover-rates`), not a parameter. On the board as 330,000,000,000.
  - 10,000 DNA repairs per cell per day, and 37 trillion cells, cited (`cellular-turnover-rates`), not parameters. Derived, as the narrator says: 10,000 × 37,000,000,000,000 = 370,000,000,000,000,000 (370 quadrillion) repairs a day.
  - The whole skeleton rebuilt every 10 years, cited (`cellular-turnover-rates`), not a parameter.
  - The Milky Way has roughly 100 to 400 billion stars (not narrated, not a parameter): drawn as a spiral a little smaller than the cast-off cells' galaxy, with no number.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the body already rebuilds and repairs itself at a scale no factory matches; aging is that repair falling behind, so it is maintenance, not fate.
- **Plates:**
  - `industrial-palace`: YOU as Fritz Kahn's "Man as Industrial Palace", his cutaway torso a factory of floors, pipes and conveyors full of tiny molecule workers; a control room in his head; a night sky out of his back.
  - `meat-river`: a river on a plain field, drawn first as a stream of dots, that turns out to run through YOU's outline and off across the paper.
