# Part 9 storyboard: "Nothing to Capture"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dih.qmd`, "What Your Decentralized Institutes of Health Outsources (Everything)" and "Anti-Capture Design".
- **The numbers:**
  - Spoken, not parameters: the word "accountability" is over four hundred years old; a committee of twelve against twelve million voters.
