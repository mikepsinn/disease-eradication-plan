# Part 8 storyboard: "How the Money Flows"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dih.qmd`, "How the Money Flows" ("The Architecture", "The Fund Flow" and its table) and "What Gets Funded: Market Failures Only"; `knowledge/solution/incentive-alignment-bonds.qmd` for the two 10% strips.
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year redirected from military budgets (1% of `global_military_spending_annual_2024`, $2.72 trillion).
  - `victory_bond_funding_pct` 10%, `victory_bond_annual_payout` $2.72 billion a year, to bondholders.
  - `iab_political_incentive_funding_pct` 10%, `iab_political_incentive_funding_annual` $2.72 billion a year, to political incentives (independent expenditures and PACs, post-office fellowships, the Public Good Score).
  - `dih_treasury_medical_research_pct` 80%, `dih_treasury_to_medical_research_annual` $21.8 billion a year, the research treasury.
  - Inside the research treasury (book text, not parameters): about 97% to D.A.L.Y.-weighted patient subsidies and about 3% to infrastructure. On the banknote, the eight research strips are ruled into 100 slivers, 97 and 3.
  - Spoken, not a parameter: Olson described the problem "decades ago" (*The Logic of Collective Action*, 1965).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the fund has no boss to corrupt; of every treaty dollar, 10 cents rewards investors, 10 cents rewards the politics that keep the treaty, and 80 cents goes to research, almost all of it following patients into trials and a sliver building the shared pipes nobody else will pay for.
- **Money rules on screen:** the political 10% goes into the Super PAC, labelled CAMPAIGN FUNDING, never into a politician's hands; the bondholder's return is labelled PROJECTED.
- **Plates:**
  - title cards: "HOW THE MONEY FLOWS"; "THE ARCHITECTURE"; "THE FUND FLOW"; "WHAT GETS FUNDED: MARKET FAILURES ONLY".
  - `vault`: the glass vault on a plain field, a door beside it marked BOARDROOM onto a long table with no chairs, and the vault's outlet pipe with a valve marked BUDGET.
  - `the-fork`: one engraved banknote as wide as the frame, "$27.2 BILLION A YEAR", ruled into ten strips, on a plain field: the bond counter and the Super PAC on the right, the patients' trial doors on the left, and bare ground beyond the doors where the pipes get laid, ending at Olson's chalkboard.

## Scene 6 · pipes

- id: pipes
- act: Market failures only

- cue: "Most research allocation will happen automatically."

- voiceover: "Most research allocation will happen automatically. Patients will choose trials, funding will flow there, and the market will handle it the way markets handle most things: imperfectly but vigorously. The one percent Treaty Fund will step in only for the things markets genuinely can't do. Infrastructure includes development and operations, competing alternative implementations, data commons storage and processing, security audits and fraud detection. These are the pipes and plumbing. Nobody builds pipes for fun. Markets don't produce pipes unless someone pays for them. So the fund pays for them. True public goods include patient trial participation subsidies, negative results publishing, and replication studies.
