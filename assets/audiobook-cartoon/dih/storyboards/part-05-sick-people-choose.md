# Part 5 storyboard: "Sick People Choose"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dih.qmd`, "Governance: Sick People Choose, Everyone Else Votes" and "Making Selfishness Cure Disease"; `knowledge/solution/wishocracy.qmd` for the pairwise ranking.
- **The numbers:**
  - Spoken, not parameters: two allocation mechanisms; subsidies weighted by D.A.L.Y.s (disability-adjusted life years) per patient.
  - The research treasury's split (about 97% to D.A.L.Y.-weighted patient subsidies, about 3% to infrastructure) is said in Part 8, not here.
  - For the picture: the bill that follows each patient is sized by how severe the disease is: a bus-sized bundle behind a patient on a gurney, one coin behind a sniffle.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the people who will die if research fails choose where research money goes, by joining trials, and the money is bigger for worse diseases; everyone ranks the shared infrastructure two options at a time; and every actor is paid to do the useful thing.
- **Plates:**
  - title cards: "GOVERNANCE: SICK PEOPLE CHOOSE, EVERYONE ELSE VOTES"; "MAKING SELFISHNESS CURE DISEASE".
  - `trial-hall`: a long hallway of trial doors, the committee's long table across the hall from them; at the far end of the hall a crowd, two signs on posts and a ranking ladder that grows.
  - `help-wanted`: a lab door on a plain field, a telephone on a post, a bed; behind the door, a cancer germ the size of a car with four actors round it that the camera circles one at a time.
  - Host scenes (host-two, dig-site): Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the window.
- **Cast:** existing: `patient` (in a bathrobe, and on a gurney), `orderly`, `trial-door`, `bill-med`, `money-bundle`, `coin`, `committee`, `citizen` (the woman with the sniffle; the crowd), `sign-board`, `yes-button`, `politician` (red and blue), `moral-human`, `halo`, `telephone`, `calendar`, `fat-cat`, `fat-cat-chair`, `iron-bed`, `scientist`, `flask`, `germ`, `jar`, `price-tag`, `mainframe`, `ticket` (the punch cards), `skeleton`, `crate`, `city-of-tomorrow`, `ballot-box`, `thermometer`. From Parts 1 and 3: `protocol-sheet`, `binder` (the menus). New: `ranking-ladder`, `alarm-clock`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 3's trial doors and the bills that trot after patients like ducklings. Part 14's grant committee, whose catalogue of fashionable diseases this committee's coin toss replaces. Part 1's menus are the restaurant ladder's rungs. Altruism's statue, toppled, is the ruin in dig-site.
- **New art:**
  - `ranking-ladder`: a tall ladder whose rungs are cards; it grows rung by rung.
  - `alarm-clock`: a twin-bell alarm clock with no snooze button, only a solid brass top.
- **Kit additions:** `duckling` (a small cut-out follows a walker at a fixed gap), a ladder that grows a rung at a time.
