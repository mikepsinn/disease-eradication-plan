# Part 4 storyboard: "Pay for Results, Publish Everything"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/dih.qmd`, "Pay for Results (A Concept Your Research Sector Has Somehow Avoided)" and "Your Data Commons: Publish Everything".
- **The numbers:**
  - Spoken, not parameters: "billions" wasted repeating mistakes; one hundred percent open publication as a condition of funding.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** money goes only to work that delivers and keeps going only while it delivers; every result, good or bad, is published, so nobody pays twice for the same mistake.
- **Plates:**
  - title cards: "PAY FOR RESULTS (A CONCEPT YOUR RESEARCH SECTOR HAS SOMEHOW AVOIDED)"; "YOUR DATA COMMONS: PUBLISH EVERYTHING".
  - `pay-line`: the protocol's pay window on a plain field with a queue in front of it; a trapdoor in the ground beside it. At the far end, the N.I.H. building's own pay window.
  - `the-pit`: a plain field with one hole in it, and a trophy wall at the side.
  - `ultra-line`: a line of generals at a map table, then a line of scientists.
- **Cast:** existing: `scientist`, `patient`, `patient-skeleton`, `iron-bed`, `trapdoor`, `committee`, `builder`, `house`, `farmer`, `loaf`, `bill-med`, `money-bundle`, `germ`, `skeleton`, `robot`, `magnifier`, `pins-and-string`, `tarp`, `case-file` (the FAILED folder, its stamp in code), `ticket` (the ULTRA slip, in code), `eisenhower`, `general`, `war-map`, `tank`, `citizen`, `sign-board`, `teacup`. From Parts 1 and 3: `protocol-sheet`, `nih-building`, `pharma-building`, `binder` (grant applications), `pay-window`. New: `pit`, `noticeboard`, `trophy-wall`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 3's three trays: tray 3, the pay window, is this part's plate. Part 1's N.I.H. pay window, which paid the man with the application, runs backwards again in results-window.
- **New art:**
  - `pit`: a round hole in the ground with dirt edges, seen from the side so the camera can look in.
  - `noticeboard`: a vast public noticeboard on posts, sheets pinned all over it, green and red tabs.
  - `trophy-wall`: a wall of gilt frames holding triumphs (a vaccine vial, a moon rocket, a smiling surgeon), polished to a shine.
- **Kit additions:** `queue` (a line that steps forward one place at a time).
