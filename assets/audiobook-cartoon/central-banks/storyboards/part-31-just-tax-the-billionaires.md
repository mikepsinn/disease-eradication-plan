# Part 31 storyboard: "Just Tax the Billionaires"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "Just Tax the Billionaires" (links `knowledge/problem/unrepresentative-democracy.qmd` for the lobbying return and the 0% correlation). The narration is `central-banks.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `us_gov_waste_corporate_welfare`: $181 billion a year in direct corporate welfare.
  - `us_gov_waste_fossil_fuel_subsidies`: $50 billion a year.
  - `us_gov_waste_agricultural_subsidies`: $75 billion a year (the top 10% of farms collect 63% of payments; hardcoded).
  - `global_government_clinical_trials_spending_annual`: $4.5 billion a year.
  - `nih_clinical_trials_spending_pct`: 3.3% of the NIH budget goes to clinical trials.
  - Hardcoded in the chapter, not parameters: lobbyists return $18 for every $1; "over $300 billion" a year flows back ($181 + $50 + $75 = $306 billion); "sixty-seven years of clinical trials" ($300 billion / $4.5 billion = 66.7); "roughly sixty-eight to one" ($306 billion / $4.5 billion = 68); 0% correlation with what voters want; "a century", "one hundred years".
- **What the viewer must leave with:** taxes go into the same captured system that hands them back to the wealthy, so the fix is a new pipe for the money, not a bigger tax.
- **Plates:**

  - `ratio-chart` (from Part 28): two bars, one bottle against columns of coins.
  - `the-pipe`: one long brass pipe on a plain field from a tax funnel to the NIH building, with YOU at the funnel.

- **Callbacks:** Part 2's money loop is steal-and-beat: there, one machine for a loaf of bread; here, one road for a tax that comes home. Part 2's family gets the inflation bill. The germs from Part 29 are still queueing. Part 28's PROGRESS flag goes round the loop.
