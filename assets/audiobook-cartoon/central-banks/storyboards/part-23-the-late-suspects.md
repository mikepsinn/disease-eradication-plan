# Part 23 storyboard: "The Late Suspects"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "But It Wasn't the Gold Standard, It Was..." (excuses two to five). The narration is `central-banks.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The narrator's dates, hardcoded in the chapter:
  - The wage gap opened in 1971.
  - Globalization: NAFTA 1994, China joined the WTO 2001; "twenty-three years late" (1994 minus 1971).
  - Computers: Apple II 1977, IBM PC 1981, the commercial internet 1995.
  - Deregulation: Reagan took office 1981; the top marginal tax rate stayed at 70% until 1982; "a decade off".
  - Shareholder capitalism: Milton Friedman's essay 1970; the Dutch East India Company 1602; "raining for four hundred years" (1602 to now).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** every excuse is dated after the 1971 break, except greed for profit, which is 400 years older than the essay it is blamed on.

  - `voc-ship`: a 1600s Dutch East India Company galleon, VOC on its flag, every cannon port smoking.
