- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The Theft That Followed" and "The Two-Income Trap" (citation `bls-working-wives-1967-2011`).
- **The numbers:** none are `_variables.yml` parameters; the chapter states them inline.
  - Since 1972: productivity +246%, wages +115%; the gap the narrator names, 246 − 115 = 131 points.
  - 1967: fewer than half of married couples had both spouses working.
  - Husband-only earner families: 36% to 19% by 2011, as narrated (the book text says "from over half to just 19%"; the screen uses the narrator's 36%).
