# Part 6 storyboard: "The Receipt"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The Receipt", through "the U.S. share of six wars."
- **The numbers** (cited in the chapter, not parameters; the costs are US spending in 2024 dollars):
  - France (the Revolutionary and Napoleonic wars): 5 million dead; running total 5 million.
  - The Civil War: $450 million in greenbacks, $112 billion today; 750,000 dead; running total 6 million.
  - The First World War: $468 billion; 20 million dead; running total 26 million.
  - The Second World War: $5.7 trillion (US alone); 60 million dead; running total 86 million.
  - Korea: $478 billion; the 1951 Treasury-Fed Accord; 3 million dead; running total 89 million.
  - Vietnam: $1 trillion; 3 million dead; running total 92 million.
  - The post-9/11 wars: $8 trillion and counting; 4.5 million dead and counting; running total 97 million.
  - The total: 97 million dead; over $16 trillion, the US share of six wars. The book now sets this receipt beside `war_deaths_since_1900` = 310 million; the narrator's total, and the screen's, is 97 million.
  - For the pile: each line adds a heap in proportion to its dead, so the Second World War's heap is twelve times France's and the Civil War's is a pebble.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the wars paid for by printing are one bill, and it comes to 97 million dead.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-receipt`: the Fed building on a plain field, its paper tray printing a long till receipt that runs down and across the floor; beside it, a heap that grows into a mountain, and a RUNNING TOTAL board at the top.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `fed-crank-up`, `factory-pay`, `bill-war`, `bill-blank` (the IOUs), `receipt`, `tally-board`, `printing-press`, `gold-bar` (Part 1), `birthday-candle` (Part 4), `treaty-scroll` (also lettered ACCORD), `wheelbarrow` (Part 3), `hitler`, `binder`, `capitol`, `you`, `skeleton`. - **Callbacks:** Part 4's chain: the receipt's third line is that war, and its fourth is the war the chain ended in. Part 4's "4" candle on the building. Episode 1 Part 10's receipt was trial years; this one is the dead.
- **New art:**
  - `helmet-skeletons`: grinning skeletons in the headgear of each war (a bicorne, a kepi, a doughboy's helmet, a 1940s steel helmet, a Korean War parka hood, a jungle helmet, a desert helmet), sitting up out of paper.
  - `lbj`: Lyndon Johnson, caricatured: huge ears, big nose, cowboy hat; sitting.
  - The plate. The receipt's lines, prices, the running total and the stamps are code; the clippings and the stapler are host props.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `unroll`: the receipt paying out of the tray, line by line, with the camera following.
