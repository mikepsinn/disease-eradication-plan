# Part 25 storyboard: "Why You Don't Know Any of This"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "Why You Don't Know Any of This" and "Same Trick, Different Costume". The narration is `central-banks.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The narrator's figures, hardcoded in the chapter:
  - About 17,000 hours of formal education for an American with a bachelor's degree; hours on how the Federal Reserve creates money and who gets it first: 0.
  - The mitochondria: "an entire semester", then "six weeks" (the narrator says both; the chart uses SIX WEEKS, the last one said).
  - 93% of your purchasing power taken (the chapter's "93% pay cut", measured in gold since 1971). Derived: a dollar keeps 7% of its size.
  - 28 states require an economics course to graduate high school (of 50).
  - "Six thousand years ago" on Wishonia; a 47-page report; a 200-page PDF.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** nobody teaches where money comes from, though it decides whether talent and factories go to curing or killing, because a system too boring to understand needs no army.
- **Plates:**
  - `classroom`: a schoolroom on a plain field: YOU at a school desk far too small for him, a blackboard whose chalk bars are the charts, and a big window. Out of the window, a road forks at a signpost: one branch to the hospital, one to the weapons factory, the Federal Reserve press standing at the signpost.
  - `costume-stage`: a bare wooden stage with a costume rack, one serf in the middle.
- **Cast:** existing: `you`, `moth`, `bill-plain`, `bill-war`, `fed-crank-up` / `fed-crank-down`, `road`, `signpost`, `hospital`, `factory`, `scientist` (a line of copies), `mushroom`, `friend-man` and `friend-woman` (the parents), `fat-cat`, `pyramid`, `loaf`, `goose`, `soldiers`, `pdf`. From earlier: `gauge` / `needle` (episode 1), `mill` (Part 22). New: `recorder-kid`, `stork`, `school-desk`, `serf`, `pharaoh`, `medieval-king`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 22's moth eats the last of YOU's dollar, and Part 22's mill turns into the weapons factory at the fork. The Federal Reserve press is the one from Parts 22 and 24. The serf asleep under the PDF is YOU, who is the viewer in every part after. The recorder kid grows up to keep Pokémon stats in Part 29.
- **New art:**
  - `recorder-kid`: a 1950s schoolkid in a striped T-shirt squeaking a recorder; the class as copies; charred.
  - `stork`: a delivery stork with a bundle sling, flying.
  - `school-desk`: a tiny 1950s school desk with an attached seat.
  - `serf`: one barefoot peasant in a sackcloth tunic, the same face throughout; stacking a rock, with a spear, with a pitchfork, reading, asleep; charred.
  - `pharaoh` (on a throne with a crook and flail), `medieval-king` (crown, ermine, a big chicken leg).
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: chalk bars on the blackboard, one of them empty, and a chalk doodle (the mitochondrion) that grows a smokestack.
  - `spin`: the signpost's arms swing and whatever is walking past turns the way they point.
