# Part 12 storyboard: "Goal Two: Stable Prices"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "Goal 2: "Stable Prices" (Take 2% Per Year)" and "The "Deflation Is Dangerous" Story".
- **The numbers:** none of these are `_variables.yml` parameters; the chapter states them inline.
  - The Fed's inflation target: 2% a year (stated policy).
  - 2% a year for 35 years compounds to a 50% loss (0.98^35 = 0.49).
  - Since Nixon (1971, 54 years): 93% lost, as narrated. The book text says "over 87%"; the screen uses the narrator's 93%. For the hull: the water line at 7% of the ship left above it.
  - The phone case: $40, lost in three months. The laptop: worth half in 18 months. The telephone queue: 14 hours, a better one in 11 months.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** "stable prices" is an announced plan to make your money worth 2% less every year, and the excuse for it (people stop buying when prices fall) is false about a species that queues in the rain for phones.
- **Plates:**
  - `sinking-chart`: an inked line chart on aged paper, "PRICES", years along the bottom; the sea is the area under the line, and an ocean liner sits on it. The line rises; the water rises with it.
  - `archery-range`: a plain lawn, a straw archery target on an easel at the right, the Fed building at the left with a bow.
  - `store-front`: the `market-of-tomorrow` as the store, its big display window (ELECTRONICS) and double doors on a plain pavement. The window is also the tech chart: the tags that drop in it ink TECH PRICES downhill across the glass, and YOU's growing pile of boxes in front of it is TECH SALES.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `fed-crank-up` / `fed-crank-down`, `you`, `economist`, `citizen`, `beard`, `skeleton`, `price-tag`, `bill-plain` (chopped for confetti), `google-phone`, `laptop`, `tv-set`, `calendar`, `wall-clock`, `cobweb`, `crate`, `cloud`, `scissors`, `gold-star`, `market-of-tomorrow`; from Part 8: `nixon`. New: `ocean-liner` (a 1950s liner, the S.S. STABLE PRICES, with a ruler painted up its hull; its funnel, a flag), `archery-target` (a straw target on an easel; the same piece straps to YOU's chest), `bow` (a longbow and arrows, held by the Fed building), `storybook` (a big children's storybook with a turning page, held by the economist; a nightcap for him).
- **Callbacks:** the Fed building is Part 1's (it cranks itself); Nixon is Part 8's. The flattened economist, still holding his storybook, is the one who returns in Part 13 with his one data point.
- **New art:** the sinking chart and the archery range, and the four new cut-outs above (the store is the existing `market-of-tomorrow`). Words on the hull, the target, signs, tags and charts are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: a line drawn over time, with a fill under it that rises as water (the sea level is the PRICES line).
  - `stack`: boxes piling into YOU's arms.
