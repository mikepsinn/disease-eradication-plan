# Part 21 storyboard: "Your 93% Pay Cut (Measured in Shiny Rocks)"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "Your 93% Pay Cut (Measured in Shiny Rocks)" and "Why Nobody Told You" (citations `census-family-income-1972`, `macrotrends-gold-history`, `census-income-2023`, `epi-productivitypay2024`).
- **The numbers:**
  - 1972: a typical family earned the equivalent of 191 ounces of gold a year (cited, not a parameter). Today that is "over $1,030,000" (about $5,400 an ounce).
  - Median household income $77,500, buying 14 ounces (cited as `census-income-2023`). The parameter `us_median_household_income_2023` is $80,610; the screen uses the narrator's $77,500.
  - 191 to 14 is a 93% cut (14 ÷ 191 = 7.3% left). "54 years" of it at "roughly 2% per year" is the book's framing (Part 12).
  - The gold-supply note: above-ground gold 93,000 tonnes (1972) to 212,000 tonnes; population 3.85 billion to 8.1 billion; gold per person 24.2 g to 26.2 g; with gold assumed 50% overpriced, the cut is still 85% (cited, not parameters).
  - The think tanks' gap: about $10,000 a year (EPI). The narrator's gap: $952,000 a year ($1,030,000 − $77,500 ≈ $952,500).
  - The ruler's three tricks: CPI redesigns; starting at 1979 instead of 1971; health insurance counted as pay.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** in the one money that can't be printed, your family's pay fell from 191 ounces to 14, and the official measure hides it by shrinking the ruler.
- **Plates:**
  - `alchemy`: a plain field, an alchemist's bench at the left, the Fed building at the right. At the end the Fed's meeting table stands between them, one empty chair.
  - `gold-pay`: a 1950s dining room on a plain field, a table, the family; later a line chart, "PAY IN OUNCES OF GOLD", 1972 to today.
  - `think-tank`: a big glass fish tank on a stand, economists swimming in it with a ruler, and a gap to be measured beside it.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `fed-crank-up` / `fed-crank-down`, `family`, `you`, `economist`, `politician`, `podium`, `scroll`, `skeleton`, `beard`, `cell`, `balance-scale` (Part 17's), `citizen`, `ruler`, `donut-box` (Part 14's), `calendar`, `coin` (tinted gold, stacked; the 191 and the 14), `scissors` (tinted beige), `shackle` (the ankle chain; its tag is code), `hot-water-bottle`, `paycheck`; from this chapter's Part 1: `gold-bar` (also tinted grey for the lead bars), `gold-nugget`, `wishonian`; from Part 2: `fomc` (the Fed's committee). New: `alchemist` (a medieval alchemist in a pointed hat with a crucible and bellows), `flag` (an American flag on a pole), `fish-tank` (the think tank: a glass tank on a stand with air bubbles; economists swim in it in their suits).
- **Callbacks:** Part 10 of episode 1, where the 1913 paycheck's loaf shrank to a crumb: here the pay is coins, and the pile shrinks from 191 to 14. The Politician at his podium is episode 1's. The Fed's committee is Part 2's `fomc`, back at the alchemy plate beside the alchemist's skeleton.
- **New art:** the three plates above and the three new cut-outs. The numbers, the chart, the tags, the GROWING banner and the plaque are code.
- **Kit additions:**
