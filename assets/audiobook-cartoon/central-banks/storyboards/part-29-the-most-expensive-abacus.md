# Part 29 storyboard: "The World's Most Expensive Abacus"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The World's Most Expensive Abacus". The narration is `central-banks.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_government_clinical_trials_spending_annual`: $4.5 billion a year.
  - Hardcoded in the chapter, not parameters: the US financial sector captures $2.3 trillion a year, 8% of GDP, and employs 6.7 million people; medical research gets 0.065% of GDP (the 2025 point from Part 28); the shared-ledger technology since 2009; 3 billion DNA base pairs; Wishonia's ledger, 4,000 years without an error.
