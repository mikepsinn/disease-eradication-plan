# Part 16 storyboard: "The 2020 Demonstration"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The 2020 Demonstration" (citations `fred-walcl-2026`, `fred-top1-wealth-2026`, `fed-wealth-inequality-covid-2021`).
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_government_clinical_trials_spending_annual`: $4.5 billion a year, every government's clinical-trial spending combined.
  - The Fed created about $4 trillion in 2020 (cited, not a parameter).
  - The top 1% gained $4 trillion in net worth over the same period (cited, not a parameter).
  - Nearly 90% of those gains came from stocks; one-third of bottom-50% households own any stocks (cited, not a parameter).
  - Derived for the picture (not spoken): $4 trillion ÷ $4.5 billion ≈ 889, so the mountain is about 889 trial-budget stacks, and drawn to scale, if the mountain is 900 px tall the trials stack is about 1 px.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the Fed printed $4 trillion in one year, almost 900 years of all governments' trial budgets, and the same $4 trillion turned up in the top 1%'s stocks.
- **Plates:**
  - `money-mountain`: the Fed building at the left of a plain field, a mountain of bills rising from its tray, and a tiny stack at the mountain's foot.
  - `two-bars`: a two-bar chart, "PRINTED" and "TOP 1% GAINED", with a table and an empty cake stand at its foot and the fat cat beside it.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `fed-crank-up` / `fed-crank-down`, `money-bundle`, `bill-plain`, `bill-med`, `scientist`, `magnifier`, `fat-cat`, `citizen`, `price-tag`, `share-certificate` (swelled in code), `hero-sash` (its words are code), `pdf` (the report), `calendar`; from Part 1: `fed-mouth` (blowing), `ape-picture` (its empty gilt frame); from Part 5: `fed-hose`; from Part 15: `cantillon`. New: `cake` (a tall layered cake of green bills on a cake stand, and the empty stand with crumbs), `fat-cat-frosted` (the fat cat smeared in green frosting, a fork in his paw).
- **Callbacks:** Cantillon, who wrote it down in Part 15, watches from Part 1's gilt frame and rolls his eyes. The fire-and-fire-department gag returns in Part 19 as the bill for the fire truck; here the Fed's hose (Part 5's) sprays bills on the flames.
- **New art:** the two plates above and the two new cut-outs. The numbers, the bars' labels, the sash, the report's title and the stamps are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `stack` to a mountain: copies of `money-bundle` piling into a heap faster and faster, the camera tilting up with it.
  - `inflate` (as Part 14): the share certificates swell like balloons.
