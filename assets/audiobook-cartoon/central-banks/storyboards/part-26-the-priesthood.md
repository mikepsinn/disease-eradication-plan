# Part 26 storyboard: "The Priesthood"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The Priesthood", "The Fake Nobel Prize" and "The Research". The narration is `central-banks.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The narrator's figures, hardcoded in the chapter:
  - 1968: Sveriges Riksbank (Sweden's central bank) created the economics prize. Nobel died in 1896.
  - Thousands of economics doctorates since 1971, four to seven years each.
  - Median income growth fell 80% after Nixon closed the gold window (1971). Derived: the growth bar keeps 20% of its height.
  - The longest US deflation, 1870 to 1896, produced the fastest growth.
  - The Federal Reserve (1913) presided over about 9,000 bank failures; Canada, without a central bank, had zero.
  - Every war that killed more than a million people required a money printer; the hard-money list is empty.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the people paid by the money system are the ones who explain it to you, and the evidence they skip is a chart anyone can read.
- **Plates:**

  - `lecture-hall`: tiered rows of economists, a big chart on the wall, a blackboard of two lists, a round window high up.
