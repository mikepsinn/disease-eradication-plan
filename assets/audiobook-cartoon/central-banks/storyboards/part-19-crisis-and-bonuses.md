# Part 19 storyboard: "The Crisis"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The Time They Broke It", from "Then your banks did something I still find remarkable" through "you give the child $5.33 billion" (citations `cuomo-bank-bonuses-2009`, `cbs-bank-bonuses-2009`).
- **The numbers:** none are `_variables.yml` parameters; the chapter cites them inline.
  - The bailout ask: $700 billion (TARP).
  - Citigroup: $45 billion in taxpayer bailout funds; $5.33 billion in executive bonuses.
  - Goldman Sachs: earned $2.3 billion; paid $4.8 billion in bonuses (about twice its earnings; the gap is $2.5 billion).
  - "Eighty years" of watching (the narrator's; the book text says 81).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** nothing real was lost in 2008 except invented numbers, the banks made you pay $700 billion anyway, and they paid themselves bonuses with it when they could have skipped them.

  - `bonus-bars`: two pairs of bars on aged paper, CITIGROUP and GOLDMAN SACHS.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
