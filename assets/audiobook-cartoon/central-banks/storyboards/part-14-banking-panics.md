# Part 14 storyboard: "The Banking Panics Defense"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The "Banking Panics" Defense" (citation `bordo-redish-rockoff-canada-banking-2015`) and "What They Could Measure Instead".
- **The numbers:** none are `_variables.yml` parameters; the chapter cites them inline.
  - The Panic of 1907; the Fed founded 1913; 16 years later (1929 to 1933) more than 9,000 US banks failed.
  - Canada: no central bank until 1935, zero bank failures in the Depression.
  - The FDIC: 1933.
  - "Most of the past five decades": about 50 report cards for the stack.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the institution built to stop bank panics fed the worst one, a neighbour without one lost no banks, deposit insurance is what stopped runs, and the Fed is never graded on whether people are healthier and richer.
- **Plates:**
  - `border-map`: the existing engraved `world-map`, close on North America, the US and Canada, a row of little bank buildings on each side of the border.
  - `bank-run`: one bank building on a plain pavement with a queue running at it.
  - `report-desk`: a plain schoolroom desk with the Optimitron and a stack of report cards.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `economist`, `fed-crank-up` / `fed-crank-down`, `bank-legs`, `citizen`, `world-map`, `scissors`, `letter` (the promise; its words are code), `ledger`, `coin`, `patient`, `iron-bed`, `optimitron`, `gauge`, `needle`, `report-card`, `gold-star`, `you`, `loaf`, `paycheck`, `desk`, `calendar`, `skeleton`, `fire-alarm` (episode 1 Part 11's fire-alarm post), `wall-clock`, `cobweb`, `pull-down-chart` (host style); from Part 5: `stock-bubble` (the 1920s bubble), `fed-hose`; from Part 10: `pay-packet`. New: `mountie-hat` (a little red-coat Mountie hat that sits on a bank building's roof), `fdic-office` (the FDIC as itself: a small, plain brick insurance office with a modest face in its lintel, a green eyeshade over it, stick arms in white gloves and a big rubber stamp).
- **Callbacks:** the bubble is Part 5's 1920s stock bubble, and the hose the Fed doses the patient with is Part 5's. The dominoes of bank buildings from Part 13's the-only-evidence come back by the thousand. The FDIC is a building, like the Fed; it is small next to the press, which is the point of scene 7. The Optimitron's HEALTH and WEALTH dials are episode 1's (Parts 14, 22).
- **New art:** the bank-run and report-desk plates and the two new cut-outs (the map is the existing `world-map`). The 9,000 and 0 counters, the stamps, the report-card grades and the map's labels are code.
- **Kit additions:**
