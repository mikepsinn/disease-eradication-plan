# Part 3 storyboard: "The Pattern: Print, Kill, Repeat"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The Pattern: Print, Kill, Repeat" (Rome, Revolutionary France, Weimar Germany).
- **The numbers** (all cited in the chapter, none are parameters):
  - Rome, third century: the denarius went from 95% silver to under 5%; prices rose 1,000%.
  - France, 1790s: hyperinflation of 13,000% in five years; the guillotine about 17,000 times.
  - Weimar, 1920s: 29,500% monthly inflation; a loaf at 1 mark in January cost billions by the autumn.
  - Wishonia has watched since 1945.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** each time, war money was printed, prices exploded, and the crowd was handed someone to blame and killed them; the curve is the same shape every time.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-loop`: a ring of six stops on aged paper: a tank, an empty cash drawer, a press, a wheelbarrow of money, a skeleton, and back to the tank.
  - `history-wall`: one long wall on aged paper with three price charts in a row, ROME, FRANCE, GERMANY, each with its scene in front of it. The camera travels right along it, one stop per country. The same big wooden THEM arrow stands in front of each.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `arrow-sign` (the THEM arrow), `coin` (the denarius), `roman-soldier`, `crowd`, `hitler`, `rulers` (its Napoleon), `printing-press`, `tank`, `cash-drawer`, `bill-war`, `crook-mask`, `family`, `loaf`, `price-tag`, `you`, `bill-plain`, `skeleton`, `flames`, `moth`, `rubble`. New: `diocletian`, `christians`, `roman-temple`, `barbarians`, `church`, `aristocrats`, `guillotine`, `iron-stove`, `wheelbarrow`.
- **Callbacks:** the printing press from Part 1. YOU's pay from Part 1's friends-first. The `rulers` sheet's Napoleon from episode 1's Part 3. The assignat is a war bill with the `crook-mask` in its portrait oval.
- **New art:**
  - `diocletian`: the emperor, caricatured: a bull neck, a laurel wreath, a purple toga, a carpenter's plane.
  - `christians`: a little group in plain robes; charred; skeletons.
  - `roman-temple`: a temple whose columns are stacks of silver coins; split; fallen.
  - `barbarians`: two hairy men in furs who wipe their feet.
  - `church`: a small stone church (it burns in Rome, and rides in a wheelbarrow in France).
  - `aristocrats`: powdered wigs and lace; their wigs, and their skeletons in wigs.
  - `guillotine`, with a counter board.
  - `iron-stove`: a cast-iron stove with a door (Part 5's oven).
  - `wheelbarrow`: empty, and heaped with marks.
  - The two plates. Chart lines, years, percentages, signs, stamps and the counter's digits are code; the French-curve stencil is a host prop.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: a price line drawn left to right, able to run past the top of its frame and up off the plate.
  - A pivot: a cut-out (the THEM arrow) swinging on its post to a named target.
  - A counter: digits that tick up, faster and faster, to a set number.
