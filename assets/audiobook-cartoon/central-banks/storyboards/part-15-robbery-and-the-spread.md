# Part 15 storyboard: "How the Robbery Works"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "How the Robbery Works" and "The Spread".
- **The numbers:** none are `_variables.yml` parameters; the chapter states them inline.
  - Richard Cantillon, 1700s (the Cantillon effect: first to get new money benefits, last pays).
  - The Fed lends to banks at about 5%, and at nearly 0% for 14 years after 2008.
  - Banks lend to you at 24% (credit-card interest).
  - The river thief: moved in about a day; Earth's version: running for a century (the Fed since 1913).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** new money reaches the banks first and you last, after the prices have risen, and the bank charges you 24% on money it got for nearly nothing and did nothing to.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-queue`: the Fed building at the left of a plain field, a long queue stretching right from its paper tray: a bank building, the weapons factory, a few citizens, and YOU at the very end in front of a little grocery counter. A shop with price tags runs along the queue.
  - `the-spread`: the Fed building at the left behind a velvet rope, a bank building in the middle dressed as a waiter (a towel over its arm, a silver tray), YOU in Part 11's diner booth at the right.
  - `the-river`: a river on aged paper, a village on one bank, a water thief with a toll gate on the path between them.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `fed-crank-up` / `fed-crank-down`, `bank-legs`, `factory-proud`, `factory-pay`, `you`, `citizen`, `grandma`, `loaf`, `bill-plain`, `price-tag`, `coin`, `cash-register`, `calendar`, `laptop`, `signpost`, `sign-board` (the shop sign, and the neon sign on its pole; the letters are code); from Part 2: `notice-board`; from Part 5: `chicken` (the hen); from Part 11: `diner` (YOU's booth); from Part 20: `ties` (the necktie). New: `cantillon` (Richard Cantillon, 1700s Irish-French banker in a powdered wig, with a quill and an open notebook), `egg-carton` (a carton of a dozen eggs with a price tag), `sampler` (an embroidered sampler in a hoop, host prop; the stitching is code), `waiter-kit` (a waiter's towel and a silver tray, sized for the bank building), `velvet-rope` (brass posts and a velvet rope), `water-thief` (a man in a tall hat with a yoke of two buckets and a toll gate with a coin box).
- **Callbacks:** the trip in Part 1 (friends first, YOU last) is here a queue, the same building at its head. Grandma is episode 1's (Parts 17, 21, 27), curlers and all.
- **New art:** the three plates above and the six new cut-outs. The interest rates, price tags, the list, the sampler's words and the logo's letters are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `pass`: a bill handed along a row of cut-outs (the queue), each price tag flipping up as it passes.
  - `march`: copies of a coin walking from one cut-out to another in single file, counted.
