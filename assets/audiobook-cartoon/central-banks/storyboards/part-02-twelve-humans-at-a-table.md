# Part 2 storyboard: "Twelve Humans at a Table"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, from "Your money comes from a building" through "anything this stupid on purpose."
- **The numbers:**
  - The Federal Open Market Committee: 12 voting members, 8 scheduled meetings a year (cited in the chapter, not parameters).
  - "Over a century": the Fed since 1913.
  - 97 million dead: the narrator's number (Part 1's note; the book now uses `war_deaths_since_1900` = 310 million). The screen uses 97,000,000.
  - 2009: the shared ledger's Earth debut.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** twelve unelected people decide how much money exists, nobody checks them, and the tool that would check them exists and goes unused.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-fed-building`: the Fed building in cutaway: inside, one round table, twelve chairs and a big brass dial on the wall, "HOW MUCH MONEY EXISTS"; a front door and a back door; outside, the pavement under its window, where YOU stands by his things.
  - `two-rings`: a plain floor with Part 1's honest ring of computers on the left and the twelve-person table on the right.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `fed-crank-up`, `you`, `auditor`, `headstone`, `calendar`, `moth`, `bill-war`, `sheets`, `magnifier`, `paint-chips`; from Part 1: `ledger-computer`, `ape-picture`. New: `fomc`, `money-dial`, `rent-row`.
- **Callbacks:** the honest ring of computers and the "NO YOU DON'T" fib from Part 1's rock-money; Part 1's ape frame now holds a dog in a hat. The moth from every empty thing. YOU from Part 1's it-is-eating-you.
- **New art:**
  - `fomc`: twelve humans of different builds in 1950s suits at one round table, each with a blank "HELLO MY NAME IS" tag; turned outward, covering their papers.
  - `money-dial`: a brass wall dial with a big lever.
  - `rent-row`: a rent envelope, a grocery bag, a pill bottle and a piggy bank, each able to shrink.
  - For Part 1's `ape-picture` frame: a dog in a little hat.
  - The two plates. Words on tags, the dial, the notice and stamps are code; the console drawer is a host prop.
- **Kit additions:**
  - A dial: a needle that swings to a set angle, and things on another layer that scale with it (the rent row).
