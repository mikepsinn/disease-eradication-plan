# Part 4 storyboard: "The American Money Printer"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The American Money Printer", through "identical to the list of important ones."
- **The numbers** (cited in the chapter, not parameters):
  - 1913: the Federal Reserve is created. 1917: the US enters the First World War, the Fed four years old.
  - 1917 to now: the dollar has lost 96% of its value. For the chart: a line from 100 cents in 1917 down to 4 cents now.
  - 1916: Wilson re-elected on "He kept us out of war". April 1917: war declared.
  - The Holocaust: 6 million Jews killed; the Second World War: 60 million more.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the Fed let the government fight a war the public didn't want without asking them to pay, and that one step set off the chain that ended in the Holocaust.
- **Plates:**
  - `fed-timeline`: an inked timeline on aged paper, 1913 to 2024, with the Fed building at 1913, Wilson's platform and banner at 1916 with a crowd in front, and a dollar chart riding above the line.
  - `two-roads-to-war`: the Capitol, YOU's front door, and the weapons factory, on a plain field; later a pipe from the Fed building.
  - `the-chain`: one long iron chain on a plain field, its links forged one by one, left to right.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `fed-crank-up`, `factory-pay`, `woodrow-wilson`, `uncle-sam` (his I WANT YOU poster), `banner`, `ballot-box`, `crowd`, `capitol`, `you`, `bill-war`, `bill-blank` (the IOU), `crook-mask`, `treaty-scroll`, `wheelbarrow` (Part 3), `arrow-sign` (Part 3's THEM arrow), `bank`, `hitler`, `skeleton`, `headstone`, `scissors`, `missile-fly`, `moth`, `city-of-tomorrow`. - **Callbacks:** the Fed building and the weapons factory from Parts 1 and 2. Part 3's wheelbarrow of marks and the THEM arrow are the middle links of the chain. Part 3's burglar mask (`crook-mask`) goes on the Fed building.
- **New art:**
  - `birthday-candle`: one fat candle with a "4" on it (Part 6 reuses it).
  - `money-pipe`: a brass pipe with elbows.
  - `policeman`: a 1950s beat cop.
  - `chain-link`: one iron link that can be stamped with a word, copied into a chain (Parts 9 and 10 reuse it as the anchor chain).
  - The three plates. Years, the dollar line, slogans, the links' words and the numbers are code; the two clipboards are host props.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: a line drawn over a set time, with a cut-out (the bill on a sled) riding it.
  - A chain: copies of one link added left to right, each swinging into place; and a release, where the links blink out one after another.

- time: 11:53.3 to 12:46.0 (52.7 s)
- voiceover: "The consequences of not asking were, as your species likes to understate, "significant." The war produced the Treaty of Versailles. The treaty imposed reparations on Germany. He did not fix everything. He killed six million Jews and started a war that killed sixty million more. The causal chain is: money printer leads to war nobody voted for, leads to punitive treaty, leads to hyperinflation, leads to Holocaust. Every link required the previous one. Remove the first link (a central bank willing to fund a war the public didn't want) and the chain doesn't form."

- bit: **The chain is forged one link per sentence, left to right, and each link carries the thing it caused.** It ends in the dead. Payoff: scissors cut the first link, every link after it never forms, and a city of tomorrow stands where the headstones were.
