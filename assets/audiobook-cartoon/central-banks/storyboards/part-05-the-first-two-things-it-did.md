# Part 5 storyboard: "The First Two Things It Did"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "The American Money Printer", from "The Federal Reserve was created in 1913" to the end of the section ("the chicken escalation problem").
- **The numbers** (cited in the chapter, not parameters):
  - The Depression: within 16 years of 1913 (1929). Over 9,000 banks failed, 1930 to 1933. The money supply fell by a third. The Fed raised rates in 1931.
  - Friedman and Schwartz, *A Monetary History of the United States* (1963).
  - The chain to the Holocaust: within 32 years (1945).
  - Today's dollar is worth 4 cents of a 1913 dollar.
