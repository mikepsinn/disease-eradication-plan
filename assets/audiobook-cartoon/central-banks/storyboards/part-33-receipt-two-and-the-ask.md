# Part 33 storyboard: "Receipt 2 and The Ask"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "Receipt 2: What the Printer Wasted" and "A Modest Proposal" (the narration calls it "The Ask"). The narration is `central-banks.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_government_clinical_trials_spending_annual`: $4.5 billion a year, the unit of the receipt (one year of the world's government clinical trials).
  - `cumulative_military_spending_fed_era`: $170 trillion since the Fed was created (1913). Derived: $170 trillion / $4.5 billion = 37,800, "nearly thirty-eight thousand years". For the receipt's far end: 38,000 years ago is the Ice Age, when Neanderthals were still around.
  - `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year, 1% of `global_military_spending_annual_2024` ($2.72 trillion).
  - `rare_diseases_count_global`: 7,000 diseases with zero approved treatments.
  - `status_quo_queue_clearance_years`: 443 years to test them at current capacity (from 2025, about 2468).
  - `dfda_queue_clearance_years`: 36 years at treaty funding (about 2061).
  - Hardcoded in the chapter, not parameters: six wars, over $16 trillion US share, 3,500 years; the financial sector $2.3 trillion a year, 8% of GDP, 511 years a year, 6.7 million people, the ledger free since 2009, "fifteen years"; the Fed's $4 trillion in 2020, 889 years; the 2008 bailout, $700 billion, 156 years; corporate welfare $300 billion a year, 67 years a year; Wishonia has watched for 80 years.
