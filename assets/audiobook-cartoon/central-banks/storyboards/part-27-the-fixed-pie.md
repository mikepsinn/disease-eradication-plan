# Part 27 storyboard: "Why "Just Print More Money for Medicine" Doesn't Work"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "Why "Just Print More Money for Medicine" Doesn't Work", "The Fixed Pie of Human Genius" and "The Numbers". The narration is `central-banks.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_population_2024`: 8 billion (the narrator's "eight billion humans").
  - `military_to_government_clinical_trials_spending_ratio`: 604 (governments spend 604 times more on weapons than on testing which medicines work).
  - Hardcoded in the chapter, not parameters: the top 10% of human talent, about 800 million people; about 40 million building things that kill (military, reserves, weapons makers); about 1 million in medical research. Derived for the pictogram: 1 figure = 1 million people, so 40 soldiers against 1 lab coat.
  - Narrator's other figures: 24 hours a day; ten years of a PhD; a restaurant that closes in 18 months; 12,000 years on Wishonia.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** money is a story about who gets real things; there is a fixed supply of brains, hours and factories, and right now 40 times more of the best brains build weapons than do medical research.

  - `one-dollar`: a plain field where one bill floats over the globe, then YOU's life passes under it.
  - `the-pie`: a giant pie on a plain table; beyond it, the engineer's drafting easel, the weapons factory and the family's house.
  - `talent-chart`: an inked pictogram chart on aged paper, TOP 10% OF HUMAN TALENT, and beside it a two-bar chart, WEAPONS and TESTING MEDICINES.

  - `pie`: a giant lattice pie, cut into wedges that lift out.
