# Part 8 storyboard: "What About Wars Under Hard Money?" and "1971: The Day Your Money Stopped Meaning Anything"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/central-banks.qmd`, "What About Wars Under Hard Money?" and "1971: The Day Your Money Stopped Meaning Anything".
- **The numbers** (cited in the chapter, not parameters):
  - Wars that killed over 1 million with every side on gold: none.
  - Britain left gold in 1797 (Napoleon); the US printed greenbacks in 1862; every belligerent left gold within weeks in 1914.
  - The Spanish-American War, 1898, fought on gold: ten weeks; 345 US combat deaths.
  - Wishonia's sign: unchanged in 4,000 years.
  - Nixon's speech: Sunday, August 15, 1971; "two hundred million people" (the US then).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** big wars need printed money because people asked to pay with real money stop paying, and in 1971 the dollar was cut loose from gold.
- **Plates:**
  - `gold-wars`: one plain field the camera travels along: three presses in a row, 1797, 1862, 1914, each chained to a gold bar; then YOU's front door; then a cannon on a hill with a coin slot and a meter, a queue of citizens and a calendar.
  - `wishonia-street`: Wishonia's world-of-tomorrow street, a big sign out front.
  - `the-living-room`: a 1970s living room on a plain field: a console TV, an empty armchair, a mantel with a piggy bank, a window to the beach.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `plan-clipboard`, `moth`, `rulers` (Napoleon), `gold-bar` (Part 1), `chain-link` (Part 4), `printing-press`, `bill-war` (the greenbacks), `capitol`, `you`, `globe`, `loaf`, `family`, `citizen`, `coin`, `headstone`, `city-of-tomorrow` (Wishonia's street), `wishonian` (Part 1), `sign-board`, `cobweb`, `spider`, `ladder`, `tv-set`, `armchair`, `rent-row` (Part 2's piggy bank), `scissors`, `price-tag`, `paycheck`, `crate`, `vault-door`, `fed-crank-up`. New: `soldier-blue-grey`, `coin-op-cannon`, `teddy-roosevelt`, `nixon`.
- **Callbacks:** the Capitol knocking on YOU's door, from Part 4. The eleven-year-old's honest ring from Part 1 sits in Wishonia's street. YOU's kid's piggy bank from Part 2.
- **New art:**
  - `soldier-blue-grey`: a Civil War soldier, half Union blue and half Confederate grey, punching himself; knocked down.
  - `coin-op-cannon`: a cannon with a coin slot, a meter with a needle; drooping, a cannonball dribbling out.
  - `teddy-roosevelt`: Theodore Roosevelt, caricatured (teeth, pince-nez, a Rough Rider hat), on horseback; sliding over its neck.
  - `nixon`: Richard Nixon, caricatured (jowls, ski-jump nose, five o'clock shadow, the double V), at a desk on TV.
  - The three plates. Words on signs, dates, the meter's numbers and the stamps are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - A meter: a needle that falls to zero over a set time, and a cut-out that stops when it does.
  - A snap: a chain that breaks at a link and whips away.
