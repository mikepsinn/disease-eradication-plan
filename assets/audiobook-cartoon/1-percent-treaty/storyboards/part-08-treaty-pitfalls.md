# Part 8 storyboard: "Avoiding Treaty Pitfalls"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd` as narrated ("Avoiding Treaty Pitfalls" and "Summary").
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1%; the summary's "one penny" is 1 cent in each dollar.
  - `iab_political_incentive_funding_pct`: 10% of whatever actually flows, so a relabeled budget shrinks the yes-voters' share.
