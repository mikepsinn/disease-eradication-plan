# Part 3 storyboard: "The Blueprint"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd` as narrated ("The Blueprint" and "What Each Country Pays").
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1% of each military budget.
  - USA: $8.86 billion, 1% of `us_military_spending_2024_annual` $886 billion.
  - `pentagon_unaccounted_funds`: $2.46 trillion the Pentagon can't account for. Derived for the chart: 278 times the US contribution, so if the US bar is 3 px, the lost bar is 834 px; the lost bar runs off the top of the frame.
  - The other contributions, as the narrator says them (not parameters; each is 1% of that country's military budget): China $2.96 billion, India $835 million, Russia $1.09 billion, Saudi Arabia $754 million, UK $686 million, France $563 million.
  - "About two hundred other wars" since 1945 (the narration, not a parameter).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** treaties are flimsy, but they're the only tool for countries acting together, and each country's 1% is pocket change next to what it already wastes.
- **Plates:**

  - `treaty-timeline`: a timeline on aged paper, 1945 to now, and a toolbox on the floor.
  - `country-bars`: a bar chart on aged paper, "WHAT EACH COUNTRY PAYS (1%)", a bar per country, each bar drawn as the thing the narrator compares it to.
  - The blueprint is the title card, not a plate.
