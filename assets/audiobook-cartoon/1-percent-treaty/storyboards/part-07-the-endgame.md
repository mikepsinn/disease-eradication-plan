# Part 7 storyboard: "What Happens If This Actually Works"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd` as narrated ("What Happens If This Actually Works", "The Endgame" and "The Ratchet Effect"); Article III of the treaty (the share can rise and never fall).
- **The numbers:**
  - 847 civilizations Wishonia has watched (the narration; episode 1 Part 13 uses the same 847).
  - Bondholders: `victory_bond_funding_pct` 10% of all inflows, forever. At 1%, `victory_bond_annual_payout` $2.72 billion a year. Derived: at 5%, $13.6 billion (10% × 5% × `global_military_spending_annual_2024` $2.72 trillion); at full redirection, $272 billion. Drawn: if the 1% bar is 9 px, the 5% bar is 45 px and the full bar is 900 px, off the top of the frame.
  - Politicians: `iab_political_incentive_funding_pct` 10%. At 1%, `iab_political_incentive_funding_annual` $2.72 billion a year; at 5%, five times that ($13.6 billion, derived).
  - Grandma's "another half-percent": the treaty going from 1% to 1.5% (the narration).
  - The URL the narrator reads: impact.warondisease.org.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** once the 1% works, three groups (the cured public, the bondholders and the yes-voting politicians) all get more when it grows, and the treaty can only turn one way.

  - `worlds-fair`: the World of Tomorrow, the countries' pavilions round a fountain, a big scoreboard.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
