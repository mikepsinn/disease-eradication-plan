# Part 2 storyboard: "This Isn't a Trade-Off"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd` as narrated (the script's "This Isn't a Trade-Off" and "Why Your Most Expensive Things Are Also Your Most Useless Things (A Mystery)").
- **The numbers:**
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1%; `treaty_annual_funding`: $27.2 billion a year.
  - The missile you can't walk: $1.5 million a year in "food" (the narration, not a parameter).
  - A bridge lasts 50 to 100 years (the narration, not a parameter).
  - Eisenhower: five stars; Normandy, the largest military operation in human history; the quote is from his "Chance for Peace" speech, 16 April 1953 (history, not parameters).
  - For the button wall: 100 buttons, 1 removed, 99 left (the 1%, drawn).
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the 1% isn't a sacrifice: it makes you safer and less sick at once, because weapons are dead capital that sit, cost and explode, while the same money in trials or bridges keeps paying.
- **Plates:**
  - `safe-street`: one short 1950s street of identical houses on a plain field, an attack dog chained in every yard. Further along: a giant thermometer on a post outside a bunker door (inside, a wall of 100 identical red buttons), a doctor's waiting room with a door marked TRIAL, and the Economist's two-bar chart on a billboard over a vacant lot.
  - `dead-capital`: a river with a stone bridge on the left; on the right bank a lot with a porch table, a missile silo hole with a hatch, a doghouse and the family's little house; a tear-off calendar on a post between them.
  - `eisenhower-stage`: Eisenhower's podium on a plain stage, the weapons factory in the wings, and a war-map table.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
