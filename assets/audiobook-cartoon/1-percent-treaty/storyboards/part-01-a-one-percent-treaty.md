# Part 1 storyboard: "A One Percent Treaty"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd` (the audio was read from an earlier draft of it: the narration script `1-percent-treaty.prepared.txt`, from the chapter title to the end of "How It Works").
- **The numbers:**
  - Overkill: the narrator says "thirteen times". The book's parameter has since become `nuclear_winter_overkill_factor` 122x; the screen uses the narrator's 13. Derived: 1% off 13 is 12.87.
  - `global_military_spending_annual_2024`: $2.72 trillion a year.
  - `treaty_reduction_pct`: 1%, so `treaty_annual_funding` $27.2 billion a year, leaving `global_military_spending_post_treaty_annual_2024` $2.69 trillion. Drawn as 100 bills and one sliver.
  - Valentine's Day: "roughly" $27.2 billion (the narrator's comparison, not a parameter; US Valentine's Day spending ran about $26 to $28 billion in 2024 and 2025). Heart-disease research gets no number in the narration, so its bar has none.
  - Wishonia fought 12,000 years before the treaty; Earth "several thousand years" (the narration, not parameters).
  - `dfda_trial_cost_reduction_factor`: 44.1x cheaper trials (from `traditional_phase3_cost_per_patient` $41,000 to `dfda_pragmatic_trial_cost_per_patient` $929; Part 6 says these out loud). Drawn as one old trial seat's price buying a bench of 44 patients and a hand.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** every country cuts the same 1% at the same time, so the balance of power stays put, and the $27.2 billion a year pays patients into trials that are 44.1 times cheaper, with every dollar on a public ledger.
- **Plates:**
  - `missile-queue`: a tan field to a low horizon. A small Earth at the left; thirteen plain missiles in a queue to the right, each holding a numbered ticket; a tally board on a post.
  - `nation-mobile`: sky-paper; a giant brass hanging mobile, its arms carrying pans of missiles under national flags, hung from a hook in the underside of a huge Earth; far below, on the floor, a three-bar chart where the dropped missiles land.
  - `treaty-machine`: one sheet of treaty paper lying on a plain floor with a small TRIAL door cut into it, and the machine built along it: the factory's pay window, a glass pipe, the vault of the 1% TREATY FUND, a wire of little hospitals, the trial door, a row of sliders and a glass gearbox, and a public ledger on a plinth.
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing names: `factory-proud`, `factory-sob`, `factory-pay`, `missile`, `small-earth`, `earth-big`, `mushroom`, `scissors`, `tally-board`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `hospital`, `patient`, `patient-skeleton`, `sick-1` to `sick-6`, `bank` (the fund's vault), `ledger`, `plinth`, `citizen`, `slider-rail`, `slider-knob`, `fat-cat`, `gold-star`, `trial-door`, `tank`, `warhead`. New: `nation-mobile`, `valentine-box`, `glass-gearbox`.
- **Callbacks:** none needed from episode 1; Part 13 of episode 1 already had "every leader hands over one paper", so this chapter's version of the 1% is the snipped sliver and the mobile instead. Set up for this chapter: the Valentine's candy box comes back in Part 4 (heart disease); the fund's vault is where Part 6's pipe splits 80/10/10; the public ledger audits a relabeled tank in Parts 8 and 9.
- **New art:** the hanging brass mobile with pans and little flag poles (flags in code); a heart-shaped candy box with a ribbon, and a stack of them; a glass gearbox with spouts.
- **Kit additions:**
  - `chart`: bars and a timeline (as in episode 1).
  - `queue`: copies of a cut-out in a line, each moving up one place in turn (the missiles).
  - `rewind`: the plate's moves played backward at speed, with a film-flicker.
  - `ride`: the camera follows one cut-out along a path through a plate (Part 2's money loop).
