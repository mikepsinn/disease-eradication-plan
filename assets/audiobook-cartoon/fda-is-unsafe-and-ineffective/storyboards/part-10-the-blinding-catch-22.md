# Part 10 storyboard: "The Blinding Catch-22"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/fda-is-unsafe-and-ineffective.qmd`, section "The Blinding Catch-22: How Knowing a Drug Works Proves It Doesn't".
- **The numbers:** no `_variables.yml` numbers are spoken. All are cited in the chapter:
  - MDMA synthesized 1912 by Merck (`mdma-synthesis-1912`); about half a million therapeutic doses by the 1970s; scheduled 1985; MAPS founded by Rick Doblin 1986; 38 years and over $140 million in philanthropic funding; two successful Phase 3 trials; 67% no longer met the criteria for PTSD; the advisory committee voted 9 to 2 that it was not effective (`fda-mdma-rejection-2024`); about 90% of the drug group guessed they were not on placebo; Breakthrough Therapy Designation in 2017.
