# Part 1 storyboard: "Locked in a Cabinet"

- **Source:** `knowledge/problem/fda-is-unsafe-and-ineffective.qmd`, its description and opening section (before "A Note on Blame").
- **The numbers:**
  - `efficacy_lag_years`: 8.2 years between proving a drug safe and letting patients take it.
  - `dfda_trial_cost_reduction_factor`: 44.1× (what today's efficacy testing costs against proven alternatives).
  - `antidepressant_trial_exclusion_rate`: 86.1% of the patients who will take the drug are excluded from the trials (about 6 in every 7).
