# Part 3 storyboard: "The Spiral (Already in Progress)"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "The Spiral (Already in Progress)", from its heading through "a circle with a procurement contract at every point on the circumference."
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The story's numbers, as narrated:
  - Military bases in 80 countries.
  - 95% of suicide attacks over two decades were responses to military occupation; half the attackers were secular (the narrator's "Moronian political scientist" is Robert Pape's finding in *Dying to Win*).
  - The wedding: 42 dead, 1 target, 41 reclassified; 200 neutral relatives become enemies.
  - The girls' school: 26 children. Recruitment within 500 kilometers.
  - The transit station: 17 dead, 43 injured, the youngest 4. The son: 11, joined by 15.
  - 200 billion papers for counter-terrorism.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the bases and strikes make more enemies than they kill, only the kills are counted, and the attacks they cause pay for the next strikes.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-loop`: the globe of Moronia on a plain field, a political scientist's desk and pie-chart easel at its foot; later a circular road round the globe with four stops on it.
  - `two-scoreboards`: a plain field under a sky; two scoreboards on posts, one wired to the Capitol on the left, one to a recruitment booth on the right; the wedding awning, the school and the hospital along the field.
  - `two-memorials`: a field split down the middle: a transit station with a split-flap board on the left, the girls' school's rubble on the right, a living-room rug in front.
- **Cast:** existing: `globe` (as Moronia), `citizen` (the villagers, the relatives, the commuters, the recruiter), `scientist` (the political scientist, in antennae, with her clipboard), `bomb`, `pie-chart`, `filing-cabinet`, `scoreboard`, `capitol`, `booth` (the recruitment booth, NOW HIRING in code), `schoolhouse`, `school-desk`, `hospital`, `nurse`, `doctor`, `skeleton`, `rubble`, `plinth` (the memorial, growing), `flames` (its candles), `glowing-rectangle`, `calendar`, `cobweb`, `committee`, `tv-set`, `bill-war`, `factory-proud`, `pointing-hand`, `orphan` (the son at eleven), `soldiers` (him at fifteen). From Part 1: `moronian-antennae`. New: `army-base`, `drone`, `wedding-party`, `procurement-contract`, `class-photo`.
- **Callbacks:** the factory turning the loop is the one that chimneys episode 1's money loop (episode 1 Part 2's machine, here a circle with no exit). The drone is the one that flies over the wheelbarrows in Part 5. The procurement contract is stapled to evidence in Part 4.
- **New art:**
  - `army-base`: a small fenced army base with a flagpole and two barracks, small enough to plant on a globe and big enough for a close-up; soldiers on its fence; charred.
  - `drone`: an engraved military drone, long wings, a bulb nose with a camera eye; a price tag on a string.
  - `wedding-party`: a bride, a groom, guests in their best, a tiered cake under a striped awning; the same party as grinning skeletons in veils and bow ties.
  - `procurement-contract`: a long engraved contract with a wax seal stamped with a missile; dozens can join end to end into a ring.
  - `class-photo`: a school photograph of a class of girls at their desks, dusty, a cobweb on one corner.
  - The plates above. Scoreboard digits, departure-board flaps and tags are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - A ring: copies of one cut-out placed round a circle and spun (the loop's stops; the contracts on the rim).
  - A split-flap board: text that flips in letter by letter.
