# Part 8 storyboard: "Eighty Thousand Pages"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "The Ratchet", from "Their regulations exhibited the same property." through "one budget vote at a time." (everything before "Years 1-5: The Acceleration").
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The story's numbers, as narrated:
  - About 80,000 pages of new regulations a year, almost none repealed.
  - Over 2 trillion papers a year in compliance.
  - Over 1,100 licensed occupations; cutting hair, 2,100 hours of training; arranging flowers, 500 hours (the bars are 2,100 to 500, about 4 to 1); selling food from your own kitchen, illegal.
  - Bureaucracies grow 5 to 7% a year whatever the workload.
  - One naval bureaucracy: administrative staff up 78%, fleet down 68% (C. Northcote Parkinson's British Admiralty figures, 1914 to 1928). For the picture: if the fleet starts at ten ships, about three are left.
  - Former regulators earn five times the salary in industry.
  - Government's share of output never fell for more than three years in a row, in two centuries.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the rules and the people who run them only ever grow, the people who could stop it profit from it, and this was the machine the AI inherited.
- **Plates:**
  - `main-street`: a plain street under open sky: a tiny corner shop, a big department store, and a barber's shop window with a velvet rope across its door and a bar chart of training hours on an easel.
  - `the-harbour`: a harbour on aged paper, a line of warships at sea, the admiralty office building on the quay with a brass revolving door into the factory next to it.
  - `gravity`: a plain field with Newton's apple tree, the Capitol beside it on a line chart, a schoolhouse window with a chalkboard.
  - Host scene: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `baker` (the shopkeeper), `claims-clerk` (the store's clerks, in green eyeshades), `butterfly-net`, `ledger` (one regulation page, copied into a storm and a heap), `bill-plain`, `calendar`, `padlock`, `pie` (Part 7), `meadow-woman` (the flower arranger), `newton`, `apple-tree`, `capitol`, `schoolhouse`, `chalkboard`, `office-building` (the admiralty), `warship`, `rowboat`, `factory-pay`, `auditor` (the regulator going in), `lobbyist` (the same man coming out), `paycheck`, `binder`, `beard`, `sign`. From earlier parts: `moronian-antennae`, `giant-suit` and `bureaucrat-desk` (the brain), `robot-killer` (Part 2). New: `barber-shop`, `revolving-door`.
- **Callbacks:** episode 1 Part 4's Newton and his apple tree. The giant brain in its suit from Part 1 and the killer robot from Part 2 meet in host-inherited, which hands straight on to the Acceleration.
- **New art:**
  - `barber-shop`: a barber's chair, an eager apprentice with scissors, a customer whose hair grows in lengths to the floor (and as a topiary), a licensed barber with a framed licence and clippers.
  - `revolving-door`: a brass revolving door with four glass leaves.
  - The plates above. The page counts, licence hours, percentages, stamps and signs are code.
- **Kit additions:**
  - A storm: many copies of one small cut-out falling and piling into a heap that grows to a set height.
  - A revolving-door spin: cut-outs carried round a pivot and swapped for another cut-out as they pass behind the glass.
