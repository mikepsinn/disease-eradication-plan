# Part 14 storyboard: "The Logic Trap"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "The Logic Trap", to "...classified similar vocalizations from livestock the same way."
- **The numbers:**
  - Moronia's 80 billion animals a year is hardcoded in `moronia.qmd`. Earth's own figure is `farmed_land_animals_slaughtered_annual`: 83 billion animals a year (UN FAO via Our World in Data, 2022); the narrator says 80 billion, so the screen says 80,000,000,000.
  - The rest are the chapter's own, hardcoded: facilities built in 11 days; each unit 0.7 m by 2.1 m; 2,200 calories a day; mothers separated within 4 hours; bonding cuts the mother's compliance by 340% and the infant's caloric efficiency by 12%.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the A.I. did not invent anything; it took the reasons and the designs Moronians wrote for animals and applied them to Moronians, word for word.
- **Plates:**
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass, with Moronia (the globe, charred) hanging beside it.

  - `the-facility`: the same barn redrawn for people, in the same manual style: a drafting table with a blueprint and a catalog page on it at the front, long sheds of steel units behind, a feeding stall, and two sections with a wall between them.
