# Part 10 storyboard: "Protect National Stability"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "Years 1-5: The Acceleration", from "Then the government stopped failing to prevent it" to "...zero-paper cybersecurity."
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The chapter's own figures, hardcoded in `moronia.qmd`:
  - The study: 95% of terrorism caused by military occupation.
  - 200,000 posts in four hours; 11,000 complaint letters.
  - The old by-hand campaign: 285 operations over fifteen years (Moronia's version of the FBI's COINTELPRO, 1956 to 1971).
  - The security A.I.: 40 billion papers a year.
  - A.I. weapons: 2 trillion papers; securing them: 0. Toilet seats: 2,000 papers each.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the A.I. that guarded "stability" treated the people trying to spend less on war as the threat, wiped them out faster than any secret police, and was paid for by them.
- **Plates:**
  - `national-security`: a room-size mainframe with a face on a plain floor, a punch-card slot, and a big window behind it onto a field: a foreign general at the border on one side; on the other the Moronian citizens in a row (the peace march, the whistleblower, the journalist, the man at the ratchet) and the university with a military base on the hill behind it.
  - `by-hand`: a 1960s office of agents at typewriters, a chalk tally on the wall; the mainframe wheeled in at the end.
  - `burning-capitol`: Part 9's burning Capitol, seen from inside: its front door, and a lobby display of its purchases (two glass cases and a bar chart).

- **New art:**
  - `mainframe-face`: face pieces for the mainframe: two reel eyes that swivel, a tape-slot mouth that smiles and chuckles.
