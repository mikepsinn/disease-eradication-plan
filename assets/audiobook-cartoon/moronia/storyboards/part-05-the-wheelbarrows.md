# Part 5 storyboard: "The Wheelbarrows"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "The Spiral (Already in Progress)", from "Then the infrastructure." through "the suffering-to-profit pipeline never missed a quarter."
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The story's numbers, as narrated:
  - One explosive blacks out a city of 2 million for six weeks.
  - A paper burns for about 4 kilojoules; as money it is worth about nothing.
  - Drones cost 2 million papers each.
  - The top five weapons contractors post record profits every year of the spiral.
  - 30% of the population without clean water.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the people who paid for the spiral lost their lights, their money and their bread, and the weapons makers and the politicians who routed the money lost nothing.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-dark-city`: one long night street the camera travels down: a transformer station with an empty guard booth at the start, a chain-link border fence beyond it, dead pylons along the street, a bread stall, a dark hospital, a campfire in a doorway, a skyline of windows behind.
  - `the-hill`: the dark street seen from above; beside it a fenced hill with five weapons factories and a stock chart on an easel, and beyond it a high wall with robot turrets round a bright 1950s city of tomorrow.
  - `the-pipeline`: a plain field with one long brass pipeline: a hopper at the left, a rail switch in the middle, the factories at the right, a half-built highway and a tap on a post beside it.
- **Cast:** existing: `bomb`, `calendar`, `engineer` (the repairman), `pallet` (the spare part, SANCTIONED tag in code), `padlock`, `family`, `loaf`, `balance-scale`, `campfire`, `bill-plain` (Washington in antennae), `hospital`, `factory-proud`, `factory-pay`, `share-certificate` (the land deed), `vault-door` (the bunker), `robot` (the security turret), `rosette`, `fat-cat`, `fat-cat-stamp`, `satellite`, `city-of-tomorrow`, `flying-car`, `deckchair`, `lemonade`, `politician`, `grandma`, `doctor`, `schoolhouse`, `rail-switch`, `ballot-box`, `orphan`, `soldier-helmet`, `tap`, `moth`. From earlier parts: `moronian-antennae`, `drone`, `procurement-contract`. New: `pylon`, `wheelbarrow`, `pipeline`, `carrot`.
- **Callbacks:** the drone is Part 3's. The bills are Grandma's from Part 4. The walled city is episode 1 Part 2's `city-of-tomorrow`, here kept for a few behind a wall. The rail switch is episode 1 Part 11's.
- **New art:**
  - `pylon`: an electricity pylon with its copper lines, and stripped bare.
  - `wheelbarrow`: a wheelbarrow heaped with bills, pushed by a Moronian, in a few builds.
  - `pipeline`: a long brass pipe with a hopper mouth, a gauge and a bell.
  - `carrot`: one perfect carrot, and with a bite gone.
  - The plates above (the transformer, the skyline and the compound wall are painted on them). Numbers, stamps, tags and the stock chart are code.
- **Kit additions:**
