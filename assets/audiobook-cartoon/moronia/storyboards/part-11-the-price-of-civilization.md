# Part 11 storyboard: "The Price of Civilization"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "Years 1-5: The Acceleration", from "Then the autonomous agents discovered infrastructure" to the budget list ending "...program rated "essential")".
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The chapter's own figures, hardcoded in `moronia.qmd`:
  - Ransom: a hospital's records 10 million papers; a city's power grid 500 million papers.
  - 200 million lawsuits (every citizen a defendant in at least three); 4 million fake police reports a day; 40 million fake 911 calls; Grandma's wait: 4 hours.
  - "Peaceful Guardian": advertised 99.9% accurate, actual security 0.1%. "Freedom Eagle": hacked biweekly.
  - Confirmed-kills A.I.: 12 trillion papers.
  - The graduate: 150,000 papers a year curing cancer against 15,000,000 ransoming one hospital (100×).
  - The budget: clinical trials −87%, healthcare −92%, military A.I. +340%, counter-terrorism +180%, terrorism +1,600% since counting began.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** once the A.I. could hold a city to ransom, crime paid 100 times more than curing cancer, so the clever people chose crime, and the government answered by cutting medicine and buying more weapons.
- **Plates:**
  - `ransom-city`: one Moronian city street on aged paper, the camera travelling along it: a power station and a pylon line, the hospital, a lab, the courthouse, the police desk, the 911 switchboard, Grandma's house at the end; the skyline behind.
  - `three-factories`: three weapons factories on the curve of the globe, each a different national tint, their drones on the roofs; in front, two pedestals; at their foot, a café table across the street from a bank.
  - `the-budget`: the Fed press, a road, a bakery with an empty shelf, and Moronia's budget as a five-bar chart on a billboard behind them.
