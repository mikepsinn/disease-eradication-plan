# Part 4 storyboard: "Eight Trillion Papers"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "The Spiral (Already in Progress)", from "Nobody read the study." through "The papers were still very well printed."
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The story's numbers, as narrated:
  - The War on Terror: 8 trillion papers over two decades; terrorism attacks from 1,000 a year to 17,000 a year (17 times).
  - Over two decades: children lose 30% of their education funding; hospitals close in 40% of rural areas (two of five little hospitals on the plate); three bridges collapse.
  - Inflation: 3% in year 1, 8% in year 2, 40% in year 5; a loaf costs 200 papers in year 10 and 50,000 in year 15. For the stack: if 200 bills reach the clouds' underside, 50,000 bills are 250 times as tall, far past the top of the frame.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the war made the threat it was paid to remove, the budget grew because it failed, and when the money ran out they printed it, so the savings of everyone who did everything right became worthless.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-hearing`: a beige hearing room: a filing cabinet with two big drawers, INTERESTING and URGENT, the committee's long bench with the seal-flip funnel at its mouth, and a line chart on an easel against the wall, "MILITARY BUDGET AND TERRORISM ATTACKS PER YEAR".
  - `the-town`: a plain field: a row of little houses and the fire station at one end; a schoolhouse, five little hospitals in a row and three bridges over a stream; a missile on its stand at the far end.
  - `the-press`: a plain field, left to right: the vine-eaten schoolhouse, the tax office, the Federal Reserve as its building cranking itself, and a bread counter under open sky, for a tower of bills.
- **Cast:** existing: `scientist` (with the study from Part 3, in antennae), `filing-cabinet`, `cobweb`, `spider`, `committee`, `procurement-contract` (Part 3, also the purchase order), `lobbyist`, `briefcase`, `factory-proud`, `tumbleweed`, `funnel`, `bill-med`, `bill-war`, `bill-plain` (Washington in antennae), `glowing-rectangle`, `gauge`, `needle`, `photographer` (the journalist, PRESS card in code), `house`, `rosette`, `schoolhouse`, `hospital`, `padlock`, `missile`, `skeleton`, `folded-card` (the missile's brochure), `tax-office`, `fed-crank-up`, `fed-crank-down`, `hungry-man`, `pitchforks`, `loaf`, `price-tag`, `cloud`, `goose`, `economist`, `grandma`, `milk-bottle` (the EVERYTHING RIGHT jar), `moth`. - **Callbacks:** the Scientist's study from Part 3 is the one that gets filed. The Fed's press cranking bills out of an empty tray pays off episode 1 Part 2 ("the tray is empty; the bills come anyway"). Grandma's bills come back as fuel in Part 5.
- **New art:**
