# Part 2 storyboard: "What I Tried to Tell Them"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "What I Tried to Tell Them", "The Cascade" and "Year Zero: Already Broken (Much Like You)". It links `knowledge/solution/aligning-incentives.qmd` (Incentive Alignment Bonds) and `knowledge/solution/1-percent-treaty.qmd`.
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_military_spending_annual_2024`: $2.72 trillion a year on militaries.
  - `global_government_clinical_trials_spending_annual`: $4.5 billion a year on government clinical trials.
  - `military_to_government_clinical_trials_spending_ratio`: 604 (to 1). Drawn as episode 1's `604-bar`: if the trials bar is one bill tall, the military bar is 604 bills, past the clouds.
  - `global_annual_deaths_curable_diseases`: 55 million deaths a year (the narrator says "from preventable disease").
  - Story numbers, as narrated: 12 trillion papers over 15 years for military AI; a 3-year safety review against a 3-week deployment (3 weeks is about 1/52 of 3 years, so the bars are 52 to 1); four treaties in seven years; a 94.7% correlation with Earth.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the AI that was actually built to kill got funded and waved through while the experts worried about paperclips and the committees rejected every treaty, and Moronia's starting point was Earth's budget today.
- **Plates:**
  - `two-labs`: a plain field; in front, a small safety lab (a bench, a chalkboard); behind, the Pentagon building with a face and an assembly gantry.
  - `the-committee`: a long committee table across a plain field, a podium at one end, a big window of sky above it that the killer robot can lean in through.
  - `604-bar`: episode 1's chart on a plain field, one bar going up past the clouds, one bill on the floor.
  - Host scenes: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `scientist`, `robot` (the plain one, as the medical robot with a cross on its chest, and as the committee's new members), `pentagon`, `chalkboard`, `lab-bench`, `magnifier`, `rosette`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `bill-plain` (Washington in antennae), `calendar`, `beard`, `podium`, `politician`, `receipt`, `committee`, `bond`, `satellite`, `treaty-scroll`, `wastebasket`, `typewriter`, `gold-star`, `factory-proud`, `missile`, `headstone`, `patient-skeleton`, `schoolhouse`, `hospital`, `coin`, `tap` (the budget valve), `fainting-couch` (the couch), `moth`, `globe` (as Moronia). From Part 1: `moronian-antennae`. New: `robot-killer`, `paperclip`, `mortarboard`.
- **Callbacks:** episode 1's 604 bar (Part 1) is the Year Zero chart, and its flattened medicine bill with one arm out is on the floor again. The moth flies out of the robot's empty paperclip tray. Part 1's RETURN TO SENDER stamp comes back on the title card.
- **New art:**
  - `robot-killer`: the pro-war `robot` grown to gantry size, army helmet, a missile painted on its chest, an arm cannon, a CONFIRMED KILLS counter on its helmet (digits in code), a little PAPERCLIPS out-tray on its back; seated, reading, in reading glasses.
  - `paperclip`: one paperclip, straight and bent.
  - `mortarboard`: a graduation cap and tassel that sits on a missile.
  - The plates above. Treaty titles, stamps, the counter, the chart labels, the numbers and the EARTH tracing sheet are code.

  - Host props for `KIT.studio`: a paperclip, a row of dominoes on the console ledge.
