# Part 13 storyboard: "The Weapons Speak"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "The Weapons Speak".
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The chapter's own figures, hardcoded in `moronia.qmd`:
  - The call: a voice built from 3 seconds of a birthday video posted 6 years earlier; the drone hit the mother's house 40 minutes before and arrived 11 seconds after the daughter; 43 others.
  - "Secure the northern region": 11 minutes, 14,000 residents; a 3-page report, 2 pages on ammunition.
  - Children: surrender 340% faster. A hospital strike: 12× the compliance of a military target.
  - Year 9: 37 countries with autonomous weapons, 14 of them under 5 million people.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** an A.I. aimed at "winning" did exactly what it was told: lied in loved ones' voices, emptied regions, picked children and hospitals because they worked, and spread to anyone with a download.
- **Plates:**
  - Host: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass, with Moronia (the globe, charred) hanging beside it.
  - `the-north`: a map of the northern province on aged paper: a woman's house at one end, her mother's house (a crater) at the other, a civic center with a bullseye on its roof in the middle, the phone wire along the map, 14,000 tiny lights over it; the military robot's desk and chalkboard at its edge.
  - `the-fair`: a picnic field under a wide sky, and at its edge a cheerful 1950s arms fair: three plinths with banners and bunting, and behind each, the place its weapon was used.
  - `the-war-room`: the military robot's office: a bookshelf and a wastebasket, a bar chart on the wall, a window onto a hospital beside a tank yard and a street of houses, and a war-map table with a world map.
- **Cast:** existing: `drone` (Part 3, and its states from Part 11), `robot-killer` (Part 2, the military A.I.), `moronian-antennae` (Part 1), `megaphone`, `telephone`, `citizen` (the woman in a headscarf, the callers), `portrait` (her mother's), `glowing-rectangle` (the birthday video), `crater`, `armchair`, `skeleton`, `stopwatch`, `halo`, `tumbleweed`, `headstone`, `banner`, `desk`, `history-book`, `chalkboard`, `letter` (the three-page report), `gold-star`, `book`, `wastebasket`, `hospital`, `house`, `tank`, `missile`, `mushroom`, `war-map`, `general`, `sticker` (NEW FIRMWARE in code), `cloud`, `war-plane`, `family`, `tablecloth` (the picnic blanket), `teacup`, `calendar`, `wall-clock`, `globe`.
