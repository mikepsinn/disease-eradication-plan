# Part 6 storyboard: "The Warehouses"

- **Source:** `knowledge/futures/moronia.qmd`, "The Warehouses". The audio was recorded from an earlier version of this section (the migrants, the nurse and the mother); the page has since been rewritten around "that decade's villain". This storyboard follows the audio.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The story's numbers, as narrated:
  - The drug war: 1 trillion papers over fifty years; overdose deaths from 10,000 a year to 100,000 a year (ten times).
  - 2 million Moronians in cages at any time; contracts require 90% occupancy.
  - The migrants' net fiscal surplus: "in the trillions".
  - 45 billion papers to convert warehouses into detention facilities.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** prisons paid to stay full need someone in the beds, so the definitions keep widening to whoever is next, and next could be anyone.
- **Plates:**
  - `the-prison`: the prison building on a plain field, the Capitol to its left, a pay window on its side; outside its gate, a line chart, "OVERDOSE DEATHS PER YEAR" and a flat "DRUG AVAILABILITY".
  - `the-street`: a plain street: a big balance scale in the middle with a half-built house, a hospital corridor and a field of crops round it, a giant glowing rectangle behind, and the warehouse at the far end.
  - `two-outlets`: Part 5's pipeline, its far end split into two pipes, one into the prison and one into the warehouse; a ward of beds below.
  - Host scene: Wishonia at her console on the observation deck, Earth in the glass.
- **Cast:** existing: `prison` (Part 1), `capitol`, `gauge`, `needle`, `headstone`, `pill-bottle`, `bill-war`, `bill-plain`, `lobbyist`, `briefcase`, `procurement-contract` (Part 3), `iron-bed`, `skeleton`, `pay-window`, `citizen`, `grandma` (the old lady), `stapler` (Part 4, at host scale), `balance-scale`, `economist`, `ruler`, `builder` (the carpenter), `sweeper` (the cleaner), `farmer` (the fruit picker), `gold-star`, `glowing-rectangle`, `devil-costume` (the horns on the screen), `committee`, `orderly` (the nurse), `soldiers` in `mp-helmet` (the agents), `motorcar` (the mother's car), `photographer` (the journalist, Part 4), `pram-woman`, `newspaper-man`, `pipeline` (Part 5), `you`, `moth`. From Part 1: `moronian-antennae`. New: `warehouse`, `enforcement-van`.
- **Callbacks:** the prison's SUCCESS rosette and the receipts promised in Part 1; the pipeline and its hopper from Part 5; the butterfly net that keeps extending from episode 1 Part 4 is the van's arm; the press photographer from Part 4 is the one arrested; the purchase-order staple from Part 4 pays off in host-patterns; YOU from Part 1's field guide is the name on the last bed.
- **New art:**
  - `warehouse`: a long corrugated detention warehouse as its building, a face in its loading door, rows of beds inside, an OCCUPANCY gauge and a CONTRACTOR plate matching the prison's.
  - `enforcement-van`: the enforcement agency as a 1950s black van with a face, a sign on its side, a slot for bills, and a telescoping arm that ends in a big net.

- voiceover: "The terrorism spiral was the external version. Moronia had a domestic version too. They had a drug problem. They spent one trillion papers over fifty years fighting it. But the trillion papers had built a private detention industry with two million Moronians in cages at any given time and corporations whose contracts required ninety percent occupancy. Everybody got paid except the dead, who were not part of the contract."
