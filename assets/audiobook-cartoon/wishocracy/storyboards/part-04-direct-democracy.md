# Part 4 storyboard: "Direct Democracy Has Been Tried"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/wishocracy.qmd`, "But Direct Democracy Has Been Tried (Yes. Badly.)". Narration: `wishocracy.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`; cited facts the chapter states inline:
  - California: Prop 8 in 2008; over $700 million per cycle on ballot campaigns (`california-ballot-proposition-overload`).
  - Brexit: a "one hundred-dimensional" trade policy (the narration's figure of speech, drawn as 100 threads); 33 million voters (`brexit-referendum-2016`); a 27-country trade bloc.
