- **The numbers:**
  - `crowd_decision_accuracy` 91% and `expert_decision_accuracy` 65%; derived, the 26-point gap (91 − 65).
  - $127 million from "Answer C": the narrator's figure for military lobbying. The book's `defense_lobbying_annual` has since become $198 million (2025, OpenSecrets); the screen uses the narrator's $127,000,000.
