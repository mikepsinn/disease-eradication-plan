# Part 6 storyboard: "Where Wishes Become Tasks"

- **Source:** `knowledge/solution/wishocracy.qmd`, "Why Markets Solved This (and Why You Still Need Wishocracy)" and "From Priorities to Projects (Where Wishes Become Tasks)". Narration: `wishocracy.prepared.txt`.
- **The numbers:** none from `_variables.yml`. The narration's:
  - Hayek's paper on prices and knowledge, 1945 (`hayek1945`).
  - Pyramids, 4,500 years ago.
  - Two example bounties: $10 billion (expand pragmatic clinical trial capacity) and $500 million (open verification tools for warhead dismantlement). These are illustrations, not parameters.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** prices tell markets what people want, but nobody can sell a public good, so Wishocracy asks people directly; then an AI breaks each priority into small tasks, posts a bounty on each, and teams anywhere compete, with funding following results.
