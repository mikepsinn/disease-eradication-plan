# Part 2 storyboard: "Where the $114 Billion Comes From"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/peace-dividend.qmd`, "Where the $114B Comes From" and the paragraphs after it ("Plus cancer gets cured as a bonus", the benefits cascade).
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_annual_war_direct_costs_total` = $7.66 trillion a year, made of:
    - `global_military_spending_annual_2024` = $2.72 trillion
    - `global_annual_infrastructure_destruction_conflict` = $1.88 trillion
    - `global_annual_human_life_losses_conflict` = $2.45 trillion
    - `global_annual_trade_disruption_conflict` = $616 billion
  - `peace_dividend_direct_costs` = $76.6 billion (1% of direct).
  - `global_annual_war_indirect_costs_total` = $3.7 trillion a year, including:
    - `global_annual_lost_economic_growth_military_spending` = $2.72 trillion
    - `global_annual_veteran_healthcare_costs` = $200 billion
    - derived: the rest (refugees, PTSD, environmental damage, lost human capital) is about $780 billion.
  - `peace_dividend_indirect_costs` = $37 billion (1% of indirect).
  - `global_annual_direct_indirect_war_cost` = $11.4 trillion; `peace_dividend_annual_societal_benefit` = $114 billion.
  - The War on Terror: $8 trillion (cited, `war-on-terror-cost-blowback`, not a parameter).
  - Derived for the receipt: each line prints a bar to scale; if $2.72 trillion is 600 px, then $1.88T is 415 px, $2.45T is 540 px, $616B is 136 px, $200B is 44 px and the indirect rest is 172 px.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** war costs $11.4 trillion a year once you count what it breaks, so 1% less of it saves $114 billion, and treating the sick also shrinks problems that bombs only multiply.
