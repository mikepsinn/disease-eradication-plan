# Part 3 storyboard: "The Two Numbers"

- **Source:** `knowledge/economics/peace-dividend.qmd`, "The Two Numbers", "The Elasticity Question" and "The Part Where the Math Gets Embarrassing".
- **The numbers:**
  - `global_military_spending_annual_2024` = $2.72 trillion; `treaty_annual_funding` = $27.2 billion a year (the Captured Dividend).
  - `peace_dividend_annual_societal_benefit` = $114 billion a year (the Societal Dividend, the narrator's best case).
  - `peace_dividend_conflict_elasticity` = 1 (95% CI 0.25 to 1.5): the narrator's e = 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 and "greater than 1.0".
  - The narrator's pessimistic floor, "roughly fifty billion dollars": derived, $27.2B + 0.25 × ($114B − $27.2B) ≈ $49 billion.
  - `global_med_research_spending` = $67.5 billion a year; derived: $27.2B / $67.5B ≈ 40% more.
  - Camouflage paint against curing Alzheimer's: no parameter; the bars carry no numbers.
- **What the viewer must leave with:** the $27.2 billion is guaranteed by the treaty whatever humans do; the $114 billion depends on them fighting less; even the pessimistic case beats every increase in medical research funding in history.
- **Plates:**
  - `belt-and-well`: a plain field with two things side by side: a conveyor from the factory, "EXPLOSION ACCOUNT", to the lab, "MEDICINE ACCOUNT", the treaty scroll nailed to its first post; and a stone wishing well, "$114 BILLION" on its roof, with four pairs of soldiers squaring off round it. Between them, a big brass dial on a post, "e", marked 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 and ">1", wired to the soldiers. They are the 1% the treaty cuts; behind them, small on the horizon, a dust cloud of the rest of the war (the other 99%) brawls on in every scene.
  - `the-floor`: a bar chart on aged paper, "MEDICAL RESEARCH FUNDING", one bar of $67.5 billion; a wooden floor at $27.2 billion and a ceiling at $114 billion drawn as a range above it.
  - `murder-budget`: Part 1's plate, at the foot of the tower: the camouflage tin and the CURING ALZHEIMER'S stack.
- **Cast:** existing, or from Part 1 where marked: `you`, `factory-proud`, `conveyor`, `lab`, `bill-war`, `bill-med`, `coin`, `treaty-scroll`, `gauge` (the dial), `soldier` (Part 1), `rock-gray`, `scissors`, `cobweb`, `rope`, `germ`, `halo`, `rosette`, `scientist-flask`, `ink-arrow`, `camo-tin` (Part 1), `painter`, `tank`, `family`, `grandma`. New: `wishing-well`.
- **Callbacks:** Part 1's camouflage tin and Alzheimer's stack (camouflage-paint) pay off here, and so does Part 1's rosette. The belt and the well come back in Part 5 (the safe bet), and the e dial in Part 6 ("that's at e equals one point zero"). The halo that will not stay on anyone is from episode 1.
