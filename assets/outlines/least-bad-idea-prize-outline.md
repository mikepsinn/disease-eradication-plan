**Description:** An Incentive Alignment Bond (assurance contract with revenue-share upside) that creates self-organizing coordination. Contributors earn 10% annually if it fails and a pro-rata revenue share of treaty funding if it succeeds. The secondary market price is a real-time signal of implementation probability. The Earth Optimization Plan v1 is the terrible starting benchmark. v2 is whatever beats it.
